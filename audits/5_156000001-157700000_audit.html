Audit Report - Locus 5:156000001-157700000 (nearest gene ADAM19)

This file records everything EXCLUDED from the final evidence table (`evidence_tables/5_156000001-157700000.md`), plus the full red-team critique reasoning (including CONTESTED/WEAKENED calls), and process notes on how ambiguous verifier verdicts were handled. This is a separate top-level directory from `evidence_tables/` so evidence tables can be handed to reviewers without also handing out this audit trail.

Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH / ambiguous-verdict)

  1. **Pérez-Rubio et al. 2016, COPD (journal); PMID 27078193; DOI 10.3109/15412555.2016.1161017.** Claim as submitted by researcher: in smokers with/without COPD, ADAM19 rs1422794 associated with lower risk of severe (GOLD III+IV) disease, while an ADAM19 haplotype was associated with higher risk (OR=2.83) of severe COPD. Verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. The article exists (confirmed via PMID/DOI) but full text is paywalled; the verifier could not confirm the specific rs1422794/OR=2.83 claims from any accessible source. Excluded pending independent access to full text.
  1. **Kim W et al. 2026, Genome Biol; PMID 41535900; DOI 10.1186/s13059-025-03921-y.** Claim as submitted by researcher: large multi-ancestry TOPMed WGS study found colocalization with lung-tissue eQTL (LTRC) for ADAM19 expression, and lung scRNA-seq showed epithelial/immune-cell enrichment specifically for ADAM19. Verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. The eQTL-colocalization portion of the claim (ADAM19 colocalizing with LTRC lung-tissue eQTL) was independently confirmed from the abstract. However, the scRNA-seq epithelial/immune-cell enrichment finding is attributed in the actual abstract to a DIFFERENT gene, HMCN1, not ADAM19 — the researcher's claim misattributed this specific finding. Per pipeline rule, a CLAIM_MISMATCH verdict excludes the citation wholesale from the evidence table rather than allowing a claim-by-claim split; the correctly-supported eQTL-colocalization fact is separately available from the Open Targets structured-database lookup (see main evidence table's Open Targets summary section) and does not depend on this citation. Note: the verifier also found this paper has a published erratum (PMID 42265570, 2026-06-09) — worth checking whether the erratum affects the eQTL-colocalization finding if this paper is revisited.
  1. **Chesneau V et al. 2003, J Biol Chem; PMID 12682046; DOI 10.1074/jbc.M302781200.** Claim as submitted by researcher: recombinant ADAM19 cleaves TNF-alpha, TRANCE/RANKL, and KIT-ligand-1 cleavage-site peptides in vitro, and negatively regulates KIT-ligand-1 shedding in cells. Verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. The in vitro cleavage of TNF-alpha and TRANCE/RANKL peptides was confirmed. However, the claim that ADAM19 "negatively regulates KIT- ligand-1 shedding" overstates what the abstract actually supports; the paper's finding about constitutive TNF-alpha release increase is a different (and separately, correctly cited) claim from Zheng et al. 2004, which IS included in the main table. Excluded wholesale per pipeline rule.

A re-run of citation-verifier specifically on this one reference would be worthwhile to resolve the discrepancy.

Red-team-skeptic critique — process caveat

The red-team-skeptic subagent disclosed that all five of its planned WebSearch queries failed to execute because the session's web-search budget was already exhausted before its task began (likely consumed by the researcher and citation-verifier subagents earlier in this same session). **It performed zero independent literature searches or contradiction checks** — its critique below is a purely inferential analysis of the evidence bundle the orchestrator supplied, not new literature discovery. This is materially weaker verification coverage than the pipeline's red-team stage normally provides. If this locus is revisited, a fresh red-team pass with working search access — specifically targeting (a) a direct ADAM19-vs-SGCD contradiction/support search, and (b) a dedicated smoking-initiation/ cessation GWAS catalog check for this 5q33 region (which the researcher also flagged as an absence-of-evidence gap it could not fully close) — would be valuable.

Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

1. Is "ADAM19 is best-supported" justified, or overclaimed?

Partially overclaimed. The statistical-genetics tier is genuinely the strongest of the five candidates: Hancock 2010 is a real genome-wide-significant FEV1/FVC hit (P=8.32e-10), which under prior 2 (spirometry concordance) is legitimate strong evidence for pulmonary biology. That part holds.

But the synthesis paragraph claims "convergent statistical genetics + structural/ developmental biology + eQTL colocalization," and two of those three legs don't hold up:

- Structural/developmental biology is not pulmonary. Kurohara 2004 is a cardiac phenotype (VSD, immature valves via endocardial-cushion EMT) — this is being used analogically to argue for a lung-structural mechanism, exactly the same evidentiary move the researcher correctly criticized for TIMD4 (non-pulmonary tissue precedent). Applying a double standard here is the clearest overclaim in the write-up. - eQTL colocalization is not lung-specific. The colocalizing tissues are esophagus, skin, testis, stomach, LCL, fat, muscle, colon, breast — explicitly not lung in the sampled data. Citing "eQTL colocalization" as a pillar of a "genuine pulmonary mechanism" argument, without noting it doesn't include lung tissue, is an overclaim.

Additional weaknesses the synthesis underweights: COPD case-control evidence for ADAM19 itself is nominal (Wilk 2012, not genome-wide-significant; Castaldi 2011, FDR 5% only) — the strong signal is for a spirometry trait, not disease case-control. London 2014's sequencing found no rare-variant burden and only one additional common variant on conditioning — arguing against a rich coding/causal-variant story despite the missense rs1422795 being in the credible set (PIP only 0.046 — low). The TNF-alpha sheddase role (Zheng 2004) is explicitly secondary to ADAM17. Immune evidence is muscle (Dehmel, myopathy)/kidney (Wang, AKI)/exploratory bioinformatics (Liu 2025, rat model + herbal-formula intervention, no functional validation of ADAM19 itself) — none is confirmed human lung tissue mechanism.

Worth noting for the record: of the four citations the verifier rejected across this whole locus, three concern ADAM19 (Pérez-Rubio, Chesneau, and the Kim 2026 paper whose cell-type-enrichment claim was actually about a different gene, HMCN1, entirely). ADAM19 has the most total citations so some base-rate effect is expected, but this pattern is still worth flagging as a sign the ADAM19 case was assembled somewhat loosely and deserves the most scrutiny, not the least.

Net: the genetic-association tier genuinely favors ADAM19 over the other four candidates. The mechanistic "convergent evidence" framing is overclaimed — most of it is analogical/ non-pulmonary/exploratory, no better in kind than what was dismissed for TIMD4 and SGCD.

2. Does never-smoker stratification rule out smoking-mediation?

No — it rules out one specific hypothesis (that the association is purely an artifact of nicotine-dependence/smoking-behavior pathways operating only in smokers) but does not establish the alternative the researcher jumps to ("genuine pulmonary-structural biology"). Legitimate points in ADAM19's favor: the signal persists in never/non-current smokers (Kim 2020 GWAS) and in an 8-year-old pediatric cohort pre-dating meaningful smoking exposure (Panasevich, though this is a single modest, non-replicated study, p=0.03). Together these are reasonable evidence against the locus being a pure smoking-behavior/neurobiological signal. But: (a) this doesn't tell us the mechanism is structural rather than immune, developmental-non-structural, or an environmental-exposure-response pathway unrelated to nicotine; (b) it doesn't address whether ADAM19 acts on smoke-exposure-conditional lung damage response (a form of smoking-mediated pulmonary biology distinct from nicotine-dependence behavior) — the Vishweswaraiah cigarette-smoke-exposed-mouse-lung finding and the Liu 2025 COPD-rat-model finding are both explicitly smoke-exposure- conditional, which sits awkwardly next to the "not smoking-mediated" framing. The researcher's inferential chain (not-smoking-behavior-mediated -> therefore genuine structural biology) has a gap.

3. CONTESTED calls

SGCD vs. ADAM19 — flag as the real tension at this locus, short of full CONTESTED but close. SGCD has something ADAM19 conspicuously lacks: a lung eQTL (P=2.57e-9) and protein localized to airway smooth muscle — an actual COPD-relevant lung tissue/cell type. ADAM19's eQTL colocalization, by contrast, spans nine non-lung tissues and none in lung. If tissue-specific eQTL/expression evidence is a criterion for prioritization (as priors 2/3 imply it should be), SGCD's evidence is more tissue-relevant than ADAM19's on this specific axis, even though SGCD's actual statistical association (Hansel 2015) is weaker (suggestive only, P 9.57e-8-4.6e-6, not genome-wide-significant, and restricted to a narrow subphenotype — airway hyperresponsiveness — within an all-smoker cohort, so smoking- mediation cannot be excluded for SGCD either). No paper explicitly arguing "it's SGCD, not ADAM19" at this locus was found (searches could not be run — budget exhausted), so this stops short of full CONTESTED, but the researcher's framing of SGCD as merely having a "plausible mechanistic story" while ADAM19 is "clear best-supported" undersells this asymmetry and should be revisited if a real contradiction search becomes possible.

TIMD4 — WEAKENED, not CONTESTED. Zero L2G, zero COPD/lung evidence of any kind; all six citations are non-pulmonary tissue analogies (skin, liver, gut, bone, Listeria infection). Worth flagging that MAGMA gene-based tests are proximity/LD-sensitive and can prioritize a gene purely because it sits near the true signal in a gene-dense region — the disconnect between MAGMA prioritization and zero L2G evidence is itself a red flag consistent with LD/nearest-gene-block bias rather than a real TIMD4 mechanism.

CYFIP2 and NIPAL4 — HOLDS as bystander calls. PIP <0.001 for both, no COPD-specific literature beyond one exploratory bioinformatic clustering paper (CYFIP2), and both genes' well-established Mendelian disease biology (severe neurodevelopmental disease; congenital ichthyosis) is respiratory-irrelevant. The researcher's "likely bystander" conclusion for these two is well-supported by what's verified.

4. Credible-set size/LD as alternative explanation

A 163-variant credible set spanning a locus window of 1.7 Mb (156.0-157.7 Mb) is large and diffuse — consistent with a broad, incompletely resolved LD block rather than a single cleanly fine-mapped causal gene. Given that rs4579242 (lead variant) sits in an ADAM19 intron and ABC/L2G prioritization is heavily influenced by physical proximity, the "highest L2G score" finding for ADAM19 is at least partly consistent with a nearest-gene/proximity prior rather than independent causal proof. SGCD's presence as a PoPS-prioritized candidate in the same broad interval, with its own lung-relevant expression data, is exactly the scenario the LD caveat warns about: the fine-mapping/statistical evidence does not clearly distinguish ADAM19 from SGCD as the operative gene. The researcher's synthesis does not engage with this alternative explanation for the pattern of evidence.

Per-gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic's final calls)

- ADAM19: WEAKENED. Real, comparatively strong genetic-association evidence (GWS FEV1/FVC, highest OT L2G/composite score) but the "convergent mechanistic" case is overstated — the developmental biology is cardiac not pulmonary, the eQTL colocalization is explicitly non-lung, immune evidence is drawn from muscle/kidney/exploratory-bioinformatic sources, and COPD case-control evidence itself is only nominal/FDR-level. Never-smoker stratification argues against nicotine-pathway mediation but does not establish a structural mechanism specifically. - TIMD4: WEAKENED. Zero COPD/lung evidence; entire case is analogical (efferocytosis biology from skin/liver/gut/bone/infection contexts). MAGMA-only prioritization with zero L2G is a plausible LD/proximity artifact. - SGCD: WEAKENED, but flagged as the strongest rival to ADAM19 on tissue-specificity grounds (lung eQTL, airway-smooth-muscle protein localization) even though its own association evidence is only suggestive and confined to a smoker-only subphenotype cohort, so smoking-mediation isn't excluded for SGCD either. This gene/ADAM19 pairing deserves a dedicated contradiction search before synthesis finalizes a single "best" gene. - CYFIP2: HOLDS as bystander (PIP<0.001, no respiratory phenotype in its well-characterized disease biology, only exploratory bioinformatic COPD "hit"). - NIPAL4: HOLDS as bystander (PIP<0.001, no lung phenotype, skin-barrier/ichthyosis biology only).

Other process notes

- **Smoking-behavior GWAS overlap:** the researcher explicitly flagged that no dedicated smoking-initiation/cigarettes-per-day/cessation GWAS hit at this 5q33 locus was found in PubMed searches (searches for rs4579242, rs1422795, and general terms returned zero hits). This is an absence-of-evidence finding, not a confirmed non-overlap — a dedicated GSCAN/smoking-GWAS-catalog lookup (as opposed to PubMed literature search) was not independently performed in this session and would be worth doing if this locus is revisited. - **RNU6-390P**, hit by one low-PIP (0.0013) downstream credible-set variant (rs7724554), is a non-coding U6 pseudogene; per pipeline scope rules no dedicated literature search was performed on it, and nothing notable surfaced in passing. - **Region-completeness check:** the researcher cross-referenced the five candidate genes (ADAM19, TIMD4, SGCD, CYFIP2, NIPAL4) against gene names recurring across all retrieved GWAS/eQTL papers for this region and found no additional protein-coding genes; however, this check was literature-based only (an independent Ensembl/region-lookup query was not run in this session) and should be treated as a secondary check, not a substitute for a formal region gene-list query if greater completeness assurance is needed.
