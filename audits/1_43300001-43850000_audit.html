Audit Report - Locus 1:43300001-43850000 (nearest gene PTPRF)

This file preserves excluded citations, contested genes, and process caveats that are deliberately left out of the main evidence table (`evidence_tables/1_43300001-43850000.md`) so that document can be handed to reviewers without the full audit trail, while this file remains available for anyone who wants to check the pipeline's work.

Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

**None.** All 23 citations produced by the researcher for this locus in the original review were verified with a VERIFIED verdict by citation-verifier (checked 2026-09-11). In the CDC20 re-opening addendum (see below), all 19 additional CDC20 citations were also VERIFIED. No citations were excluded from the evidence table on verification grounds at any stage. Full per-citation verifier reasoning is in `evidence_tables/_status/1_43300001-43850000/verified_citations.md`.

One notable resolution during the original verification: the researcher had flagged uncertainty about whether PMID 40964458 (Fu et al. 2025, MR+ML hypoxia-genes/COPD study) specifically implicated SLC2A1 or a different hypoxia-related gene in its causal estimate. The citation-verifier confirmed from the abstract that SLC2A1 specifically is the gene with the reported causal estimate (OR=1.32, 95% CI 1.02-1.71).

One disambiguation flagged during CDC20 verification: PMID 21088493 (Lizé et al., Cell Cycle 2010, miR-449a/mucociliary differentiation/tobacco smoke) concerns **CDC20B**, a distinct paralogous gene at a different genomic locus, **not CDC20 itself** (the candidate gene at this locus). Citation-verifier confirmed this disambiguation is correct. This citation is retained only as a flagged disambiguation note and was deliberately excluded from the main evidence table as CDC20-supporting evidence — it must not be read as literature support for CDC20.

Process caveats (original review)

  1. **Structural finding on candidate-gene physical location:** the researcher's region enumeration (via Ensembl REST API) found that both SLC2A1 (chr1:42,925,353-42,958,904) and ERMAP (chr1:42,817,078-42,845,920) lie ~340-500kb outside the stated locus boundary (1:43,300,001-43,850,000) and ~615-755kb from the lead variant rs2842171 (1:43,574,179) — i.e., neither gene is physically within the region despite being MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized "for this locus." This is a material caveat that should inform interpretation of any locus where MAGMA/PoPS nominate a gene physically distant from the credible set. **This was later confirmed to be a genome-build error in the MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file (see re-opening addendum below).**
  1. **Red-team-skeptic search-budget exhaustion:** the red-team-skeptic explicitly disclosed that all of its WebSearch calls (covering PTPRF+COPD, SLC2A1+COPD, ERMAP+COPD, rs2842171 colocalization, and ST3GAL3+COPD queries) returned "session search budget exhausted," meaning it was unable to run any independent contradiction search for any of the four genes discussed. Its critique is based entirely on re-analysis of the researcher's own reported findings and the Open Targets structured data supplied in the dispatch prompt (which itself functions as a substantial independent check, since it was generated before and agnostic to the researcher's narrative), not on fresh independent literature search. This should be weighed when assessing how much additional scrutiny this locus's critique received relative to loci where the skeptic had full search capacity.
  1. **No FEV1/FEV1-FVC/lung-function evidence found anywhere** in this locus's review — not in Open Targets (structured lookup), not in the researcher's independent PubMed search, and not contradicted by the red-team-skeptic (who also found none, though under the search-budget constraint above). This absence, combined with strong colocalization between the COPD credible set and multiple smoking-behavior credible sets, is the primary basis for this locus being characterized as more likely smoking-mediated (prior #1) than pulmonary-biology-mediated (prior #2), per both the researcher's and skeptic's independent assessments.

RE-OPENING ADDENDUM (2026-09-11): CDC20

Background

The genome-build error noted in caveat #1 above (SLC2A1/ERMAP both ~340-730kb outside the stated locus window) was traced to a bug in the MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file used to build `loci/copd-all-gene-prioritization.txt`. A corrected re-merge surfaced **CDC20** as a candidate gene for this locus that was never evaluated in the original review. This locus was re-opened specifically to evaluate CDC20 following the pipeline's standard process (Open Targets check → researcher → citation-verifier → red-team-skeptic → synthesis update).

Independent physical-overlap check (orchestrator, via Open Targets/Ensembl)

CDC20's genomic coordinates (chr1:43,358,928-43,363,207, GRCh38, via Open Targets `target.genomicLocation`) genuinely and unambiguously fall inside this locus's stated window (1:43,300,001-43,850,000) — unlike SLC2A1 and ERMAP, which sit entirely outside the window. CDC20 therefore could not be ruled out at the physical-overlap-check stage the way SLC2A1/ERMAP could, and was sent for full researcher work-up. However, CDC20 sits ~145kb upstream of the nearest credible-set variant and ~211-266kb upstream of the lead variant rs2842171 and the bulk of the PTPRF-annotated credible set — i.e., it overlaps the broad locus window but not the credible-set span itself. A direct Open Targets query of all 70 GWAS credible sets fine-mapped at this lead variant (including the COPD-labeled study GCST90476027) found **zero L2G support for CDC20 in any of them** — the same absence pattern previously found for SLC2A1 and ERMAP, though CDC20's physical placement is more defensible than theirs. Full detail in `evidence_tables/_status/1_43300001-43850000/opentargets.md` (re-opening addendum section).

Gene marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

"CDC20 is genuinely inside the stated 550kb locus window... which is a real and important distinction from the SLC2A1/ERMAP build-bug artifacts... CDC20 cannot be dismissed that way. But 'inside a 550kb analyst-defined window' is a weak form of positional evidence on its own... CDC20 sits 145kb from the nearest credible-set variant and 211-266kb from the lead variant/bulk of the credible set, entirely outside the credible-set span itself... So while CDC20 escapes the 'artifact' label, it does not escape the 'distant bystander gene in a wide window' concern — it's a plausible candidate to *keep on the list*, not one with a physical-proximity case comparable to PTPRF's.

Zero L2G support across all 70 fine-mapped GWAS studies at this lead variant — not just the COPD study, but every trait Open Targets has fine-mapped here — is a strong negative signal, because Open Targets' L2G model incorporates eQTL/pQTL colocalization and chromatin-interaction features, not just distance. A long-range regulatory link... despite the 145-266kb gap is exactly the kind of thing L2G is designed to catch, and it caught nothing. Combined with the COPD target-disease association score (0.0074, ~17x below PTPRF's 0.123, below even SLC2A1's artifactual 0.064) and zero FEV1/FEV1-FVC evidence, the statistical-genetics case for CDC20 at this locus is essentially null, not merely thin.

The researcher's CDC20 literature is verified and is handled with unusual care (correctly flagging the CDC20B/CDC20 mixup..., correctly noting the non-smoking cohort..., correctly labeling the cannabis-methylation hit as sub-epigenome-wide-significant) — this is good-faith, non-overclaiming synthesis, not inflation of weak evidence. But nearly all of it is in IPF (pulmonary fibrosis) and PAH (pulmonary arterial hypertension) models, plus lung-cancer/toxicology cell biology, not COPD/emphysema... their core pathophysiology... is mechanistically distinct from COPD/emphysema's protease-antiprotease-driven alveolar destruction and airway inflammation/remodeling. This is structurally the same problem flagged for ERMAP's immune story in the original pass: literature that is plausible-sounding for 'lung disease' in general but not COPD-specific. The one genuinely interesting mechanistic thread — Cdc20 downregulation triggering p53-dependent fibroblast senescence (PMID 35988650) — is at least thematically resonant with COPD's known senescence/SASP biology, but this is presented with no eQTL or expression-direction link back to the actual GWAS variants; it's a hypothesis, not a demonstrated connection...

CDC20 is better characterized as a second 'narratively appealing but statistically unsupported' candidate, in the same family as ERMAP's immune story — but note it is not equivalent to ERMAP, since ERMAP was outright outside the window (artifact) while CDC20 is genuinely inside it. The right characterization is: CDC20 is 'inside the window, but outside the signal'...

**This should not change the final call.** Reaffirm PTPRF, confidence MODERATE, unchanged. CDC20 does not rise to co-favorite or tie status... CDC20 doesn't resolve that ambiguity in either direction, it's simply a weaker candidate on every axis measured."

**Verdict: CDC20 — WEAKENED** (per red-team-skeptic: real, verified mechanistic literature exists, but it is disease-adjacent [IPF/PAH/cancer] rather than COPD-specific, and the statistical/L2G/distance evidence argues against direct involvement at this locus; do not promote above PTPRF).

Process caveats (re-opening)

  1. **Red-team-skeptic search-budget exhaustion (again):** exactly as in the original review, the red-team-skeptic disclosed that its WebSearch budget was exhausted before its CDC20 critique turn (both of its attempted queries — "CDC20 COPD emphysema lung function GWAS" and a locus/lead-variant-specific query — failed with "session search budget exhausted," reported as 200/200 used). The CDC20 critique above is therefore based entirely on re-analysis of the researcher's reported findings, the citation-verifier's verdicts, and the Open Targets structured data supplied in the dispatch prompt, not on fresh independent literature search. The skeptic explicitly recommended that "if search budget becomes available again, a targeted CDC20 + COPD/lung-function/eQTL check should be run before this gene is closed out either way." This is flagged for any future re-opening of this locus.
  1. **Lung-tissue eQTL check not available:** both the original PTPRF critique and this CDC20 critique independently converge on the same missing piece of evidence — a lung-tissue-specific eQTL check (GTEx or equivalent) at rs2842171/the credible-set variants, for both PTPRF and CDC20, vs. brain/CNS or other tissue. Neither gene's case can be fully adjudicated without this, and it is recommended as the highest-value follow-up for this locus should the study team wish to resolve the PTPRF MODERATE-confidence call further.
  1. **CDC20B disambiguation:** PMID 21088493 concerns CDC20B, a distinct paralogous gene, not CDC20. See "Excluded citations" section above.
