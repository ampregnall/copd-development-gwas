Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

CCL20 — CONTESTED (skeptic's reasoning, verbatim)

"This is the weakest of the four genes and I disagree with treating it as a serious independent candidate at this locus.

- Never nominated by L2G across 18 GWAS studies (COPD or otherwise), and its aggregate OT COPD association score is driven almost entirely by literature/text-mining, not genetics. This is close to a textbook case of PoPS picking up a gene based on pathway/expression similarity to "inflammation genes" generally, untethered from the actual statistical fine-mapping at this specific locus (~820kb from the lead variant, well outside the credible set). - The supporting literature itself undercuts a specific causal role: a UC/gut paper (Labbé 2011), a review mostly about IBD/psoriasis/RA (Xu 2026), a mouse IL-17A model where CCL20 rises alongside IL-6 (Ding 2025), a rat CO-induced (not genetic) emphysema model with CCL20 as one of several co-expressed cytokines (Chen 2025), and — most tellingly — Tasena et al. 2026, where the verifier explicitly notes IL-6, not CCL20, was the mechanistically proven driver in that same paper. That last point is a direct internal contradiction: the citation being used to support CCL20 is a paper whose own data point away from CCL20 specifically. - Li et al. 2024 (review) states CCL20-CCR6 shows 'heightened expression' in COPD and proposes blockade as a therapeutic target — this is correlative/aspirational language from a review, not a causal or genetic claim, and should not be strengthened into 'CCL20 drives COPD inflammation' in any synthesis. Reviews citing other reviews on a common 'inflammation gene' narrative risk circularity — worth flagging explicitly.

Verdict: CONTESTED — I did not find new contradicting citations myself (search budget exhausted), but the evidence already assembled is internally contradictory (Tasena 2026 downgrades CCL20 relative to IL-6) and structurally nonspecific (never genetically nominated at this locus, evidence is entirely animal-model or other-organ-system literature, score driven by text-mining not genetics). The researcher's own characterization — 'real but nonspecific/secondary to its primary disease associations elsewhere' — supports downgrading rather than treating CCL20 as a live candidate gene at this locus."

Process caveats

  1. **Red-team-skeptic search budget exhaustion.** The skeptic's web-search tool reported its session search budget (200/200) was exhausted before any query returned results. Verbatim from the skeptic: "None of the four genes received any independent search from me this session — my critique below is based entirely on the researcher's provided findings, the Open Targets structured data, and my own reasoning about what that evidence does and doesn't support... synthesis-lead should treat my 'CONTESTED' calls below as reasoned from internal inconsistencies in the evidence already assembled, not as freshly verified by a new search." This means the critique is a reasoning-based audit of the researcher's assembled evidence, not an independently re-searched contradiction check. The skeptic explicitly recommended a follow-up search pass on: PID1 replication in non-Korean cohorts, SPHKAP-sphingolipid-pathway COPD literature, and any paper naming an alternative causal gene at the 2q37/CCL20 region.
  1. **Locus-window vs. candidate-gene distances.** Per the researcher's Ensembl REST lookup (structured lookup, not a literature claim), none of the three protein-coding candidate genes (PID1, SPHKAP, CCL20) physically falls inside the stated locus window (2:228,450,001-228,800,000); all are flanking genes at increasing distance from the lead variant (PID1 ~213kb, SPHKAP ~455kb, CCL20 ~820kb). The entire credible set sits inside the neighboring lncRNA LINC01807, which itself is prioritized by none of L2G/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC. This is presented in the main evidence table as context for why PID1's "nearest gene" status is a weaker signal than usual, and why no gene-specific coding/splice mechanism is expected at this locus.
  1. **Background-only citations (PMID 30643251, 33082346, 38816585).** These three GSCAN/ smoking-behavior GWAS/TWAS papers were verified as real, non-retracted publications, but were cited by the researcher only as negative/background search targets (i.e., PID1 and SPHKAP were searched for and NOT found named in any of them). They are included in the main evidence table for completeness and traceability but were not subjected to the red-team-skeptic's gene-level critique, since they make no claim about any candidate gene at this locus.
