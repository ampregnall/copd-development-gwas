Audit Report - Locus 11:76400001-76650000 (nearest gene EMSY)

This file preserves the excluded citations, contested-gene reasoning, and process notes that are not included in the main evidence table (`evidence_tables/11_76400001-76650000.md`), so that table can be handed out on its own without also handing out the full critique trail.

Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

None. All 24 citations produced by the researcher for this locus were checked by citation-verifier and returned VERIFIED — existing, not retracted, and matching the claims attributed to them (see full verdicts in `evidence_tables/_status/11_76400001-76650000/verified_citations.md`). No citation was excluded from the evidence table on verification grounds.

One citation (PMID 42270499, Preglej et al. 2026, Ann Rheum Dis) was initially flagged by the orchestrator as having a suspiciously high PMID number for its stated 2026 publication date, raising concern it might be fabricated or mis-transcribed. The citation-verifier confirmed it is a genuine, legitimate PMID for a real article published June 2026, and the claim attached to it (incidental mention of GVQW3 in a rheumatoid arthritis PBMC transcriptomic study, not a functional characterization) was confirmed accurate. This is recorded here as a process note, not an exclusion.

One citation (PMID 26990894, Meyer-Martin et al. 2016, Allergy) was surfaced by the researcher's search but initially left without an attached claim because the researcher did not review its content in detail. The citation-verifier independently reviewed it and confirmed it shows GARP (LRRC32) inhibition reduces allergic airway inflammation in a humanized mouse model. The red-team-skeptic flagged this as directly lung/airway-relevant functional evidence for LRRC32 that should not be left out. It has been restored to the main evidence table as a VERIFIED LRRC32 finding, not excluded.

Genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic

EMSY — gene assignment CONTESTED (not the underlying locus-disease association)

The skeptic's verdict: "EMSY-as-the-causal-COPD-gene is CONTESTED." Reasoning, preserved verbatim from the critique:

The contesting evidence is already in the researcher's own material and the Open Targets context, not new: (1) L2G evidence across ~15 studies at this exact interval favors LRRC32 over EMSY; (2) essentially every discovery paper names the locus jointly, reflecting unresolved gene assignment; (3) no FEV1/FEV1-FVC or COPD-specific functional evidence for EMSY exists; (4) the EMSY-expression eQTL claim (Amaral 2014) is tied to a non-credible-set variant in an atopy/immune context, not lung tissue. I'm not asserting LRRC32 is proven instead — only that the case for EMSY specifically (as opposed to "this locus, gene TBD") is weaker than the writeup's structure (which spends most of its mechanistic firepower on EMSY) implies. This doesn't require new verification — it's a synthesis of already-verified material — but flag explicitly for synthesis-lead as a reason to present this locus as "gene ambiguous, EMSY vs. LRRC32" rather than "EMSY locus."

The skeptic also specifically critiqued two individual claims as overclaiming:

**Elias 2019 skin-barrier study over-extended to airway** (c): Filaggrin/filaggrin-2/ ceramide-based stratum corneum barrier biology is keratinocyte-specific machinery; airway epithelium uses a structurally and mechanistically different barrier (tight junctions, mucociliary clearance, different lipid biology) and is exposed to inhaled irritants/smoke rather than mechanical/allergen skin exposure. Calling this "directly analogous" to airway epithelial barrier in COPD overstates what the cited abstract (keratinocyte organotypic culture, atopic dermatitis skin biopsies) actually supports... the phrase "directly analogous" should be struck or softened to something like "suggestive of a barrier-regulatory role for EMSY, untested in airway epithelium."
**Interferon-repression "through-line" is the weakest piece of reasoning in the writeup** (d): It stitches together an atopy paper's *proposed* (not directly tested) IFN-stimulated-gene repression mechanism (Amaral 2014, via a non-credible-set variant rs2155219) with a separate NSCLC literature on EMSY repressing type-I IFN in tumor cells (Marzio 2021, Sheban 2023, Baykara 2017). Different diseases, different cell types, no single study connecting the two, and no COPD data anywhere in the chain... Recommend it be explicitly downgraded to "hypothesis-generating only; no evidence in any lung-relevant COPD tissue or cell type."

LRRC32 — WEAKENED (incomplete evaluation), not fully CONTESTED

Plausible TGF-beta/Treg airway-remodeling mechanism, original discovery paper naming, and Open Targets' own L2G repeatedly favors it over EMSY, but the one COPD-specific L2G row is unresolved/unconfirmed, and a directly relevant airway-inflammation functional paper (Meyer-Martin 2016) was found but never integrated [prior to this synthesis step, now restored to the main evidence table]. Needs another research pass before it can be confidently ranked against EMSY.

ACER3 — CONTESTED / effectively unsupported

ACER3 sits ~210 kb outside the stated window and >270 kb from the credible-set interval, separated from it by two other genes; it has zero L2G, zero fine-mapping, zero COPD/FEV1 Open Targets evidence, and zero lung/COPD literature (the only mechanistic hit is a colitis mouse model, non-lung). Its sole support is internal MAGMA/PoPS statistical prioritization — gene-based/functional-similarity methods that are well known to pick up signal from LD/gene-density artifacts belonging to physically closer genes (EMSY, LRRC32) rather than ACER3 itself. This is effectively a positional/statistical-bias problem in the opposite direction from typical "nearest gene" bias — here a non-nearest gene is being kept in contention purely by a method susceptible to exactly the contamination this pipeline is designed to catch. I'd recommend ACER3 be reported as **CONTESTED/effectively refuted as a candidate at this locus**, not merely "low weight."

GVQW3 — HOLDS as a non-candidate

Correctly included only for completeness with no inference built on it; no critique needed beyond that.

Other skeptic observations preserved for the record

- **Lead variant has no literature.** rs7926914 (lead, PIP=0.247) is not tied to any cited paper — all literature support attaches to lower-PIP credible-set members (rs7130588, PIP 0.141; rs7927894, PIP 0.214). The strongest statistical signal in the credible set is functionally silent in the literature reviewed. - **Cross-population caveat.** Zhang 2020 (EMSY haplotype/asthma) and Amaral 2014 (rs2155219/expression) both use SNPs distinct from the credible set, in different populations (Chinese Han; ECRHS European) with potentially different LD structure. - **Smoking-mediation (Biological Prior #1) is an incomplete negative.** The researcher's web-search budget was exhausted before a GWAS-Catalog cross-check for smoking-initiation/heaviness/cessation associations at this locus could be completed. No positive evidence was found in the searches that were completed, but this should not be read as a confirmed absence. The skeptic's inference (allergic-sensitization/atopy genetics are a priori less likely to be smoking-behavior-mediated than loci with direct neuro-nicotinic biology such as CHRNA3/5) is noted as reasoning, not a completed finding. - **Process caveat from the skeptic:** its own planned contradicting-evidence searches ("GARP LRRC32 airway lung COPD"; "EMSY FEV1 spirometry GWAS") could not be run because the session's web-search budget was already exhausted by the time of its review. The critique above is based on the researcher's and orchestrator's materials plus biological-prior reasoning, not independent re-verification of literature absence. A second-pass literature search at this locus is recommended before treating the EMSY vs. LRRC32 ranking as final.

Physical-position discrepancy note

The researcher's Ensembl-based gene-in-region enumeration found that two of the internal pipeline's own candidate genes for this locus — LRRC32 (MAGMA/PoPS did not flag it; it was added post hoc by the orchestrator) and ACER3 (MAGMA + PoPS prioritized) — both lie physically outside the stated 76,400,001-76,650,000 window (LRRC32 by ~7.5 kb; ACER3 by ~210 kb), while only EMSY and the uncharacterized gene GVQW3 fall fully or partly inside it. This suggests the internal prioritization pipeline used a wider LD/regulatory window than the coordinates stated in the locus input file. This is recorded here as a data-provenance note for the study team, not resolved further by this review.
