Audit Report - Locus 13:35500001-35750000 (nearest gene NBEA)

This is the excluded/contested appendix for this locus. It is kept separate from `evidence_tables/13_35500001-35750000.md` so that evidence tables can be shared without also sharing the full audit trail.

Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

All five citations below were checked directly by the citation-verifier against full text (where available via PMC) or targeted searches. All five papers **exist and are not retracted** — the issue is that none of them support the NBEA-specific claim the researcher tentatively attached to them (the researcher had already flagged this as unconfirmed prior to verification). Verdict category used: NOT_FOUND, applied to the specific claim ("NBEA/this locus is a reported signal in this paper"), not to the paper's existence.

  1. **Sakornsakolpat P, et al. "Genetic landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype associations." Nature Genetics 2019;51(3):494-505.** PMID 30804561, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2. Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA among reported loci). The paper exists, is not retracted, and does identify 82 COPD risk loci (verified via PMC6546635 full text), but **NBEA is not mentioned anywhere in the paper, and chromosome 13q13.3 (35.5-35.75 Mb) is not among the reported 82 loci.** The closest chromosome 13 locus reported is SERP2 at 13q14.11, outside the NBEA region. Excluded from the evidence table as non-supporting.
  1. **Shrine N, et al. "New genetic signals for lung function highlight pathways and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease associations across multiple ancestries." Nature Genetics 2019;51(3):481-493.** PMID 30804560, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7. Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA among reported signals). Paper exists (has 2019 and 2024 errata, not retracted) and defines 279 lung-function signals (verified via PMC6397078 full text), but **NBEA is not mentioned and 13q13.3 is not among the reported signals.** Only DOCK9 (13:99,665,512) is reported on chromosome 13, well outside the NBEA region. Excluded from the evidence table as non-supporting.
  1. **Shrine N, et al. "Multi-ancestry genome-wide association analyses improve resolution of genes and pathways influencing lung function and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease risk." Nature Genetics 2023;55:410-422.** PMID 36914875, DOI 10.1038/s41588-023-01314-0. Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA among the 559 mapped genes). Paper exists (October 2023 erratum, not retracted) and reports 1,020 signals/559 causal genes (verified via PMC10011137 full text), but **NBEA does not appear anywhere in the text or among the 559 mapped genes.** Genes highlighted include TGFB2, NPNT, LTBP4, FGFR1, GATA5, ABCA3, CLDN18 — not NBEA. This is the single most decisive available check for whether NBEA has independent lung-function GWAS support (per biological prior #2), and it is negative. Excluded from the evidence table as non-supporting.
  1. **Liu M, Jiang Y, Wedow R, et al. "Association studies of up to 1.2 million individuals yield new insights into the genetic etiology of tobacco and alcohol use." Nature Genetics 2019;51(2):237-244.** PMID 30643251, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0307-5. Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA/rs403904 for smoking). Paper exists, not retracted, reports 566 variants/406 loci (verified via PMC6358542 full text), but **NBEA, the 13:35.5-35.75 Mb region, and rs403904 are not mentioned and do not appear among the reported loci.** Excluded from the evidence table as non-supporting.
  1. **Saunders GRB, Wang X, Chen F, et al. "Genetic diversity fuels gene discovery for tobacco and alcohol use." Nature 2022;612(7941):720-724.** PMID 36477530, DOI 10.1038/s41586-022-05477-4. Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA for smoking). Paper exists, not retracted, reports 3,823 variants/2,143 loci (verified via PMC9771818 full text), but **NBEA, 13q13.3, and rs403904 are not mentioned and do not appear among the reported smoking/tobacco loci.** This was the single most important open item flagged for this locus (the researcher hypothesized this paper as the likely source of the Open-Targets-reported NBEA-smoking- initiation association); it is negative. Excluded from the evidence table as non-supporting.

**Material consequence of this finding:** NBEA does not appear, by name or by region, in any of the three major prior COPD/lung-function GWAS (Sakornsakolpat 2019, Shrine 2019, Shrine 2023) or either of the two major smoking/tobacco-use GWAS (GSCAN 2019, Saunders 2022) that were checked. This is independent, verified negative evidence bearing directly on this locus's novelty and on the smoking-mediation hypothesis (prior #1) — see the CONTESTED determination below and the Prioritized Gene section of the main evidence table.

CONTESTED gene determination (red-team-skeptic, verbatim reasoning preserved)

**Gene: NBEA — marked CONTESTED** (with respect to a COPD-relevant pulmonary or smoking-behavior mechanism specifically; NOT contested as the correct candidate gene at this locus, since it is the only protein-coding gene present and all internal methods converge on it).

Full verbatim critique from red-team-skeptic:

1. "Smoking-behavior/neurobiological mechanism" hypothesis — speculative overreach

Orchestrator follow-up to the red-team's thoroughness flag

The red-team-skeptic asked whether a direct lung/immune-tissue eQTL/sQTL check for NBEA (parallel to the Open Targets brain-QTL finding) had actually been performed. The orchestrator ran this check directly against Open Targets' full QTL credible-set catalogue for NBEA (104 credible sets across all tissues/cell types). Result: NBEA does have a bulk lung eQTL (GTEx, lead variant rs562861, 13:35,106,321) and multiple immune-cell eQTLs (BLUEPRINT monocytes; Schmiedel 2018 Th1/Th2/Th17/Tfh/Treg; Nathan 2022 and OneK1K single-cell CD4/NK) in Open Targets — so lung/immune QTL resources for NBEA do exist and were queried. However, none of these lung/immune eQTL signals are fine-mapped to, or overlap, this locus's credible-set variants (rs403904/rs374452); their lead variants are all tens to hundreds of kb away (closest ~74 kb, the NK-cell eQTL). Only the brain exon-usage/splicing QTL (CommonMind DLPFC, ROSMAP) is fine-mapped specifically to rs403904/rs374452. This confirms the brain-restricted finding is a genuine feature of which tissue's QTL signal coincides with this locus's specific variants, not an artifact of unconsulted resources — while still not proving the complete absence of a pulmonary/immune effect at this exact variant, since QTL study power varies substantially by tissue and sample size. Full detail: see the ADDENDUM section of `evidence_tables/_status/13_35500001-35750000/opentargets.md`.
