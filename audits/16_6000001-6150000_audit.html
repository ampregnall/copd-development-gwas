Alternative explanations flagged by red-team-skeptic (not previously considered by researcher)

  1. **Winner's-curse/LD-artifact false positive:** "RBFOX1 is one of the largest genes in the genome (~1.7 Mb), sits in a gene-poor, low-recombination region, and MAGMA and ABC both failed to prioritize any gene here — a notable disagreement with FLAMES/PoPS... Large genes have disproportionately more 'COPD-associated' intronic SNPs simply by virtue of covering more genomic real estate and more measured variants, independent of causal mechanism... a scattershot pleiotropy pattern that is at least as consistent with a statistical/LD magnet effect as with true multi-organ pleiotropy."
  1. **Uncharacterized lung-specific isoform/splicing function:** "RBFOX-family paralogs (RBFOX2 in particular) do have described roles in epithelial/mesenchymal splicing programs, so a lung-relevant RBFOX1 isoform is not biologically implausible, merely unstudied. This is a real 'absence of evidence, not evidence of absence' case."
  1. **Brain sQTL colocalization as a distinct hypothesis (not yet literature-confirmed):** The Open Targets CommonMind DLPFC brain-splicing-QTL cluster overlapping this locus "is a specific, testable mechanism... but it is database context, unconfirmed in peer-reviewed literature, and should not be casually merged into the smoking-mediation narrative as if it were verified support. It's a hypothesis-generating hint, not evidence."
