However, ASTN2/9q33.1/rs7034049 is not mentioned in the abstract, and supplementary locus tables are not accessible to confirm presence/absence of this specific locus." **Why excluded:** The researcher wanted to cite this as likely supporting evidence that ASTN2 is among this landmark COPD GWAS's 82 reported risk loci (on the reasoning that ASTN2 is already a known lung-function locus and 47 of the 82 loci were "previously described"), but this is an inference, not a confirmed fact. The paper's existence and general scope are not in question — only the gene-specific inclusion claim, which could not be verified and is therefore excluded from the evidence table rather than presented as support.

Genes/claims marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

TRIM32 (physically-adjacent gene, not in original pipeline candidate list)

**Skeptic verdict: CONTESTED (for the audit trail).** **Verbatim reasoning:** "Not contested in the sense of literature actively arguing against ASTN2/TRIM32, but contested in the sense that the only COPD-specific (as opposed to spirometry-specific) signal at this locus cannot be attributed to ASTN2 over TRIM32, and TRIM32 has not been run through the pipeline's own gene-prioritization tools. Recommend synthesis-lead either (a) explicitly present TRIM32 as a co-candidate with the same caveat, or (b) task the researcher/a follow-up pass with a proper TRIM32 workup (Open Targets score, GTEx lung expression, ABC/MAGMA if computable) before finalizing."

Additional context: TRIM32 was substituted by the researcher for an erroneous premise in the original dispatch prompt suggesting "ASTN2/CTNNA3 deletion syndromes" are a feature of this locus. The researcher independently verified via Ensembl that CTNNA3 is located on chromosome 10 (65.9-67.8 Mb), not chromosome 9, and found no PubMed literature linking ASTN2 and CTNNA3 at all (a direct PubMed search returned zero results). The well-documented contiguous-gene CNV relationship at this locus (9q33.1) instead pairs ASTN2 with TRIM32 (Lionel et al. 2013, PMID 24381304, VERIFIED), which the researcher substituted as the biologically-relevant physically-adjacent gene. This correction is treated as reliable but was not independently re-confirmed by red-team-skeptic (search budget exhausted for this session — see below).

COL27A1 (PoPS-prioritized candidate gene)

**Skeptic verdict: CONTESTED, trending toward effectively ruled out.** **Verbatim reasoning:** "A 2.6 Mb gap between the gene body and every credible-set variant is far outside typical cis-regulatory range (even generous enhancer-promoter loop distances rarely exceed ~1 Mb without specific Hi-C/chromatin-interaction evidence, which is not presented here). PoPS is explicitly a genome-wide similarity method that does not require or imply physical proximity, so its flag cannot substitute for a distance-based or regulatory (ABC) mechanism, and MAGMA/ABC did not flag COL27A1 at all. Combined with the complete absence of COPD-specific literature and a Mendelian phenotype (Steel syndrome) with no respiratory component across three independent case series, this is about as clean a 'ruled out' case as this pipeline is likely to produce for a PoPS-only candidate... I use CONTESTED rather than a flat 'ruled out' only because I could not independently re-verify the 2.6 Mb Ensembl coordinate claim myself this pass (search budget exhausted) — that specific factual claim (COL27A1 at 9:114,155,486-114,312,511 vs. the credible set at 9:116.9-117.1 Mb) is load-bearing for this entire conclusion and should get one direct independent coordinate check (e.g., Ensembl/UCSC browser) before synthesis-lead treats it as fully settled, even though it was reported as verified fact by the researcher via Ensembl REST."

ASTN2 overall genetic-evidence framing

**Skeptic verdict: WEAKENED** (not a full CONTESTED, but flagged as overclaimed in its original framing). **Verbatim reasoning:** "The researcher's summary line — 'genetics evidence is real and multi-study' — overstates what's actually verified. Strip out the NOT_FOUND sub-claims (ASTN2's presence in the Sakornsakolpat and Shrine locus tables is unconfirmed) and the unconfirmed GSCAN locus-specificity, and the gene-specific genetic backbone is really just: one spirometry GWAS (Soler Artigas 2015) and one COPD gene-based test that cannot distinguish ASTN2 from TRIM32 (Joo & Himes 2022, and that's a conference proceedings paper, not peer-reviewed). 'Multi-study' implies independent replication that hasn't actually been shown."

Joo & Himes 2022 (PMID 35308900) specifically

**Skeptic verdict: CONTESTED** as unambiguous ASTN2-specific evidence (verified as a factual citation, but gene-ambiguous). **Verbatim reasoning:** see TRIM32 section above; the gene-based test "cannot statistically separate the two genes," so this paper cannot be used to claim ASTN2-specific (as opposed to ASTN2-or-TRIM32) COPD association.

Plumb et al. 2011 mouse lung-defect interpretation (PMID 22206015)

**Skeptic verdict: CONTESTED** (researcher's causal interpretation, not the citation itself). **Verbatim reasoning:** "The interpretation of [the mouse lung defect] as 'likely a secondary restrictive/chest-wall mechanism... not a primary airway/parenchymal defect' is explicitly flagged by the researcher as their own unconfirmed interpretation, not something drawn from the abstract... we do not actually know from verified text whether the mouse lung phenotype is secondary-mechanical or primary-parenchymal."

Seo et al. 2026 (PMID 42329017) and Tan et al. 2025 (PMID 40034336)

**Skeptic verdict: CONTESTED** for relevance to COPD specifically (citations themselves VERIFIED). **Reasoning:** Both papers study idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis / TGF-β1-induced EMT models, which are mechanistically distinct fibrotic-lung-disease contexts from COPD/emphysema; COL27A1's role in both is enrichment-analysis/expression-level, not independently validated as functionally causal even within those non-COPD disease models. Included in the main evidence table as general COL27A1 lung-biology context, but explicitly not COPD evidence.
