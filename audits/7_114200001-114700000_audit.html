Genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

MDFIC — CONTESTED as the causal/prioritized gene at this locus

Verbatim from the red-team-skeptic's critique:

**Verdict on MDFIC: CONTESTED.** I'm marking this CONTESTED rather than merely WEAKENED because there is a genuine, multi-source case against MDFIC as the causal gene at this locus: (1) it lies outside the physical credible-set interval with no regulatory link, (2) Open Targets' own L2G model — the same class of tool used to prioritize it — actually favors FOXP2 by an order of magnitude at this variant, (3) MDFIC has no genetic-association evidence for COPD in Open Targets' aggregate (score driven entirely by literature, and even that literature is not COPD-specific), and (4) every piece of literature evidence assembled is non-pulmonary or only cell-line/cross-context lung-adjacent (one A549 GR study). This isn't a single contradicting paper so much as a convergent absence of support plus a positional/statistical mismatch — which is exactly the pattern the LD/nearest-gene-bias prior warns about, except inverted (MDFIC is the distal, non-credible-set gene being favored by burden/PoPS-type statistical methods over the gene that actually contains every fine-mapped variant). Synthesis-lead should treat FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS prioritization of MDFIC as fragile and likely reflecting a false-positive gene-level statistical signal (e.g., driven by MDFIC's baseline expression/connectivity profile in PoPS features, or by LD/distance artifacts in MAGMA) rather than genuine causal biology, pending any ABC/regulatory evidence connecting the credible-set interval to MDFIC that has not yet been found.

FOXP2 — direct pulmonary-biology mechanism WEAKENED (gene assignment itself HOLDS)

Verbatim from the red-team-skeptic's critique:

**Verdict on FOXP2:** **WEAKENED.** The fine-mapping/gene-assignment inference (this locus = FOXP2, not a neighbor) is sound. But the case that FOXP2 drives COPD via *direct pulmonary biology* is weak and is actively contradicted by a well-powered lung-function GWAS null result (PMID 36221131) and by the overwhelmingly neuropsychiatric/behavioral pattern of L2G hits genome-wide. The strongest statistical-genetics evidence (antisocial behavior GWAS, rg with smoking) points toward smoking-behavior mediation (prior #1) rather than direct airway biology. The one lung-epithelial mechanistic finding is from an IPF model and should not be presented as established COPD mechanism without that caveat front and center. I would not call this CONTESTED at the level of "wrong gene at the locus" (fine-mapping is unambiguous), but I would flag the "FOXP2 → COPD via pulmonary biology" narrative specifically as contested/undercut by direct evidence.

Disclosed coverage gaps (from the red-team-skeptic)

The red-team-skeptic's own independent contradiction-search calls for this locus returned "search budget exhausted" (0 additional searches executed on its end). Its critique above is based entirely on re-weighing the researcher's already-verified findings plus the Open Targets structured lookup provided in its dispatch prompt — it did not independently confirm or extend the contradiction search with new literature. This is disclosed here as a coverage gap: neither FOXP2 nor MDFIC received an independent second-pass literature search from the skeptic beyond critique of existing evidence.

The skeptic additionally flagged that the "no ABC link reported" statement for MDFIC should be treated as **unresolved**, not settled-negative — it may reflect either a genuine absence of regulatory linkage or simply that this pipeline's upstream ABC prioritization step was not run/did not surface a hit for this locus, and a dedicated chromatin-interaction follow-up was not performed in this review.
