Skeptic's overall summary judgment (verbatim)

"The most defensible overall picture, given only what's verified, is: STAG1 has the strongest statistical/positional case but zero mechanistic lung evidence (statistics-only prioritization, appropriately caveated); PPP2R3A has the most specific (if indirect and non-COPD) structural-lung biology; NCK1's T-cell-signaling story is real biology being stretched to fit a COPD inflammatory prior without a shred of COPD- or lung-specific data, and its lack of LoF constraint further weakens a common-variant dosage argument; PCCB's high aggregate score is correctly identified by the researcher as very likely a scoring artifact and should not be carried forward as a serious candidate; IL20RB lacks any directly-annotated variant and should be demoted from 'candidate' to 'positional note' pending better regulatory evidence; MSL2 and SLC35G2 are both accurately characterized as weak with no overreach. None of the genes at this locus currently have concordant FEV1/FEV1-FVC spirometric evidence or smoking-behavior-trait data (both explicitly absent, per the prompt), which — per priors #1 and #2 — should keep the whole locus's causal-gene assignment at low-to-moderate confidence regardless of which single gene synthesis ultimately favors."

Other notes for reviewers

- **Name-collision risk:** the symbol "STAG1" ambiguously matches two Open Targets/Ensembl records — the intended gene (ENSG00000118007, cohesin subunit) and PMEPA1 (ENSG00000124225, an unrelated TGF-beta/androgen-responsive transmembrane protein with an old alias also rendered "STAG1"). All literature and Open Targets data in the evidence table were confirmed to be about the cohesin subunit; no PMEPA1 mislabeling was found in the researcher's search results, but this remains a risk for any future re-search of this locus. - **Gene-assignment ambiguity at the 3' boundary:** the two NCK1-annotated 3'UTR credible-set variants (rs856662, rs835647; PIP=0.0057 each) sit essentially at the transcription start site of the neighboring gene IL20RB, meaning these variants are plausible IL20RB promoter/regulatory variants as well as NCK1 3'UTR variants. This ambiguity is unresolved by current evidence (no eQTL or chromatin-contact data available for either gene at this specific position) and is preserved here rather than arbitrarily assigned. - **Open Targets colocalization gap:** no molecular-QTL colocalization data was retrievable for any gene at this locus through the available Open Targets GraphQL query surface. This is explicitly a limitation of the API layer accessible to this pipeline, not confirmed evidence of a true absence of colocalization — a caveat that should not be forgotten when reading the "no colocalization evidence" rows in the Open Targets summary as if they were negative findings.
