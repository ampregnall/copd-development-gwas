HACD2 — Verdict: WEAKENED

The locus-assignment evidence for HACD2 is genuinely strong on its own terms (26/26 credible-set variants, consistent OT L2G ranking 0.61-0.76 at this exact position). But that evidence is entirely about *which gene sits under the statistical signal*, not about *what the signal does biologically* — and on the latter question there is a total vacuum: zero COPD literature, zero FEV1/FVC literature, and the one disease-relevant claim in Open Targets (smoking initiation, score 0.465) could not be independently verified in primary literature at all. Two things deserve more skepticism than the researcher gave them: 1. The OT fine-mapping evidence being leaned on is for body-fat-percentage, not COPD. Using "HACD2 is top L2G gene at this variant" as support for a COPD-prioritization decision silently substitutes a different phenotype's fine-mapping for this one's... Combined with HACD2's top disease hits being obesity/T2D/corneal-neovascularization/ sinusitis (not respiratory), a live alternative hypothesis is that this locus is fundamentally an adiposity/metabolic locus, and its COPD-GWAS signal... could reflect obesity's mechanical effects on spirometry (restrictive pattern lowering FVC, confounding FEV1/FVC ratio) rather than any obstructive-lung mechanism. 2. gnomAD pLoF oe=0.41 with CI 0.20-0.94 is appropriately described as wide/uncertain by the researcher — good, no overclaiming there. **Verdict: WEAKENED.** The gene-assignment (HACD2 = the gene under the credible set) holds up; the *inference that this represents lung-relevant COPD biology* does not — it rests on statistical fine-mapping borrowed from an unrelated trait (adiposity) plus an unverified single-database smoking-initiation flag. This is not CONTESTED in the sense of a paper actively arguing against HACD2 — no such paper was found or (given my search failure) searched for — but the case for a lung mechanism is essentially unsupported and an adiposity-mediated alternative is at least as plausible as anything currently on the table.

MYLK — Verdict: CONTESTED

MYLK has a real, verifiable literature base (asthma genetics, ALI/ARDS, sepsis, nmMLCK mouse knockouts, endothelial permeability biology), and the researcher did not overclaim this — it explicitly said "biologically plausible" rather than "causal for COPD," and explicitly separated asthma/acute-lung-injury biology from chronic-airflow-obstruction biology... none of the seven MYLK citations concern COPD or chronic airflow obstruction. They cluster in an acute/allergic inflammatory paradigm... that is mechanistically and temporally distinct from COPD's chronic, often smoking-driven, structural remodeling and neutrophilic/CD8+ inflammatory biology described in the priors. Generalizing "MYLK affects airway/vascular smooth muscle and permeability in asthma and ALI" to "MYLK is a COPD gene" would be an unwarranted leap. More importantly, there is a genuine internal contradiction the researcher surfaced but didn't push hard enough on: MYLK's nomination rests entirely on FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS gene-level scores, with zero credible-set variant overlap and zero eQTL/Hi-C/ABC link to the HACD2 interval — and Open Targets' own independent fine-mapping/L2G data at this exact lead variant (across multiple traits) never ranks MYLK among candidates at all, consistently favoring HACD2/ADCY5/PDIA5/CCDC14/ROPN1 instead. This is a structural, not merely absence-of-evidence, contradiction... A known limitation of PoPS/MAGMA-style methods is that they can be biased toward genes with rich pre-existing disease/pathway annotation (MYLK has abundant asthma/ALI literature feeding into feature similarity) even when that gene is not the true regulatory target of the local variants — which is exactly the pattern here. **Verdict: CONTESTED.** The contradiction is that Open Targets' own independent fine-mapping/L2G evidence at the exact lead-variant position directly disfavors MYLK... while favoring HACD2 and several unexamined neighbors... Combine that with the fact that MYLK's entire literature support is for a different disease context (asthma/ALI, not COPD/FEV1), and the case for MYLK at this locus is weaker than the researcher's "biological plausibility" framing suggests, even though I could not run new searches to find an explicit "MYLK is not the causal gene at 3q21 for COPD" paper.

Alternative genes at this locus (ADCY5, PDIA5, CCDC14, ROPN1) — not evaluated, flagged as coverage gap

This is a real gap, and it exists independent of anything the researcher did wrong — these genes were never evaluated because they are outside the pipeline's internal candidate-gene list, yet Open Targets' own fine-mapping ranks them as L2G alternatives to HACD2 at the exact lead variant for the (admittedly different, adiposity-related) traits it has ingested. I was not able to search these genes at all (budget exhausted before I could query ADCY5/PDIA5/CCDC14/ROPN1 individually). ADCY5 in particular is worth flagging by name to synthesis-lead/researcher for a follow-up pass: from general domain knowledge (not a verified search result — flag as needing verification) ADCY5 is a well-established GWAS locus for fasting glucose/T2D/birth weight, which would be consistent with the adiposity/metabolic-pleiotropy hypothesis raised above for HACD2, but I have no verified evidence either way on a direct lung-function link. This should be logged as an explicit coverage gap: the candidate-gene list itself may be incomplete at this locus, and synthesis-lead should decide whether to route ADCY5/PDIA5/CCDC14/ROPN1 back to the researcher for a proper pass before finalizing HACD2-vs-MYLK as the only two contenders.

Other process observations from the critic

- Researcher's treatment of the Russell 2010 negative replication (MYLK, no association with lung-injury severity in CAP) alongside the positive ALI papers is good practice — it wasn't cherry-picked out. - Ancestry specificity of the two main MYLK-asthma citations (Flores 2007, Gao 2007 — both largely African-ancestry cohorts) is stated by the researcher but not flagged as a limitation on generalizability to whatever ancestry composition underlies this COPD GWAS. Synthesis should treat the asthma-genetics support for MYLK as ancestry-restricted, further narrowing its relevance. - No instance found of the researcher upgrading "associated with" to "causes" — the two "Researcher's overall judgment" paragraphs for both genes are appropriately hedged and flag the HACD2/MYLK discordance explicitly for synthesis rather than resolving it themselves, which is the correct behavior at this stage.
