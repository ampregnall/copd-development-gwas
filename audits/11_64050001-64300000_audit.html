Audit Report - Locus 11:64050001-64300000 (nearest genes FLRT1, MACROD1)

This file preserves the full verification and critique trail for reviewers who want to inspect what was excluded, contested, or weakened, separate from the clean evidence table in `evidence_tables/11_64050001-64300000.md`.

Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

**None.** All 29 citations submitted by the researcher were returned VERIFIED by the citation-verifier, including two unusually high PMIDs (42362029, 42574321 — both 2026 publications on FERMT3/kindlin-3) that were specifically flagged by the orchestrator for extra scrutiny given their implausibly-high-looking PMID numbers; both were independently confirmed to resolve to real, matching articles with matching authors, journals, and claims. See `evidence_tables/_status/11_64050001-64300000/verified_citations.md` for the full citation-by-citation verification record.

One genuine name-collision risk was caught and self-corrected by the researcher (not an excluded citation, but worth recording): several initial "STIP1" search hits were actually about the distinct gene **STUB1** ("STIP1 homology and U-box containing protein 1"), including a paper on cigarette-smoke-induced bronchial epithelial senescence in COPD (PMID 40895192, among others). These were excluded from STIP1's evidence base by the researcher before submission and were never sent to citation-verifier as STIP1 evidence. The one STIP1 citation that was submitted (PMID 33208131) was independently re-confirmed by citation-verifier to genuinely concern STIP1 (stress-induced phosphoprotein 1), not STUB1.

Genes flagged CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (reasoning preserved verbatim)

RPS6KA4 — CONTESTED

This is the most overclaimed gene in the set. The "strong dual-pathway candidate" framing conjoins one UNVERIFIED database association (smoking initiation/cessation, not independently confirmed by PubMed or GWAS Catalog lookup — the researcher's own session says so) with one VERIFIED but generic mechanism (MSK2 suppressing macrophage IFN-β/ cytokine output via DUSP1) that has no lung or COPD specificity at all — the other four MSK1/2 citations are pancreas, kidney, colon, and breast biology. Critically, RPS6KA4 has zero credible-set variant support — it's a MAGMA/PoPS gene-level prioritization only, meaning the statistical case rests on genomic proximity/expression-pattern heuristics rather than fine-mapped causal variants. Combining an unconfirmed smoking claim with a tissue-nonspecific immune mechanism and calling the composite "strong" overstates what's actually nailed down. The prior #1 (smoking-mediation) framing is doing rhetorical work here that the evidence doesn't yet support. CONTESTED: not because I found literature actively contradicting RPS6KA4, but because the "strong dual-pathway candidate" characterization is not supported by what's actually verified — one pillar (smoking) is explicitly unconfirmed and the other (macrophage inflammation) is non-lung-specific. This should be downgraded to "plausible but unconfirmed on both pillars" pending (a) an independent GWAS Catalog/PhenoScanner check on the smoking association, and (b) any lung/airway-macrophage-specific MSK2 literature. Flag for synthesis-lead: the RPS6KA4-smoking claim should be re-attempted via GWAS Catalog API or PhenoScanner directly (not PubMed, since it's a structured-database association, not a paper) before it's allowed to influence prioritization language.

FLRT1 — WEAKENED

The researcher is right that no relevant literature exists, but "no biology found" is being allowed to do more work than it should. This gene carries the lead variant and the top PIP mass, is intronic/5'UTR (i.e., a plausible regulatory-variant host), yet the write-up leans on FLRT1's coding biology (axon guidance, FGFR potentiation) and paralog biology (FLRT2/3 in cortical migration) as if absence-of-fit there closes the case. It doesn't: an intronic credible-set variant in FLRT1 could equally be acting as a distal eQTL for a neighboring gene (RPS6KA4, PLCB3, STIP1, FERMT3 are all within the stated 250kb window) and simply be "hosted" in FLRT1 without FLRT1 being the functional gene at all. No GTEx/lung-eQTL or colocalization evidence is reported for FLRT1 itself — the researcher's dossier is silent on whether FLRT1 expression correlates with the credible-set variants in any tissue, lung or otherwise. That's a real gap, not a minor one, given FLRT1 is nominally the top candidate by fine-mapping alone. WEAKENED: the "no biology" framing is being used to deprioritize the top-PIP gene without ruling out the LD/regulatory-target-uncertainty alternative (variant regulates a different gene) — this needs an eQTL/coloc check before being written off, and the case as presented is not sound for confidently deprioritizing FLRT1, nor for prioritizing it; it's simply unresolved.

PLCB3 — WEAKENED

This is the second most concerning overclaim. The researcher explicitly reports a null result: the tested PLCB3 missense variant (rs35169799) was NOT significantly associated with COPD in a 9,888/27,060 case-control meta-analysis (OR=1.05, P=0.247). The researcher's defense — "different variant, doesn't bear on the common-variant GWAS signal" — is a reasonable technical point (a null rare-variant association genuinely doesn't rule out a separate common regulatory variant's effect), but it is being used to fully neutralize a genuine negative data point rather than to merely limit its scope. The remaining "mechanistic" evidence is a grab-bag: CF bronchial epithelium IL-8 (a distinct disease context, cystic fibrosis not COPD), MUC5AC mucin regulation, and FcɛRI/mast-cell allergic inflammation (Th2/allergic axis, arguably in tension with prior #3's neutrophilic/CD8/Th1/Th17 COPD-relevant programs, and not clearly reconciled with COPD's non-allergic phenotype). Calling this "the strongest mechanistically supported gene at this locus" overstates coherence — it's a diverse set of PLCB3-airway-inflammation papers across different diseases (CF, allergic asthma, generic airway epithelium) that were assembled post hoc, alongside one directly negative COPD genetic result for a different variant in the same gene. WEAKENED: the null GWAS result deserves more weight than "doesn't bear on this" — at minimum it shows PLCB3 coding variation is not obviously COPD-associated at reasonable power, which is relevant context that should be surfaced in synthesis, not filed away. The "strongest mechanistically supported" superlative should be softened to something like "mechanistically plausible across multiple airway-inflammatory contexts, but with an existing negative COPD genetic association for a different variant and no COPD-specific positive evidence."

MACROD1, FERMT3, NUDT22, PPP1R14B, FKBP2, STIP1 — HOLDS (no overreach detected)

The skeptic reviewed each of these and found the researcher's hedged conclusions appropriately proportionate to the (generally thin) evidence — none were contested or weakened. Verbatim notes of interest:

FERMT3 — ...is exactly the right hedge given kindlin-3/LAD-III causes severe monogenic leukocyte-adhesion deficiency (extreme, well-established, but a rare Mendelian phenotype, not a graded COPD-relevant common-variant mechanism) and the Open Targets COPD tag is explicitly text-mining, not genetic. The researcher does not overclaim here — it flags the evidence as "thin for COPD specifically" despite compelling biology. HOLDS as an honestly-hedged low/moderate-confidence candidate; no overreach detected.
STIP1 — I'd flag the researcher's active exclusion of STUB1 papers (including a cigarette-smoke/bronchial-senescence COPD paper) as a genuinely good catch — that's exactly the kind of gene-name-collision error this locus, sitting near a lot of short similarly-named genes, could easily produce, and the researcher's discipline here should be noted positively to synthesis-lead.

Genes/evidence flagged as scope creep (background only — no fine-mapping/MAGMA/PoPS support)

The researcher additionally enumerated all protein-coding genes physically within the 11:64,050,001-64,300,000 region via an Ensembl overlap lookup, beyond the genes flagged by the pipeline's own prioritization methods (nearest gene, MAGMA, PoPS, credible-set annotation). The skeptic flagged this expansion explicitly:

This is scope creep, and should be flagged as such. None of these genes carry any credible-set variant, MAGMA, or PoPS support — they were pulled in purely by physical-overlap lookup. Including VEGFB (hypoxic vascular remodeling) and KCNK4 (airway mechanosensory neurons) as if they were on par with the fine-mapped/prioritized genes above risks diluting the evidence table and implicitly suggesting these deserve comparable prioritization weight... Flag for synthesis-lead: rank/label these six separately (e.g., "no fine-mapping or MAGMA/PoPS support — background only") so the final table doesn't imply they're competitive candidates.

Per this guidance, VEGFB and KCNK4 (which had citable literature) are retained in the main evidence table but explicitly labeled "no fine-mapping/MAGMA/PoPS support — background only" in the Candidate gene column, and are excluded from the Prioritized-gene synthesis comparison entirely. BAD, GPR137, DNAJC4, and TRPT1 (which had no COPD/lung/immune-relevant literature identified at all) are omitted from the main evidence table and are recorded here only: the researcher searched each and found no plausible connection to COPD or pulmonary/ immune biology in this session (see `evidence_tables/_status/11_64050001-64300000/researcher.md` for the per-gene notes).

Cross-cutting gaps flagged by red-team-skeptic (not gene-specific)

No eQTL or colocalization evidence is reported anywhere in this dossier for any gene at the locus (GTEx lung, or any other tissue). For a locus where the lead variant is intronic and the credible set spans 8+ genes across ~250kb, distinguishing "which gene does this regulatory variant actually act on" is the single most important open question, and it appears untouched.
Smoking-mediation (prior #1) is under-evaluated overall. Only RPS6KA4 has any smoking-trait association claim, and it's unverified. No gene at this locus is reported to have FEV1/FEV1/FVC spirometry-trait support (prior #2) either — this is worth stating plainly in synthesis: at present, no gene at this locus has both a smoking-independent lung-function signal AND a validated mechanism, which should push overall locus confidence down regardless of which single gene is favored.
Overall locus verdict: given (a) no eQTL/coloc data pinning any regulatory variant to a specific gene, (b) the two highest-PIP genes (FLRT1, MACROD1) having literally no disease-relevant biology, (c) the MAGMA/PoPS gene (RPS6KA4) resting on one unverified claim plus one non-lung-specific mechanism, and (d) the best mechanistic story (PLCB3) coexisting with a negative genetic association — I'd characterize this locus overall as weakly resolved, and recommend synthesis-lead not present any single gene here with high confidence language.
