Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

CAMKMT — CONTESTED

The red-team-skeptic's full reasoning (transcribed in `evidence_tables/_status/2_44800001-45050000/critique.md`) is preserved here verbatim:

Several independent lines argue against this gene, and it should be flagged as CONTESTED rather than simply "no evidence": - Zero COPD/lung/immune/eQTL literature (researcher's own search). - Open Targets L2G assigns essentially zero weight to CAMKMT for COPD (0.00665, literature-only) and, in every one of ~28 other-trait credible-set rows examined, the L2G algorithm picks SIX3, never CAMKMT — a direct methodological contradiction between the internal pipeline (FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS unanimously prioritizing CAMKMT) and an independent genetics-based prioritization method. That disagreement is itself evidence worth surfacing, not just noting in passing. - Bartholdi et al. 2013 describes a human contiguous-deletion syndrome spanning CAMKMT (PREPL + CAMKMT +/- SLC3A1) with hypotonia, growth failure, cleft palate, and genital anomalies — and no reported pulmonary phenotype. This is a stronger form of negative evidence than "no papers found": it's a human loss-of-function model with systematic phenotyping that did not turn up lung involvement. This is genuine contradicting evidence and should be treated as such. - CAMKMT tolerates LoF well (o/e 1.20, not constrained) — consistent with, though not proof against, a non-essential/non-lung role. - The physical-mapping claim (CAMKMT ending ~24kb before the window start, i.e., entirely outside 2:44,800,001-45,050,000) is the most serious issue. [The critic could not independently re-verify this via search due to exhausted budget; the orchestrator subsequently confirmed it independently via Open Targets' genomic-location field — see below.] If this coordinate claim holds up under independent verification, it means three of four gene-prioritization methods (FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS) nominated a gene that isn't even inside the defined credible-set locus window, which is a serious flag about those methods' distance-weighting behavior for this locus (CAMKMT is a large gene, which is exactly the failure mode where MAGMA/PoPS gene-based tests can over-attribute signal to large flanking genes). Given the convergence of (i) zero disease-relevant literature, (ii) a human deletion model with no pulmonary phenotype, (iii) contradiction from an independent genetic-prioritization algorithm, and (iv) an unresolved but plausible physical-mapping disqualifier, CAMKMT should be marked CONTESTED, with an explicit recommendation to synthesis-lead: verify the Ensembl coordinates independently before treating CAMKMT as in-locus at all; if confirmed outside the window, the FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS unanimity should be substantially discounted as a proximity/gene-size artifact rather than treated as convergent multi-method support.

**Orchestrator follow-up (coordinate verification):** independently queried via Open Targets' own genomic-location field (separate from the researcher's original Ensembl REST lookup): CAMKMT chr2:44,361,947-44,772,592 (GRCh38); locus window is 2:44,800,001-45,050,000. This confirms CAMKMT ends ~27.4 kb before the window start and lies physically outside the defined locus. The critic's recommended discount of FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS unanimity for CAMKMT as a likely proximity/gene-size artifact is adopted in the final synthesis (see main evidence table's "Prioritized gene" section) rather than left open.

SIX3 — WEAKENED (not fully CONTESTED, but flagged — included here for completeness)

The skeptic did not contest SIX3's standing as the best-supported gene by fine-mapping/L2G criteria, but explicitly WEAKENED any framing of SIX3 as an established "lung-intrinsic COPD gene" or as a settled "smoking-behavior gene." Verbatim reasoning:

The statistical case for SIX3 as the gene at this locus is strong and multiply convergent (nearest gene, PIP 0.579+0.366 ~= 0.945 in intergenic variants nearest SIX3, Open Targets L2G 0.7145 — the highest-confidence tier). But the researcher is right to hedge, and the synthesis-lead should not let "statistically implicated" collapse into "COPD-relevant." [...] Smoking-mediation adequacy: this rests on exactly one MTAG paper (Cheng et al. 2023) showing an AUD/smoking-status colocalization at SIX3, plus an unverified Open Targets pattern [...]. That pattern is suggestive and internally consistent with prior #1, but it is database output, not peer-reviewed literature [...]. It should be stated as the best-supported hypothesis, not a conclusion. [...] The complete absence of any FEV1/FEV1-FVC signal [...] is itself important disconfirming evidence against a lung-intrinsic mechanism, per prior #2 — this cuts for the smoking-pathway interpretation by elimination, not because smoking-mediation is independently well-evidenced.

This WEAKENED framing is carried through into the main evidence table's "Prioritized gene" section (confidence capped at LOW).

SIX2 — WEAKENED / not supported

Skeptic's verbatim assessment: "No pipeline method flagged SIX2; the researcher added it out of due diligence and found only renal biology (nephron progenitor maintenance, CAKUT), with the cited paper (Faguer 2012) actually reporting a null finding in its own cohort. There is a known biological pathway connecting severe renal developmental defects to secondary pulmonary hypoplasia (oligohydramnios sequence), but that is a distinct prenatal-mechanical mechanism irrelevant to adult-onset airflow obstruction, and nothing in the evidence base invokes it. No overclaiming here — the researcher correctly does not push this. Keep SIX2 in the record as a documented negative for completeness but it shouldn't compete with SIX3 in synthesis."

LINC01833 — insufficient evidence, flagged as an underweighted alternative mechanism

Skeptic's verbatim note: "No functional or disease literature exists; correctly not elevated by the researcher. Worth naming explicitly, though, as a placeholder for an underweighted alternative: a regulatory/enhancer mechanism. SIX3 is a classic developmental homeodomain transcription factor known [...] to be controlled by long-range, gene-desert cis-regulatory enhancers — the intergenic PIP-dominant credible-set architecture (lead + second variant both intergenic, PIP 0.579/0.366) is exactly the pattern expected if these variants sit in a distal regulatory element for SIX3 (or conceivably LINC01833 acting as a host/regulatory transcript) rather than acting through any protein-coding change. [...] This is a genuine gap: absence of ABC/eQTL support doesn't argue against SIX3, but it also means the 'SIX3 vs. an intergenic regulatory element with unknown target' question was not resolved, only assumed in SIX3's favor by proximity."

Process/limitation notes

Its critique above is therefore based entirely on interpretation/logic applied to the researcher's verified findings and the Open Targets context supplied in its dispatch prompt, not on independent literature re-discovery. The one load-bearing factual claim it flagged as unverified (CAMKMT's physical location relative to the locus window) was independently confirmed by the orchestrator via a second data source (Open Targets genomic-location field) before finalizing this synthesis. The skeptic's second recommended follow-up — a targeted search for SIX3 long-range enhancer/regulatory literature (e.g., "SIX3 enhancer," "SIX3-AS1 regulatory element") — was NOT performed in this pipeline run and remains an open gap; it is carried forward as a stated limitation in the main evidence table's "Prioritized gene" section rather than resolved. - Per pipeline scope rules, only the lead variant (rs501964) and the second highest-PIP variant (rs11692742) received dedicated statistical-genetics/colocalization search; the remaining six credible-set variants (all PIP<0.03, all intergenic/intronic/non-coding, none missense/splice) were rolled into gene-level evidence per instructions and were not searched individually. This is expected pipeline behavior, not a completeness gap.
