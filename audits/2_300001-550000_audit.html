Audit Report - Locus 2:300001-550000 (nearest gene LOC124905962)

This file records every citation NOT admitted to the evidence table (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH verdicts, or verifier verdicts treated as equivalent — see orchestrator note below), plus every gene the red-team-skeptic marked CONTESTED or otherwise flagged, with reasoning preserved verbatim from the verifier and skeptic. Evidence tables are meant to be handed out without this file; this file is the traceability record for how exclusions were decided.

Excluded citations

1. Thorleifsson G et al., "Genome-wide association yields new sequence variants at seven loci that associate with measures of obesity," Nature Genetics, 2009. PMID 19079260.

**Claim as stated by researcher:** independently identified the same TMEM18 locus among seven new obesity/BMI loci in an Icelandic-led GWAS.

**Citation-verifier verdict:** NOT_VERIFIED — "The abstract and MEDLINE record do not explicitly list the seven new loci by name. While the abstract mentions 'variants at seven loci not previously connected with obesity,' TMEM18 is not named in the accessible abstract, and the full results table listing all seven loci is not available in the abstract or MEDLINE record. Cannot confirm TMEM18 is among the named loci."

**Orchestrator treatment:** Treated as CLAIM_MISMATCH (paper is real and not disputed; the specific claim that TMEM18 is among its seven named loci could not be substantiated from accessible text) and excluded from the evidence table on that basis. This is a conservative treatment given the verifier's tool-access limits, not a determination that the claim is false.

2. Liu M et al., "Association studies of up to 1.2 million individuals yield new insights into the genetic etiology of tobacco and alcohol use," Nature Genetics, 2019 (GSCAN consortium). PMID 30643251.

**Claim as stated by researcher:** primary GWAS of smoking initiation/cessation/heaviness and alcohol use (406 loci); cited as the likely primary source underlying TMEM18's Open-Targets-aggregated smoking-initiation/cessation/behavior association scores.

**Citation-verifier verdict:** NOT_VERIFIED (Critical Claim Unconfirmed) — "the paper identifies 566 genetic variants in 406 loci for tobacco and alcohol use. However, the abstract and full text do not explicitly mention TMEM18 or the chr2p25.3 region among the named loci for smoking initiation, smoking cessation, or cigarettes-per-day. The supplementary tables listing all 406 loci are referenced but not accessible in the full text provided. Cannot confirm TMEM18 is a lead locus for any smoking phenotype in this paper."

**Orchestrator treatment:** Treated as CLAIM_MISMATCH and excluded. This is the single most consequential exclusion for this locus's synthesis: it means the "TMEM18 acts via smoking-initiation biology" hypothesis is NOT supported by a verified locus-level citation, despite Open Targets' aggregated smoking-trait scores for TMEM18 — those scores may reflect broader multi-trait/LD-based aggregation rather than a confirmed genome-wide-significant hit for TMEM18 specifically in GSCAN.

3. Sakornsakolpat P et al., "Genetic landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype associations," Nature Genetics, 2019. PMID 30804561.

**Claim as stated by researcher:** the major COPD case-control GWAS meta-analysis (82 loci); cited as a reference framework to check whether TMEM18/chr2p25.3 is a named COPD locus.

**Citation-verifier verdict:** NOT_VERIFIED (Critical Claim Unconfirmed) — "the paper identifies 82 COPD loci. The full text does not mention TMEM18 or chr2p25.3 among the named candidate genes or loci. Supplementary tables listing all 82 loci are referenced but individual loci are not enumerated in the main text. Cannot confirm TMEM18/chr2p25.3 is among the genome-wide significant COPD loci."

**Orchestrator treatment:** Treated as CLAIM_MISMATCH and excluded. This is the second of three consequential exclusions: TMEM18 could not be confirmed as a genome-wide-significant COPD-susceptibility locus in the field's primary COPD case-control GWAS.

4. Shrine N et al., "New genetic signals for lung function highlight pathways and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease associations across multiple ancestries," Nature Genetics, 2019. PMID 30804560.

**Claim as stated by researcher:** the major SpiroMeta/UK Biobank lung-function GWAS (279 signals, 139 new); cited as a reference framework to check whether TMEM18/chr2p25.3 is a named lung-function locus.

**Citation-verifier verdict:** NOT_VERIFIED (Critical Claim Unconfirmed) — "the paper reports 279 lung function signals (139 new). The full text does not mention TMEM18 or chr2p25.3 among the 107 putative causal genes identified, nor is this locus listed among named signals. Cannot confirm TMEM18/chr2p25.3 is among the genome-wide significant lung-function loci."

**Orchestrator treatment:** Treated as CLAIM_MISMATCH and excluded. This is the third of three consequential exclusions: TMEM18 could not be confirmed as a genome-wide-significant lung-function (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) locus either. Combined with exclusions #2 and #3 above, all three of the primary GWAS papers that could have substantiated a direct COPD/lung-function/ smoking-initiation mechanism for TMEM18 at this exact locus failed verification.

Orchestrator note on verifier verdict labeling

The citation-verifier used the non-canonical label "NOT_VERIFIED" (rather than one of the four defined verdicts: VERIFIED / RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH) for the four citations above. In each case the underlying paper was not disputed as existing/real — only the specific TMEM18/chr2p25.3-locus claim attributed to it could not be confirmed from the abstract/metadata accessible to the verifier's tools. These were mapped to CLAIM_MISMATCH for the purposes of the evidence table's "VERIFIED-only" inclusion rule.

Genes flagged CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic

TMEM18 — CONTESTED

Red-team-skeptic's full reasoning (verbatim from critique):

"This is the crux of the locus, and it does not hold up well. **Physical/LD bias:** TMEM18 sits 110-240kb outside the 2:300,001-550,000 interval and does not overlap the credible set at all. The lead variant (PIP=0.351) and the second-ranked variant (PIP=0.224) both sit physically inside LINC01865. TMEM18's candidacy here rests entirely on 'nearest protein-coding gene' logic plus its independent fame as a well-studied obesity gene — exactly the streetlight-effect pattern the framework warns about. Open Targets' own statement that there is 'no locus-specific L2G/colocalization available' for TMEM18 confirms that even a dedicated variant-to-gene algorithm finds no direct evidence tying this specific signal to TMEM18; the case is prior-based, not colocalization-based.

**Pattern of rejected citations:** Every single citation that would have established a locus-specific COPD, lung-function, or smoking-initiation link for TMEM18 came back CLAIM_MISMATCH — Liu/GSCAN (smoking), Sakornsakolpat (COPD GWAS), and Shrine (lung-function GWAS) all failed to confirm TMEM18/chr2p25.3 is even a named locus in those papers. Three-for-three mismatches on exactly the citations needed to support the 'TMEM18 acts on COPD via smoking or lung function' story is not noise — it's a strong signal that this locus is not actually a hit in the largest smoking, COPD, or spirometry GWASes, or if it is, the researcher could not substantiate it from accessible text. This is corroborated independently by Open Targets: no genetic_association score for COPD, no FEV1/FEV1-FVC trait anywhere in TMEM18's disease list at all — a direct contradiction of prior 2 (spirometric concordance), which the researcher and I should state explicitly rather than paper over.

**Overclaiming on the BMI/obesity mechanism:** Yes, this is overclaimed in the synthesis. Çolak et al. is being used to bridge 'TMEM18 -> obesity -> COPD,' but the actual study (a) uses TMEM18 as only 1 of 3 SNPs in a composite BMI genetic instrument, not as an isolated exposure; (b) is conducted entirely within already-diagnosed, spirometrically-confirmed COPD patients; and (c) measures exacerbation/pneumonia rate, not COPD susceptibility/ case-control risk. Converting this into 'TMEM18 is mechanistically linked to COPD via BMI' — which is close to how the researcher's overall conclusion frames it — inflates a study about exacerbation risk-in-existing-disease into evidence of a susceptibility locus. Those are different questions, and the paper answers only the former, weakly, and only via a multi-SNP instrument.

**Smoking-mediation:** Open Targets shows aggregated smoking-initiation/behavior/cessation scores (0.399/0.369/0.259), but since the one citation that could have shown TMEM18 as a genome-wide-significant smoking-initiation locus (GSCAN) was CLAIM_MISMATCH, it's unclear whether these OT scores derive from a genuine independent GWAS hit at this locus or from broader multi-trait/LD-based aggregation. The smoking-mediation hypothesis for TMEM18 is therefore also unconfirmed, not just the pulmonary one — neither pathway in the smoking-vs-lung-biology framework is actually nailed down here.

**Inflammation:** Directly contradicted/inapplicable — targeted searches for TMEM18 in airway/alveolar epithelium, protease-antiprotease biology, macrophage/T-cell programs, and cytokine signaling all returned zero hits. There is no inflammatory story for TMEM18 at all; say so explicitly rather than reaching for one.

**Verdict: CONTESTED.** The contradiction is a structural/evidentiary pattern rather than a single rival paper (an explicit rival paper could not be sourced due to search-budget exhaustion this session — flagged for a follow-up pass): TMEM18 lies outside the physical locus, has no locus-specific colocalization, has zero confirmed COPD/lung-function/smoking GWAS hits despite three attempted citations, has no spirometric trait association in Open Targets, and its only verified relevant citation (Çolak) is being stretched beyond what it shows. The gene's biological fame (adipocyte differentiation, CNS/obesity GWAS) is real, but real fame in an unrelated phenotype is not evidence for this locus's COPD signal."

**Disclosure from red-team-skeptic:** its web-search budget was exhausted before it could run independent contradiction searches (e.g., for a rival causal-gene paper at this locus, or a direct check of GSCAN/Shrine/Sakornsakolpat summary statistics). Its CONTESTED verdict is based on close reading of the researcher's findings, verifier verdicts, and Open Targets data already provided, not on new independent search evidence. Flagged as a residual gap for any future re-review of this locus.

LOC124905962 — WEAKENED

Red-team-skeptic's reasoning (verbatim): "Not a resolvable Ensembl gene, zero literature, likely an artifact/synonym for one of the same unnamed lncRNA transcripts already covered under LINC01865 or a flanking transcript. This isn't a gene that can be evaluated on the merits at all — recommend synthesis-lead treat it as a pipeline/annotation artifact rather than a genuine third candidate, and not weight its 'nearest gene' label as if it carried independent biological meaning. **Verdict: WEAKENED** — not because a real claim overreaches, but because the 'candidate' itself is not a validated biological entity; any inference drawn from its name should be discounted to near zero."

LINC01865 — not contested (included for completeness)

Red-team-skeptic explicitly found this gene's treatment fair and non-overclaiming: "The researcher's own language ('plausible... however speculative') is appropriately hedged and does not overclaim; there's no inflated inference here to knock down... **Verdict: HOLDS** — as a modest, explicitly-hedged claim ('structurally best-placed candidate, biologically uncharacterized') it is fair.
