Researcher's original claim: "genetically proxied APOB inhibition (mimicking mipomersen) was associated with increased asthma risk... while LDLR was the significant COPD drug-target hit, not APOB." **Why excluded:** citation-verifier found the paper's abstract actually reports APOB was PROTECTIVE for asthma (OR 0.86), the opposite direction from the claim as stated.   1. **Delporte C, Van Antwerpen P, Vanhamme L, et al.** "Low-density lipoprotein modified by myeloperoxidase in inflammatory pathways and clinical studies." Mediators Inflamm. 2013;2013:971579. PMID 23983406, DOI 10.1155/2013/971579. Researcher's original claim: review "discussed in relation to comorbid conditions including COPD." **Why excluded:** citation-verifier confirmed the mechanistic content (myeloperoxidase- oxidized LDL/ApoB-100, macrophage foam-cell formation, TNF-alpha/IL-8 induction) but found no evidence the review specifically discusses COPD or respiratory disease comorbidity as claimed. Excluded because the COPD-relevance link in the claim is unsupported, even though the underlying mechanistic biology is real.
