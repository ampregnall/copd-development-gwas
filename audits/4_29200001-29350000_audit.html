Audit Report - Locus 4:29200001-29350000 (gene desert)

This appendix documents excluded/downgraded citations and contested genes for this locus, preserved from the citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic outputs. See `evidence_tables/4_29200001-29350000.md` for the assembled evidence table (verified citations only) and Open Targets/prioritization synthesis.

Excluded citations

**None.** All 6 citations returned by the researcher for this locus were verified as VERIFIED by the citation-verifier (no RETRACTED, NOT_FOUND, or CLAIM_MISMATCH verdicts). Two citations (Sakornsakolpat 2019 re: PCDH7/DLCO, and Al-Soufi & Costas 2023 re: PCDH7/schizophrenia-smoking) were flagged by the researcher as tentative/unconfirmed pending full-text check of whether PCDH7 was genuinely named — the citation-verifier confirmed both were genuine, named associations upon full-text/results-table check (in fact somewhat stronger than the researcher's cautious framing: Sakornsakolpat 2019 explicitly lists PCDH7/rs7664851 as a suggestive DLCO locus and reports a nominal COPD case-control signal near PCDH7; Al-Soufi & Costas 2023 explicitly names PCDH7 as a colocalization locus between schizophrenia and smoking-related trait genetic signals).

Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic

LINC02472 — verdict: HOLDS as an honest report, but WEAKENED if presented as a confident gene-level call

Verbatim skeptic reasoning:

The researcher's own characterization — "cannot currently be prioritized on any independent mechanistic grounds; genuine absence-of-evidence" — is accurate and not an overclaim; as written it **HOLDS** as an honest report. But if synthesis-lead's final output assigns LINC02472 as "the prioritized gene at this locus" (rather than "no gene can currently be prioritized; LINC02472 is the only transcript overlapping a high-confidence but functionally uncharacterized signal"), that step **WEAKENED** — assigning a specific gene name by default, when every independent line of evidence is null, manufactures a false impression of resolution. I'd recommend this locus be reported explicitly as unresolved/uncharacterizable rather than "LINC02472."

Additional skeptic points preserved: - "Containment in a lncRNA intron is weak causal evidence, weaker than the equivalent claim for a protein-coding gene... an intron_variant/non_coding_transcript_variant MODIFIER annotation carries essentially no functional prior beyond 'this DNA is not exonic.'" - "The PIP=0.999 is nonetheless a real, strong statistical signal... This is a case where statistical resolution has outpaced biological annotation, not a case where the biology has been checked and found wanting." - "The GTEx testis-only/lung-null result should be weighted as weak-to-moderate, not dispositive, against a lung role... A 0.0 TPM bulk-tissue null for a lncRNA is much less informative than the same null would be for a protein-coding gene." - "Alternative mechanism not addressed: because this is a gene desert, the credible-set variant could equally be a distal regulatory element for a gene well outside the 150kb window (gene-desert enhancers acting over long range on distant genes are a recognized phenomenon, e.g., the FTO/IRX3 precedent)... 'the causal gene is LINC02472' and 'the causal gene is some distant unannotated target' are currently indistinguishable."

**How this is reflected in the evidence table:** the Prioritized-gene synthesis section names LINC02472 but at confidence INSUFFICIENT, explicitly per the skeptic's recommendation, rather than presenting it as a resolved call.

PCDH7 (context gene, ~1.43 Mb from credible-set variant) — verdict: WEAKENED

Verbatim skeptic reasoning:

**Verdict on PCDH7:** WEAKENED as a candidate for this specific locus — the underlying citations are sound and the researcher did not overclaim (explicitly called it "background context only," not a competing prioritized candidate), but I want to be more explicit than the researcher was: without an LD/coloc check, there is no basis to assume rs7664851 and rs76849161 represent the same causal signal, and the smoking/behavioral pleiotropy at PCDH7 should not be transferred to this locus's smoking-mediation assessment.

Additional skeptic points preserved: - "The DLCO signal (Sakornsakolpat 2019, rs7664851, P=3.03E-06) is real but suggestive, not genome-wide significant, and — critically — no LD or colocalization analysis was performed between rs7664851 and rs76849161. At 1.43 Mb, these are very unlikely to be the same haplotype/signal under typical LD decay, but this pipeline did not check." - "Using PCDH7's smoking-genetics profile to argue this broader locus is smoking-mediated would conflate two statistically distinct signals under one cytoband/'neighborhood' umbrella."

Methodological caveat on this locus's critique stage

The red-team-skeptic's WebSearch tool budget (200/200) was already exhausted before it could run any independent searches for this locus — it disclosed **zero independent searches completed**. Its critique is therefore based entirely on internal-consistency review of the researcher's and citation-verifier's reported findings, not on independent adversarial literature search. This is a materially different (weaker) evidentiary basis than critiques for loci where the skeptic had search budget available, and should be weighed accordingly by the study team — this locus's critique stage should be considered for re-running with a fresh search budget if higher confidence is needed.

Open Targets absence-of-evidence note

Not an exclusion, but worth flagging in the audit trail: LINC02472 returned `count = 0` for every Open Targets query attempted (COPD-specific genetic evidence, all-disease associations, colocalization) and an empty list for genetic constraint and tractability. All queries executed successfully (no tool/API errors) — this is a genuine, confirmed absence of any prior annotation for this gene in Open Targets, not a data-retrieval failure.
