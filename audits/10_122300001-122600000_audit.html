Structural issues flagged by red-team-skeptic (locus-wide, not gene-specific)

**(a) Locus-membership uncertainty.** Verbatim: "The researcher could not confirm that this region is actually among Sakornsakolpat 2019's 82 reported COPD loci or Shrine 2019's 279 lung-function signals — the only evidence is the negative inference from Open Targets' own missing credible set. If this signal isn't genuinely genome-wide-significant in either source GWAS, the entire 'which gene is causal' exercise may be moot. Flag for synthesis-lead: confirm locus membership in the source GWAS tables before treating this as an established COPD hit."

**(b) LD-block gene assignment is unresolved even for the disease this locus is famous for.** The skeptic searched independently for COPD-specific ARMS2/HTRA1/PLEKHA1 causal-gene literature, COPD-AMD shared genetic architecture, and smoking-GWAS membership, finding: no paper anywhere argues for or against a specific gene at this locus for COPD specifically (the causal-gene debate that exists is entirely about AMD); a COPD→AMD comorbidity study (Bair et al. 2021, PMID 34302051, VERIFIED) that is epidemiological, not genetic-pleiotropy, evidence and should not be used to argue for shared germline causality at this locus; and a book chapter (Kortvely & Ueffing 2016, PMID 26427389, VERIFIED) making explicit that AMD risk haplotypes at 10q26 affect ARMS2 expression specifically, not HTRA1 — a point the original researcher's write-up did not surface despite already citing the underlying primary papers (Fritsche 2008, Kanda 2007) for ARMS2. On smoking-mediation: neither the GSCAN paper nor further searches could confirm or deny whether this locus is a smoking-behavior locus — an unresolved evidence gap, not a resolved negative finding.

Bottom line for reviewers

No citation was rejected (RETRACTED/NOT_FOUND/CLAIM_MISMATCH) at this locus — the entire evidentiary complication here is that the *strongest* verified literature (the AMD mechanistic and causal-gene literature) is for a different disease at the same genomic locus, and it does not unambiguously support HTRA1 over its neighbors ARMS2 and PLEKHA1. Treat the "Prioritized gene" call in the main evidence table as provisional pending an LD lookup between this locus's lead variant and the classic AMD variants, and pending confirmation of whether this locus is genuinely genome-wide-significant in the published COPD/lung-function GWAS.
