Audit Report - Locus 6:108850001-109000000 (nearest gene ARMC2)

This is the excluded-citation and contested-gene appendix for `evidence_tables/6_108850001-109000000.md`. It exists so the evidence table can be distributed without also handing out this audit trail.

Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH / NOT_FOUND)

  1. **Sakornsakolpat P, Prokopenko D, Lamontagne M, et al. "Genetic landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype associations." Nature Genetics, 2019.** PMID 30804561 / PMC6546635 / DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2. **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** Abstract confirms 82 COPD GWAS loci were identified (35,735 cases/222,076 controls, UK Biobank + ICGC) but does NOT explicitly name ARMC2 or rs2798641. The researcher had flagged this citation as only a plausible, unconfirmed source of the ARMC2-tagged lung-function associations; citation-verifier could not confirm the gene/variant is explicitly named at this locus from the abstract text (supplementary tables were not checked). Excluded from the evidence table because the claim as originally framed (this paper supports ARMC2 at this locus) is not confirmed.
  1. **Shrine N, Guyatt AL, Erzurumluoglu AM, et al. "New genetic signals for lung function highlight pathways and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease associations across multiple ancestries." Nature Genetics, 2019.** PMID 30804560 / PMC6397078 / DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7. **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** Abstract confirms 279 lung-function signals (400,102 individuals, European ancestry) but does NOT explicitly name ARMC2 or rs2798641. Note: article has 2 published errata but is not retracted. Same exclusion rationale as above — this is very likely (per the researcher's own hedged framing) one of the source studies behind the GWAS Catalog's ARMC2 gene-mapping for rs2798641, but that specific attribution is unconfirmed and should not be presented as verified literature support.
  1. **Markelov VA, Korytina GF, Aznabaeva YG, et al. "[Expression of Long Noncoding RNAs and Protein-Coding Genes Involved in Oxidative Stress and Cell Senescence in Patients with COPD]." Molekuliarnaia Biologiia (Mosk), 2024.** PMID 39970122 / DOI 10.31857/S0026898424050119. **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** The researcher's claim was that "FOXO3 mRNA levels positively correlated with lung-function parameters" specifically; citation-verifier found the abstract lists FOXO3 among a multi-gene panel found to correlate with lung-function parameters but does not individually specify FOXO3's own direction/ magnitude of correlation. Excluded pending full-text confirmation of the FOXO3-specific finding.
  1. **Giordani E, Houdeline S, Court M, et al. "ARMC2 loss impairs cilia structure and leads to primary ciliary dyskinesia symptoms in mouse organs." Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology, 2026.** PMID 42272638 / PMC13246696 / DOI 10.3389/fcell.2026.1695239. **Verdict: NOT_FOUND.** Citation-verifier could not resolve this citation in PubMed; assessed as likely fabricated/hallucinated (the 2026 publication date on a paper the researcher itself flagged as needing confirmation is a red flag). This was the single most consequential exclusion in this locus's review: it was the researcher's only cited evidence for a *direct* in vivo airway/tracheal ciliary phenotype in ARMC2-deficient mice (impaired ciliary beating, tracheal mucus accumulation, PCD-like features). Its removal downgrades the ARMC2 mechanistic case from "confirmed direct airway mechanism" to "plausible mechanism by analogy from sperm-flagellum and algal-flagellum biology, unconfirmed in mammalian airway" — see red-team-skeptic critique below and the Prioritized Gene section of the evidence table for the full discussion. **This citation must not be used or cited anywhere downstream of this pipeline.**

Additional PMIDs noted by researcher but never submitted for verification

The researcher flagged six additional PMIDs (40158138, 39417902, 38492154, 35543806, 34493464, 31621862) as redundant MMAF/ARMC2-infertility genetics papers, not individually pulled for full metadata since they reiterate the same core Coutton et al. 2019 finding (biallelic ARMC2 loss-of-function causing MMAF/asthenozoospermia). These were not sent to citation-verifier and are not used anywhere in the evidence table; listed here only for completeness/spot-check if a future reviewer wants to check them.

Contested / weakened gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic)

Red-team-skeptic did not find any citation-level or literature-level evidence actively contradicting either serious candidate gene at this locus, and explicitly declined to mark either gene CONTESTED in the strict sense ("credible contradicting evidence found that argues against this gene or for a different gene"). Both serious candidates were instead marked **WEAKENED**, for distinct reasons — reasoning preserved verbatim below (full critique also in `evidence_tables/_status/6_108850001-109000000/critique.md`):

ARMC2 — WEAKENED

"The statistical genetics (L2G, GWAS-GWAS colocalization, intronic position of all three credible-set variants) remains genuinely strong and this is very likely the correct gene at this locus by position/statistics. But the "mechanistic" case for a lung/airway-ciliary mechanism specifically has collapsed to inference-by-analogy (sperm flagellum, algal flagellum) now that the one direct-evidence citation (Giordani, PMID 42272638) is confirmed fabricated/NOT_FOUND, and the "reduced lung function" phrase in the surviving Lechtreck citation is likely circular back-citation to the same GWAS papers already separately flagged as CLAIM_MISMATCH rather than independent corroboration. Synthesis-lead should present ARMC2 as "strong positional/statistical candidate, mechanism in lung tissue not established," not as a gene with confirmed structural biology in the airway."

Additional points raised: (1) no eQTL/pQTL/sQTL colocalization exists for ARMC2 at this locus (explicitly checked, absent) — so there is no expression-based functional-genomics confirmation that ARMC2 expression specifically mediates the signal; (2) ARMC2's known disease biology (MMAF) is a *recessive* loss-of-function phenotype restricted to the sperm flagellum, a different zygosity and tissue context from what a common noncoding GWAS variant of modest effect on lung function would need to be doing (most plausibly a partial regulatory/expression effect in airway epithelium specifically — an alternative hypothesis not directly tested by any evidence gathered here); (3) an independent PubMed check by the skeptic for "ARMC2 respiratory/airway phenotype in MMAF patients" returned zero hits, consistent with no published clinical report of respiratory/PCD-like symptoms in ARMC2-mutant MMAF patients despite this being a well-studied recessive disease since 2019 — noted as mild contradicting context, not proof of no effect.

FOXO3 — WEAKENED

"All the FOXO3 mechanistic literature (senescence/autophagy/SIRT-AMPK-FOXO3 axis, immune hub-gene analyses) is real, verified, and biologically coherent for a general "aging/senescence/oxidative-stress" contribution to COPD pathophysiology broadly, but none of it is tied to this specific locus/credible set, is smoke-exposure-response biology (arguably downstream consequence of disease/exposure rather than a germline causal mechanism), and is contradicted by the absence of any L2G/coloc/fine-mapping signal for FOXO3 at this locus. This is not evidence that FOXO3 is wrong as a general COPD-pathway gene — it's evidence that FOXO3 is not well supported as the causal gene *at this GWAS locus specifically*."

The skeptic also pushed back mildly on dismissing PoPS too quickly: "PoPS... is not nothing, and dismissing it because 'L2G doesn't support it' is somewhat circular, since PoPS and L2G are different, partially non-redundant methods... it should be reported as 'one method flagged this gene; the other major statistical method and all mechanistic/variant-specific literature checks failed to corroborate it,' not silently dropped." The skeptic separately flagged that the Becher 2018 (Treg/FOXO3-FOXP3) and Parvin 2025 (mustard-gas PBMC hub genes) immune citations, while verified as existing and accurately described, are both non-COPD-specific (autoimmune/tumor immunology, and a chemical-injury lung-disease proxy respectively) and should never be presented without that caveat attached.

SESN1 — not contested

No independent search was performed by the skeptic (concurring with the researcher's own treatment of SESN1 as a non-serious candidate given its very low Open Targets L2G score ~0.06 and the complete absence of COPD/lung-specific literature).

Coverage-gap disclosure (from red-team-skeptic)

The skeptic disclosed that its own WebSearch quota was exhausted session-wide before it could run a full contradiction-search pass on both ARMC2 and FOXO3; it substituted a small number of direct PubMed WebFetch queries as a fallback (all returned zero hits, which is itself informative but is "a shallow, single-pass check on each gene, not an exhaustive contradiction search"). This is flagged here as a known coverage gap for any future, more thorough re-review of this locus.
