Gene-level skeptic verdict

**AFAP1: WEAKENED** (red-team-skeptic verdict, not CONTESTED). Full reasoning is reproduced in `evidence_tables/_status/4_7750001-8050000/critique.md`; summary: the statistical/ fine-mapping case for AFAP1 as the physically-implicated gene at this locus is comparatively clean (single gene body, all 39 credible-set variants intronic to it, three concordant prioritization methods, spirometric-trait concordance, no smoking-pathway confound, one confirmed named replication in Lin WD 2024). But the inference that AFAP1 is a mechanistically confirmed pulmonary-disease gene overreaches the evidence: most of the statistical-genetics citation list does not name AFAP1 at all (5 of 10 were CLAIM_MISMATCH), there is zero pulmonary mechanistic/expression/functional evidence anywhere in the structural or immune literature, and the gene's only well-validated human functional biology is ocular (primary open-angle glaucoma/intraocular pressure), with a lead-variant-level pleiotropy signal (rs73073939 itself resolving to a glaucoma credible set in Open Targets, L2G=0.78) that remains unresolved for lack of a colocalization analysis. No paper argues AFAP1 is the wrong gene or that a different gene at this locus is causal for COPD (hence not CONTESTED), but that check could not be independently run this pass due to the search-budget exhaustion noted above.
