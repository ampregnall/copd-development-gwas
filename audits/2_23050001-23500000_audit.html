CONTESTED gene: KLHL29

**Red-team-skeptic verdict: CONTESTED.** Full verbatim critique preserved in `evidence_tables/_status/2_23050001-23500000/critique.md`; key reasoning summarized here:

  1. The credible set at this locus (41 variants, all MODIFIER-impact intronic/intergenic/regulatory-flanking) carries **zero gene-level annotation** — the internal pipeline's own fine-mapping does not anchor to KLHL29 at the variant level. KLHL29 enters this review purely via nearest-gene heuristic and FLAMES prioritization; no MAGMA, PoPS, or ABC-model support was supplied, and no colocalization (eQTL/pQTL) evidence was retrieved from Open Targets.
  1. Despite Open Targets reporting a strong, repeated FEV1/pulmonary-function-measurement L2G signal for KLHL29 (up to 0.816, across roughly a dozen-plus independent studies), **neither of the two largest, most systematic human lung-function/COPD variant-to-gene mapping papers in the field** — Shrine et al. 2019 (107 named putative causal genes) or Shrine et al. 2023 (559 genes via systematic V2G mapping) — could be confirmed, by the researcher OR by an independently re-checking citation-verifier, to name KLHL29 among their gene lists. This is an unresolved discrepancy between a strong structured-database signal and an absence of corroborating primary-literature gene attribution (noting: absence of confirmation is not the same as a confirmed negative, since full supplementary tables were not accessible to either agent).
  1. KLHL29's #1- and #2-ranked disease/trait associations in Open Targets are behavioral/addiction traits — "smoking initiation" (L2G-like score 0.930/0.927) and "alcohol drinking" (0.68) — both higher than any single FEV1/pulmonary-function evidence row. The flagship GSCAN tobacco/alcohol GWAS (Liu et al. 2019, PMID 30643251) names PDE4B and CUL3 (KLHL29's own CUL3-ligase binding partner, per Yao et al. 2023) in its main text as loci for all five substance-use phenotypes, but does NOT name KLHL29 itself — raising the possibility that any true signal near this locus for smoking-related traits could reflect LD with a CUL3-pathway regulatory element rather than KLHL29 specifically. The skeptic judges this smoking-vs-lung-function tension as the central unresolved issue at this locus: current evidence cannot determine whether KLHL29 (or a neighboring/LD-partner gene) is acting through pulmonary biology, smoking-behavior neurobiology, or both, or whether the COPD signal at this locus (in the internal pipeline's own GWAS, which is NOT the same COPD study indexed in Open Targets) is confounded entirely by uncontrolled smoking behavior.
  1. The only mechanistic/functional paper on KLHL29 (Yao et al. 2023, PMID 37845393, VERIFIED) establishes it as a genuine CUL3 E3-ubiquitin-ligase substrate adaptor that degrades DDX3X and acts as a tumor suppressor — but entirely in triple-negative breast cancer cell biology, with zero lung, airway, or pulmonary-development data. The skeptic explicitly flags this as at risk of being overclaimed as "lung-relevant mechanism" in synthesis if the tissue caveat is dropped; it has been preserved here.
  1. KLHL29 recurs as a candidate/QTL gene across unrelated non-human GWAS (porcine body size, flatfish sexual-size-dimorphism, dairy-cattle milking speed, catfish heat stress) with no consistent phenotypic theme and no immune/lung-specific pattern. The skeptic interprets this as evidence AGAINST gene-specific causal biology — a signature of a gene simply being positionally swept up near many independent GWAS peaks across species (local LD/gene-density architecture), rather than evidence of a shared causal role.
  1. **Disclosed search-coverage gap:** the red-team-skeptic's own independent contradiction/neighboring-gene search queries were blocked by exhausted web-search budget in that subagent's session (0 of 2 attempted queries executed). This means no independent check was completed for (a) whether KLHL29 is named in the Shrine 2019/2023 or GSCAN supplementary gene-level tables, or (b) what other genes physically sit in the 2:23,050,001-23,500,000 interval and whether any has stronger lung/smoking literature support. This is an acknowledged, unresolved limitation of this locus's review, not a clean pass — flagged here for the study team's awareness and potential follow-up with a fresh search budget.

**Net effect on synthesis:** KLHL29 remains the prioritized-gene recommendation only because it is the sole candidate gene supplied for this locus by the internal pipeline — there is no competing named candidate to prefer instead. But per the skeptic's CONTESTED verdict, this should be read as "best/only available candidate," not as a literature-confirmed causal-gene call. Confidence in the final evidence table is capped at LOW/INSUFFICIENT accordingly.
