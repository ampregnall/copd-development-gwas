Excluded citations (verdict CLAIM_MISMATCH)

All three citations below were reported by the researcher as the likely primary-literature source of ZNF652's Open Targets FEV1 genetic-association signal. The citation-verifier confirmed each paper exists and matches the stated cohort/signal-count description, but could NOT confirm that ZNF652 is actually named as a hit/gene in any of them from the abstract or accessible full text (supplementary per-locus gene tables were not accessible to the verifier). They are excluded from the evidence table on that basis.

  1. **Shrine N, Guyatt AL, Erzurumluoglu AM, et al.** "New genetic signals for lung function highlight pathways and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease associations across multiple ancestries." Nat Genet. 2019;51(3):481-493. PMID 30804560, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7. Verifier reason: paper confirmed to exist and matches the stated cohort/signal-count description (UK Biobank + SpiroMeta, 400,102 individuals, 279 signals), but ZNF652 is not named in the abstract or accessible full text/methods.
  1. **Wain LV, Shrine N, Artigas MS, et al.** "Genome-wide association analyses for lung function and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease identify new loci and potential druggable targets." Nat Genet. 2017;49(3):416-425. PMID 28166213, DOI 10.1038/ng.3787. Verifier reason: paper confirmed to exist and matches the stated cohort/signal-count description (UK Biobank extreme-phenotype GWAS, 97 signals), but ZNF652/chr17:49.3Mb is not named in the abstract.
  1. **Shrine N, Izquierdo AG, Chen J, et al.** "Multi-ancestry genome-wide association analyses improve resolution of genes and pathways influencing lung function and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease risk." Nat Genet. 2023;55(3):410-422. PMID 36914875, DOI 10.1038/s41588-023-01314-0. Verifier reason: paper confirmed to exist and matches the stated cohort/signal-count description (580,869 participants, 1,020 signals, 559 genes), but ZNF652 is not named among genes in the abstract; supplementary gene list not accessible to confirm inclusion.
