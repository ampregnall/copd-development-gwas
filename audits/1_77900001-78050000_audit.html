Audit Report - Locus 1:77900001-78050000 (nearest gene DNAJB4)

This file preserves everything excluded from, or flagged as contested within, the main evidence table at `evidence_tables/1_77900001-78050000.md`, plus process notes for traceability. Nothing in this pipeline run produced a RETRACTED, NOT_FOUND, or CLAIM_MISMATCH citation verdict — all 18 citations returned by the researcher were verdict VERIFIED by citation-verifier. This audit is therefore dominated by CONTESTED/WEAKENED gene verdicts from red-team-skeptic and process/data-quality flags, reproduced verbatim below.

Citation verification: no exclusions

Gene-level skeptic verdicts (verbatim reasoning from red-team-skeptic)

DNAJB4 — CONTESTED

Verdict: CONTESTED — direct, verified eQTL evidence (Dicanio 2022, PMID 36082445; Shi 2023, PMID 37932299) at the exact credible-set variant points to FUBP1, not DNAJB4, as the regulatory target; DNAJB4's only disease mechanism is a phenotypically discordant (restrictive, not obstructive) Mendelian myopathy."

ZZZ3 — CONTESTED

Verdict: CONTESTED — the coordinate/locus-overlap discrepancy is a potentially disqualifying problem that the researcher flagged but did not resolve, and should be resolved (build check, confirm actual PoPS window definition) before ZZZ3 is used in synthesis at all. Even setting that aside, there is no COPD- or lung-function-specific mechanistic literature; this looks like a PoPS statistical prioritization without biological corroboration, and possibly one anchored to the wrong locus entirely."

**Orchestrator follow-up (independent confirmation, not part of red-team-skeptic's own search budget):** the synthesis-lead independently queried Open Targets' `genomicLocation` field for ZZZ3 (ENSG00000036549) directly and obtained chr1:77,562,416-77,683,422 (GRCh38, strand -1) — matching the researcher's Ensembl-derived coordinates. Since both Ensembl and Open Targets report the same GRCh38 coordinates, a simple genome-build mismatch (e.g. GRCh37 vs. GRCh38) is ruled out as the explanation; the discrepancy more likely reflects the internal PoPS window for this locus being wider than the printed credible-set/locus interval (1:77,900,001-78,050,000). This does not resolve whether ZZZ3 belongs at this locus — it only narrows down the likely cause of the mismatch for whoever investigates the internal pipeline further.

FUBP1 — WEAKENED

Verdict: WEAKENED — the eQTL convergence at the exact credible-set variant is real and is legitimately stronger locus-assignment evidence than nearest-gene/FLAMES heuristics for DNAJB4, but 'strongest mechanistic candidate for COPD' overstates evidence that is (a) borrowed from non-COPD GWAS contexts, (b) not colocalized against the COPD signal itself, and (c) bridged to COPD via a plausible but unconfirmed antiviral-epithelial mechanism rather than any direct COPD/spirometry association."

NEXN and GIPC2 — HOLDS

Verdict: HOLDS (for both) — appropriately deprioritized, nothing to contest, though note per the search-budget caveat above I could not independently confirm the absence of contradicting literature."

Process / data-quality flags

  1. **ZZZ3 coordinate discrepancy (unresolved).** See above — ZZZ3 does not physically overlap the stated locus interval per two independent GRCh38 sources (Ensembl, Open Targets). Root cause not determined; flagged for the internal pipeline team to investigate (possible PoPS window definition wider than the printed credible-set interval).
  2. **Red-team-skeptic search-budget exhaustion.** The red-team-skeptic subagent's web-search budget was exhausted before any of its planned independent contradiction-search queries (DNAJB4-COPD, FUBP1-COPD/spirometry, ZZZ3 coordinates, rs34517439-smoking behavior) could execute. Its critique is based entirely on the researcher's already-verified dossier, not independent confirmation. "No contradicting evidence found" statements in both the researcher's and skeptic's output should be read as "not found in searches performed," not as an exhaustively cleared literature search.
  3. **Smoking-mediation prior not checked against primary GWAS summary statistics.** Neither the researcher nor the red-team-skeptic was able to directly query smoking-behavior GWAS (e.g. GSCAN) summary statistics at rs34517439; both relied on PubMed literature search, which returned no hits. This is flagged as the single highest-value follow-up check per the skeptic, given the locus's high fine-mapping PIP and its cross-trait pleiotropy with COVID-19/IPF/telomere length (traits with plausible smoking-related confounding). Treat "no smoking-GWAS overlap found" as an open item, not a cleared check.
  4. **Open Targets coverage gap.** FUBP1, NEXN, and GIPC2 were not part of the original Open Targets structured lookup (step 1 of the pipeline), because they were only identified as candidates later, during the researcher's literature-driven genomic-region scan. Their L2G/colocalization/constraint metrics were not pulled from Open Targets in this run — a gap for a future iteration to fill, not a finding that these genes lack such data.
  5. **Cross-trait pleiotropy interpretation.** rs34517439 is independently reported (across the citations above) as a shared/pleiotropic variant for multiple myeloma risk, COVID-19 severity (two independent studies), idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, and leukocyte telomere length. Per the skeptic, this pattern is at least as consistent with a broad host-defense/ interferon-response or cellular-senescence locus as with a COPD-specific obstructive-airway-disease locus. This is presented as an alternative hypothesis for the study team's consideration, not a resolved conclusion — no cited study connects this locus to COPD case-control status, spirometry, or exacerbation phenotypes directly.
