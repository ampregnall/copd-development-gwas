Methodological caveats carried forward from researcher + skeptic

- **GRCh37/GRCh38 build mismatch (structural, not literature):** GTF2I and GTF2IRD1's GRCh38 gene coordinates fall several hundred kb outside this locus window, while their GRCh37 coordinates fall inside it. This strongly suggests the internal pipeline's MAGMA/PoPS gene-to-locus assignment used mismatched genome builds. **Update 2026-09-11: confirmed** — the study team found and fixed this bug in the MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file, and the corrected re-merge surfaced RFC2 as a new candidate at this locus (see below). This retroactively validates the original review's hypothesis about GTF2I/GTF2IRD1's likely erroneous inclusion. - **Red-team-skeptic search-budget note:** the skeptic's WebSearch calls returned "budget exhausted" errors and it was unable to perform any independent contradiction-search of its own for any of the four genes; its critique is based entirely on scrutinizing the researcher's findings and the citation-verifier's verdicts, not on independent literature discovery. This is disclosed here rather than silently treating the critique as if an independent search had been performed. (The same search-budget limitation recurred in the RFC2 critique pass below.) - **Smoking-mediation prior:** no evidence (positive or negative) was found connecting this locus to smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS signals; this should be described as untested, not as ruling out a smoking-mediated mechanism. - **Spirometric-concordance prior:** the two candidate sources that could have established FEV1/FEV1-FVC concordance for ELN (PMID 30804561, PMID 30804560) were both CLAIM_MISMATCH/unconfirmed from abstracts alone; full-text locus-table verification (checking whether this chr7:74.0-74.1 Mb region appears in either paper's reported loci tables) was not performed in this pass and is recommended as the highest-value follow-up to strengthen or weaken the ELN case.

TARGETED ADDITION (2026-09-11): RFC2 evaluation

**Background:** the genome-build bug hypothesized above (GRCh37 gene models used against a GRCh38-defined locus/credible-set window) was confirmed and fixed by the study team in the MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file. The corrected re-merge surfaced RFC2 as a candidate gene at this locus that was never evaluated in the original review. Unlike GTF2I/GTF2IRD1, RFC2's candidacy is NOT itself a build-mismatch artifact — orchestration independently confirmed via Ensembl REST that RFC2 (chr7:74,223,231-74,254,458, GRCh38) genuinely overlaps the locus window (73,950,001-74,250,000) in correctly matched GRCh38 coordinates (~26.8 kb of the ~31.2 kb gene body falls inside the window). RFC2 was therefore researched in full rather than ruled out on procedural grounds alone.

RFC2 citations — all VERIFIED as real papers, but none support any COPD/lung/immune claim

- **PMID 29247873** — VERIFIED as existing (Aghdam et al., "The Ability of Different Imputation Methods to Preserve the Significant Genes and Pathways in Cancer," Genomics Proteomics Bioinformatics 2017), but confirmed to be a false-positive hit: this is a statistical-imputation-methods paper for cancer microarray data that surfaced in search only via the generic MeSH term "Replication Protein C," and makes no substantive claim about RFC2's biology or lung relevance. Excluded from the evidence table as non-informative (not a CLAIM_MISMATCH in the technical sense — the researcher never claimed it supported a COPD link — but excluded for the same reason as verified-but-irrelevant citations elsewhere in this file). - **PMID 42244520, 38669181, 34751190, 16980295** — all VERIFIED as real, accurately described papers on RFC2's biochemical role in the RFC/CTF18-RFC clamp-loader complex (PCNA loading during DNA replication and repair). None of the four have any lung, airway, immune, or COPD relevance whatsoever — they are foundational/general molecular biology and cancer-therapeutics-mechanism papers only. Excluded from the evidence table for the same reason as GTF2IRD1's verified-but-irrelevant general-biology papers above: real and accurate, but not evidence for or against this locus's COPD association.

RFC2 — WEAKENED, bordering on non-candidate (red-team-skeptic verdict)

Red-team-skeptic also confirmed the classical "elastin causes emphysema" mechanistic prior was correctly kept out of the RFC2 assessment in both directions: "Nothing in the RFC2 evidence table invokes elastic-fiber or structural-protein biology; the researcher evaluated RFC2 purely on its own DNA-replication/clamp-loader biology and explicitly declined to force a mechanistic narrative it doesn't have."
