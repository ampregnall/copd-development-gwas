Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

KLF12 — CONTESTED

Reasoning preserved verbatim from the red-team-skeptic's critique (`evidence_tables/_status/13_74250001-74450000/critique.md`):

**KLF12: CONTESTED.** Not because any paper directly refutes "KLF12 causes COPD" (no such paper exists to refute, since none claims it directly either), but because there is genuine on-point contradicting-pattern evidence: (a) Hoskins et al. 2016's demonstration that nearest-gene reasoning fails in this exact chr13q22.1 KLF-family desert, with the true target reached by long-range chromatin interaction to a completely different, non-nearest gene; (b) a COPD-specific gene-prioritization paper (Zainab/Anzawa 2024) that scanned this region and did not surface KLF12; (c) a major pulmonary-function GWAS (Wyss 2018) that likewise doesn't name it; (d) the only quantitative "association" (Open Targets FEV1/FVC 0.356) is circular, derived from the same credible set under review, not independent replication. Taken together this is a real, converging pattern arguing that "nearest gene/PoPS" is being asked to carry more inferential weight than the field's own precedent in this locus supports.

Supporting detail from the same critique on point 1 (nearest-gene reasoning):

The Hoskins et al. 2016 (PMID 28172817) precedent is more than background color — it is a directly analogous, on-point piece of evidence that undercuts the core inferential move being made here. Same genomic neighborhood (chr13q22.1, the KLF5/KLF12/PIBF1/BORA "desert"), same nearest-gene candidates including KLF12 itself, and the field's own functional-validation work in that region found the causal gene was DIS3, reached via a 570kb chromatin loop — not any of the nearest genes. This is not merely "no evidence for KLF12," it is affirmative regional precedent that nearest-gene reasoning fails in this specific desert.

And on point 2 (circularity of the Open Targets FEV1/FVC signal):

The 0.356 score is generated 100% by the `gwas_credible_sets` datasource, which is Open Targets' own locus-to-gene (L2G) assignment of this same credible set to KLF12 (presumably via distance/regulatory-feature weighting in their L2G model). Citing this score as "evidence KLF12 is associated with FEV1/FVC" is close to restating the premise ... as if it were an independent line of evidence. It is not independent; it's a database-internal reflection of the same nearest-gene assumption under scrutiny.

Genes marked WEAKENED (not CONTESTED, but flagged) by red-team-skeptic

LINC00402 — WEAKENED

Reasoning preserved verbatim:

**LINC00402: WEAKENED, not CONTESTED.** No literature argues against LINC00402 specifically — the issue is absence of disease-specific evidence, not contradicting evidence. But "WEAKENED" here should be read charitably: the researcher's summary ("unsupported beyond physical overlap") somewhat undersells that the physical overlap plus a genuine T-cell-intrinsic IL-2/TCR mechanism is a nontrivial starting hypothesis for an immune-mediated disease locus, even without lung-specific data. I would not let synthesis quietly drop LINC00402 in favor of KLF12 on current evidence; both are speculative, and if anything LINC00402 has the more mechanistically coherent (if tissue-mismatched) immune story plus the positional argument in its favor.

On the immune-evidence directionality point (point 3 of the critique), also preserved verbatim because it materially affects how the KLF12 immune citations should be read:

That bridge should be resisted, not just because the tissue/disease context differs (NSCLC tumor microenvironment vs. airway/parenchyma), but because the *direction* of the proposed mechanism is arguably opposite to what COPD biology would need: in the tumor model, KLF12/PD-L1 activity *suppresses* CD8+ T-cell infiltration/activity (immune evasion, "bad" for the host antitumor response); in COPD, CD8+ T-cell activity is generally considered a pathogenic effector process, not something whose suppression would be disease-relevant in the same direction.

Thoroughness gaps flagged by red-team-skeptic (not a citation issue, but load-bearing for confidence level)

The skeptic could not complete planned searches (GTEx/lung eQTL for this region, GWAS Catalog smoking-trait lookups for rs1331781, joint KLF12/LINC00402 locus papers) due to exhausted search budget.
