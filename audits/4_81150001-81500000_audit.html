Locus-level framing issue

GENE: PRKG2 — WEAKENED

Verdict: WEAKENED. The organ biology is real and directionally plausible but is generic organ physiology, not COPD-specific or even airflow-obstruction-adjacent evidence, and it is being asked to carry the weight of an entire gene nomination in the total absence of any genetic (GWAS/coloc/MR/TWAS) or spirometric evidence.

On the immune-eQTL question: the researcher's framing as an "unexplained gap" is correct and appropriately cautious. These are QTL-only credible sets with explicitly no disease-GWAS colocalization per Open Targets; broad eQTL detection across many immune cell subsets is a common feature of many loci with strong local regulatory variants and is not on its own informative about disease-relevant tissue of action — if anything, the conspicuous absence of a lung-tissue eQTL despite 41 total QTL credible sets is a mild negative signal for a lung-autonomous mechanism. This should remain an open question, not evidence.

GENE: RASGEF1B — WEAKENED (borderline not-a-peer-candidate)

The TLR3/TLR4/NF-kB/macrophage RasGEF1b literature is genuinely reproducible and mechanistically detailed, but it is a generic innate-immune-activation pathway (chemotaxis, IL-6, CXCL1, ICAM-1) demonstrated in general macrophage/LPS/TLR contexts, COVID-19 PBMCs, and one goat bronchial-epithelial-infection model — none in COPD, none in human lung tissue under smoke exposure, and no GWAS/coloc/MR tie to COPD, spirometry, or smoking whatsoever. A generic macrophage TLR-signaling gene is a plausible biological category fit under the immune-complexity prior, but plausibility of category is not the same as locus-specific evidence.

Verdict: WEAKENED, bordering on should-not-be-treated-as-a-candidate-at-all. The case for RASGEF1B rests entirely on (1) weak positional overlap that no statistical fine-mapping/colocalization method endorses, and (2) a real but disease-nonspecific mechanism. Including RASGEF1B alongside PRKG2 and CFAP299 as a peer "candidate gene" risks implying a level of statistical support it does not have.

GENE: C4orf22 / CFAP299 — CONTESTED

The second citation (PMID 30820741) undermines the intuitive "CFAP-family -> cilia/ PCD-like mucociliary dysfunction -> COPD" hypothesis that the gene name invites: the actual functional data show a testis-specific germ-cell apoptosis/cell-cycle role via bcl-2/caspase signaling, not an axonemal/ciliary-motility phenotype. No PCD/situs/airway- cilia phenotype has been reported for this gene despite its family name.

Combined with zero lung or immune-cell eQTLs in Open Targets (testis/brain/fibroblast only) and zero GWAS/coloc/MR evidence beyond the PoPS score itself, CFAP299's case is thin, and the one piece of "prior candidate gene" history at a different locus is actively unfavorable.

Verdict: CONTESTED. Not because a paper argues "CFAP299 is not the COPD gene at 4:81.15-81.5Mb" specifically (no such paper exists), but because (a) the gene has a documented history of being considered and rejected as a causal candidate in a GWAS gene-cluster context at a different locus, and (b) the one plausible lung-relevant mechanistic hypothesis suggested by its gene family name is directly contradicted by the only specific functional data available. This should be flagged as evidence undermining rather than supporting the PoPS prioritization, not merely "no supporting literature found."
