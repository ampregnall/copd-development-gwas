  • Verdict: RETRACTED.** Confirmed independently by citation-verifier: retraction notice PMID 38767306 (Ann Clin Transl Neurol 2024, DOI 10.1002/acn3.52086) states images in Figure 6 and Figure 4B were previously published in unrelated articles (Zhao et al. 2019/2020, Shao et al. 2018); authors did not respond to requests for original data; data declared unreliable.
