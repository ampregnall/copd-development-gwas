CONTESTED gene/claim, with red-team-skeptic's reasoning preserved verbatim

**Gene: SOX6 — CONTESTED component: the "direct pulmonary mechanism" framing of the researcher's synthesis (the SOX6-to-locus gene assignment itself is NOT contested and is well supported).**

Red-team-skeptic's verdict, in full:

The gene-to-locus assignment (SOX6) itself is robust and not in dispute. What is contested is the mechanistic interpretation: the researcher's findings, read together with the structural/immune citations, leave open a "direct pulmonary developmental/immune mechanism cannot be ruled out" framing that overstates evidence which is exploratory (Pniewska, single PBMC study) or developmentally/temporally distant (Cao, embryonic patterning). Meanwhile, converging statistical-genetic evidence (8/8 credible sets = smoking/mood traits, independently reconfirmed GWAS Catalog record for smoking cessation at the exact lead SNP, and confirmed absence from both flagship COPD and lung-function GWAS) makes smoking-mediation the dominant, best-supported interpretation, not a co-equal alternative. CONTESTED — not on the SOX6 gene call, but on the "direct pulmonary mechanism" component of the researcher's synthesis. Recommend synthesis-lead write this locus up as: SOX6 robustly implicated, association most parsimoniously explained by a smoking-behavior/neurobehavioral pathway rather than direct lung biology; structural/immune evidence noted as weak, exploratory, and insufficient to establish an independent pulmonary mechanism.

Specific critiques of the two citations driving the contested "direct pulmonary mechanism" framing (both citations remain in the main evidence table as VERIFIED, but flagged there with this caveat):

- **Cao S et al. 2023, Cell Res. PMID 37085732** (SOX6 as a distal/alveolar epithelial patterning regulator in human embryonic lung scRNA-seq): "Embryonic distal/alveolar epithelial patterning is developmental biology; adult COPD pathophysiology (emphysematous destruction, protease-antiprotease imbalance, airway remodeling, mucus hypersecretion) is a distinct, largely acquired, smoking-triggered adult process. If SOX6 truly had an obligate role in distal lung patterning, the more biologically expected phenotype would be a rare congenital/structural lung anomaly ..., not adult-onset susceptibility to a smoking-driven disease decades later. The statement 'a direct pulmonary developmental mechanism cannot be ruled out' is technically defensible ... but is doing rhetorical work disproportionate to the evidence — it should be presented as speculative/unresolved, not as counterweight evidence to the smoking-mediation case."

- **Pniewska E et al. 2015, Int Arch Allergy Immunol. PMID 25634111** (SOX6 induced in COPD-patient PBMCs after LPS stimulation): "A single exploratory PBMC/LPS-stimulation differential-expression study cannot establish disease specificity or mechanism: PBMCs are not lung tissue/airway macrophages/epithelium; LPS/TLR4 stimulation is a generic innate-immune challenge used across countless inflammatory-disease transcriptomic screens, not a COPD-specific perturbation; no replication cohort is cited; and a transcript-level change in an exploratory screen is exactly the kind of finding vulnerable to multiple-testing artifacts and reverse-causation/confounding by underlying smoking status of subjects .... This citation supports 'SOX6 expression differs in some immune-stimulation context in COPD-diagnosed subjects' — it does not support 'SOX6 has COPD-specific immune biology.'"

Coverage caveat flagged by red-team-skeptic

Only two structural/immune citations (Pniewska 2015, Cao 2023) appear to underpin the "biology" side of this locus's case. Given how strong and one-directional the smoking-genetics evidence is, worth stating explicitly in the synthesis that adult lung-tissue/airway-epithelium SOX6 expression/functional literature (beyond PBMC/embryonic) was searched for and not found, rather than leaving the impression that coverage of that angle was exhaustive and simply came up empty elsewhere.

Per the researcher's own checkpoint (researcher.md): PubMed searches "SOX6 pulmonary fibrosis emphysema" (0 hits) and "SOX6 lung epithelium airway development" (0 hits) were run and returned no hits; this is stated as a genuine negative finding by the researcher, not an unexamined gap — noted here to close the loop on red-team-skeptic's caveat.

Other locus-disambiguation note (not excluded, included in main table as VERIFIED, repeated here for clarity)

Ziyatdinov A et al. 2019/2020, Eur J Hum Genet, PMID 31836859 — a smoking-by-ancestry admixture-mapping study reporting a lung-function locus at cytoband 11p15.2-3 in African-American COPDGene participants — was checked specifically because of the similar-sounding cytoband name.
