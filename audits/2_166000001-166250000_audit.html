2. PMID 30643251 — CLAIM_MISMATCH

Citation-verifier's verdict (verbatim): "Real GWAS consortium paper, not retracted. However, abstract states: 'We report evidence for the involvement of many systems in tobacco and alcohol use, including genes involved in nicotinic, dopaminergic, and glutamatergic neurotransmission.' It does NOT mention 'sodium, potassium, and calcium voltage-gated channels' as a highlighted pathway class in the abstract. Researcher's claim that pathway/ gene-set enrichment found this pathway cannot be verified from the abstract—the main findings emphasize nicotinic/dopaminergic/glutamatergic systems instead."

Red-team-skeptic's assessment (verbatim, from cross-cutting critique): "This is a load-bearing failure: it was the only piece of evidence connecting this specific gene family to smoking behavior, and it didn't hold up even at the abstract level. Until someone checks the GSCAN full text/supplement directly, this claim should be treated as unsupported, not merely 'unresolved' — the burden of proof was on the researcher's specific claim, and it failed verification."

**Disposition:** Excluded from the evidence table and from the smoking- mediation narrative in the Prioritized-gene section. Note for any future follow-up: citation-verifier checked only the GSCAN abstract, not full text or supplementary tables, so it remains possible (but unconfirmed) that this detail appears in the body text — this should be treated as an open item for a future pass with fuller document access, not as a settled negative.
