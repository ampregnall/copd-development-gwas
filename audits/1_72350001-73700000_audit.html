Audit Report - Locus 1:72350001-73700000 (nearest gene LRRIQ3)

This appendix records every citation excluded from the evidence table, every gene marked CONTESTED/WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic, and process limitations disclosed during this locus's pipeline run. Reasoning is preserved verbatim from the source subagent where possible.

Excluded citations

1. Sakornsakolpat P, McCormack M, Bakke P, Gulsvik A, Make BJ, Crapo JD, Cho MH, Silverman EK. "Genome-Wide Association Analysis of Single-Breath DLCO." American Journal of Respiratory Cell and Molecular Biology, 2019. PMID: 30694715, PMC6503619, DOI: 10.1165/rcmb.2018-0384OC.

**Original claim (researcher):** In a GWAS of single-breath diffusing capacity (DLCO) conducted in four COPD-enriched cohorts (COPDGene, NETT, GenKOLS, TESRA), NEGR1 was one of 12 loci showing suggestive (not genome-wide-significant) association with DLCO in the European-ancestry white analysis (P<1e-5 combined, P<0.05 in both COPDGene and GenKOLS). The researcher self-flagged that the PubMed abstract had gene-symbol formatting corruption and reported having cross-checked against PMC full text directly, but still marked the citation UNVERIFIED pending citation-verifier confirmation.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH (excluded).** Citation-verifier reasoning, verbatim: "Abstract retrieved from PubMed has severe formatting corruption with gene names replaced by commas/punctuation throughout the results section ('including variants near ,,,,,,,,,,,, and'). Cannot verify NEGR1 appears in the 12 suggestive loci list as specific gene names are corrupted. Full text retrieval also returned empty."

**Why this matters (consequential, not routine):** This was the ONLY citation identified in this entire locus review that would have provided directly-pulmonary (as opposed to neurobehavioral/psychiatric) evidence for either candidate gene. Both the researcher and the citation-verifier independently hit the same abstract-corruption obstacle and neither could obtain usable full text to confirm NEGR1's presence in the results table. Per red-team-skeptic: "the researcher should say explicitly ... that zero verified evidence in this dossier supports a direct pulmonary mechanism for either gene; the case for prior #1 over prior #2 here rests entirely on an absence of pulmonary evidence plus an unverified database colocalization, not on any positive verified pulmonary finding." This citation is a candidate for a manual/follow-up re-check outside this automated pipeline (e.g. directly retrieving the PMC6503619 PDF or supplementary table) if the study team wants to close this gap, since the underlying paper may well contain the claimed finding — it is excluded here only because it could not be confirmed through the tools available in this pipeline, not because it was shown to be false or fabricated.

Additional skeptic reasoning on gene-specificity (section b of critique, verbatim): "Johnston et al. explicitly describe this as a 'gene-dense region' containing LINC01360/LRRIQ3/FPGT/FPGT-TNNI3K, with SNPs linked to brain/CNS expression of 'nearby genes' (plural, unspecified). NEGR1 — WEAKENED, verging on CONTESTED for candidate-gene status itself

Process limitations disclosed during this run

  1. **Red-team-skeptic search budget exhaustion.** The red-team-skeptic agent disclosed verbatim: "WebSearch was unavailable for this task (session budget exhausted before I could run any queries — all four of my search attempts returned 'budget used' errors). I substituted direct WebFetch calls against PubMed search URLs as a partial workaround. This gave a shallow contradiction-pass on both candidate genes plus the unlisted third candidate (FPGT-TNNI3K): 'LRRIQ3 lung,' 'NEGR1 lung function FEV1,' 'NEGR1 COPD,' and 'FPGT-TNNI3K lung/COPD/smoking' — all returned zero relevant hits (one NEGR1-COPD fetch failed on a cookie wall rather than genuinely returning zero, so treat that one as unsearched, not confirmed-null). This is weaker than a full independent search and should not be read as an exhaustive contradiction search." This means the critique's own contradiction-search coverage is shallower than the pipeline's usual standard, even though its analytical critique of the researcher's existing citations (below) is substantive and was not limited by the search-budget issue.
  1. **Lead variant literature gap.** A targeted PubMed search for the lead variant rsID "rs12142152" returned zero results. All gene-level GWAS literature for LRRIQ3 in this dossier is anchored to a different (LD-related, not identical) variant, rs640561, from the same broader chromosomal region. This substitution is disclosed in the evidence table and should be weighed accordingly.
  1. **FPGT-TNNI3K not evaluated as an independent candidate.** The researcher noted that published GWAS repeatedly group LRRIQ3/FPGT/FPGT-TNNI3K/LINC01360 together as one pleiotropic LD block, and FPGT-TNNI3K sits immediately adjacent to (3' of) LRRIQ3, but FPGT-TNNI3K was not treated as an independent candidate gene given distance from the lead variant and pipeline scope (it was not in the locus input file's candidate-gene list). Red-team-skeptic's shallow contradiction pass on "FPGT-TNNI3K lung/COPD/smoking" returned zero hits, but flagged this coverage as shallow (see limitation #1). The study team may wish to independently evaluate FPGT-TNNI3K as a candidate if this locus is prioritized for follow-up, given that the strongest cited GWAS/statistical-genetics source for this locus (Johnston et al. 2019) names it alongside LRRIQ3.
