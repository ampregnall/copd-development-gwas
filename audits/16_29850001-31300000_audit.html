Excluded citations

CLAIM_MISMATCH

**Madera-Salcedo IK, Danelli L, Tiwari N, et al. "Tomosyn functions as a PKCδ-regulated fusion clamp in mast cell degranulation." Sci Signal 2018. PMID 29970602, DOI 10.1126/scisignal.aan4350.**

Researcher's original claim: "tomosyn-1 dissociates from STX4 (and associates with STX3) during IgE-mediated mast-cell activation, implicating STX4 as one of several SNARE partners regulating mast-cell mediator release; tomosyn-1 abundance is also elevated in allergic patients."

Citation-verifier's verdict (verbatim): "CLAIM_MISMATCH - Title is 'Tomosyn functions as a PKCδ-regulated fusion clamp in mast cell degranulation.' The claim states 'tomosyn dissociates from STX4 (associates with STX3)' but the title does not mention dissociation or STX3/STX4 specifically - it focuses on tomosyn function as a 'fusion clamp' in degranulation. Claim appears to overstate or mischaracterize the primary finding (verifier examined title/abstract-level evidence only, could not confirm the specific STX4-to-STX3 partner-switching detail as stated)."

Excluded from the evidence table. Not used to support or oppose any gene in this locus's synthesis.

No RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND citations were identified for this locus (38 of 39 citations returned VERIFIED).

Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

The red-team-skeptic's full critique is preserved verbatim in `evidence_tables/_status/16_29850001-31300000/critique.md`; the CONTESTED-gene reasoning is reproduced here for standalone audit purposes.

**Search-budget disclosure (applies to all verdicts below):** "My independent WebSearch calls returned 'budget exhausted' before returning any results (0 of my planned queries executed). My critique below is therefore based entirely on internal-consistency analysis of the researcher's own findings, the citation-verifier's verdicts, and the stated priors — not on new independent literature. This is a real coverage gap on my end: no gene got an independent contradiction-search pass from me this session."

**Locus-level framing:** "This is the single most important finding in the whole dossier, and it's somewhat buried: Open Targets structured L2G shows zero genetic evidence for COPD, FEV1, or FEV1/FVC for any of the 9 genes, and the lead variant's own GWAS Catalog hits are triglycerides/CVD, not lung function or smoking behavior. ... The dominant colocalization signal (lipids, anthropometrics, warfarin, obesity, hypertension, PD) plus the fact that COPD case-control status is frequently confounded by comorbid cardiometabolic disease in EHR/case-ascertainment-based GWAS makes a parsimonious alternative hypothesis plausible: the COPD association at this locus is a pleiotropic/comorbidity artifact (obesity-cardiometabolic-driven case misclassification or shared regulatory architecture with lipid/CVD traits) rather than a lung-mechanism signal, and the PIP concentration on PRSS8/KAT8 reflects LD and regulatory density in this haplotype block, not lung relevance. ... Note also that prior #1 (smoking-mediation) doesn't obviously rescue the locus either — there's no smoking-initiation/cessation/pack-years signal reported at the lead variant. So this doesn't look like a 'smoking behavior gene' case either; it looks more like a metabolic/pleiotropy-driven false-positive-for-lung-biology candidate."

**PRSS8 — CONTESTED.** "The mechanistic case (ENaC/mucociliary clearance) is real but built almost entirely on cystic fibrosis and kidney knockout models, not COPD or cigarette-smoke models — a disease-context leap the researcher discloses but that would become overclaiming if softened in synthesis. More importantly, there is genuine contradicting evidence already in-hand: Casselbrant et al. 2022 found PRSS8 associated with a combined CAD+airflow-obstruction phenotype but explicitly not with airflow obstruction alone — i.e., the one COPD-adjacent genetic finding argues against a pure lung mechanism and toward the cardiometabolic-pleiotropy explanation above. Combined with zero COPD/FEV1 L2G despite PRSS8 harboring the lead SNP at PIP=0.43, 'mechanistic plausibility without genetic corroboration' understates how much active counter-evidence exists. This is oversold if presented as the front-runner without heavy caveats."

**BCKDK — CONTESTED.** "The only genetic-adjacent evidence (rs14235 PD association) failed replication in two independent studies. There is no COPD/lung/smoking literature at all. The case for this gene isn't just weak, it's actively undermined by its own best-available genetic evidence."

**STX1B — CONTESTED.** "Shares the same failed-replication PD signal with BCKDK (can't even discriminate between the two genes at that variant), has an unrelated neuronal-epilepsy disease biology, and the one mast-cell paper cited in its favor actually shows other SNAREs (STX4, VAMP7/8, SNAP-23) driving degranulation functionally, not STX1B. This is a case where the cited evidence contradicts the gene's own candidacy."

**STX4 — CONTESTED.** "Direct head-to-head contradiction already in the dossier: Sanchez et al. 2018 concludes STX3 (not STX4) is required for mast-cell exocytosis, directly opposing the Lam 2020/Puri 1999-era STX4 mast-cell claims. Compounding this, the citation-verifier already flagged Madera-Salcedo et al. 2018 as CLAIM_MISMATCH — the specific 'tomosyn dissociates from STX4 and associates with STX3' mechanism was not supported at the abstract level. This is a pattern (contested underlying biology + an overclaimed citation) that should push STX4 to CONTESTED rather than merely weakened."

**KAT8 — WEAKENED (bordering CONTESTED), not formally CONTESTED but reasoning preserved here for completeness.** "The neutrophil/M2-macrophage evidence comes exclusively from allergic-asthma (papain, HDM) and LPS acute-lung-injury models — none in COPD or smoke-exposure models. This is a real overclaiming risk ...: COPD's inflammatory signature (neutrophilic, CD8+/Th1/Th17-skewed, chronic) is mechanistically distinct from Th2-skewed allergic-asthma biology, even though LPS-ALI neutrophilia is a closer analogy than HDM/papain. ... Also note KAT8's own top Open Targets L2G hits at this locus are metabolic/anthropometric/neurological, directly undercutting a lung-mechanism story."
