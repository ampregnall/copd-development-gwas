Audit Report - Locus 5:153050001-154200000 (nearest gene GRIA1)

This file is the exclusion/contest appendix for `evidence_tables/5_153050001-154200000.md`. It is intended to be handed out only to reviewers who need the full traceability record, separately from the evidence table itself.

Citation verification: excluded citations

**None.** All 23 citations returned by the researcher were verdict VERIFIED by citation-verifier (0 RETRACTED, 0 NOT_FOUND, 0 CLAIM_MISMATCH). Full verification detail, including per-citation evidence quoted from PubMed abstracts, is in `evidence_tables/_status/5_153050001-154200000/verified_citations.md`.

One citation (Ding et al. 2025, PMID 41131870) has an unusually high PMID for its stated year; citation-verifier explicitly checked and confirmed it is a legitimate, existing 2025 publication, not a hallucinated identifier.

Locus-level data-quality note (from researcher)

The researcher flagged that SAP30L (ENSG00000164576), the MAGMA-prioritized gene for this locus, is physically located at 5:154,445,976-154,461,053 — approximately 246kb beyond the stated locus end (154,200,000) — and did not appear in a direct Ensembl overlap query of the locus interval itself. MAGMA gene-based tests use a SNP-to-gene annotation window that can extend gene assignment beyond a locus's nominal fine-mapping boundary, which is a plausible explanation, but this is recorded as an unresolved discrepancy rather than an error, per the researcher's own framing.

The researcher also independently identified, via direct Ensembl region query (not via any of the internal pipeline's four prioritization methods), three additional protein-coding genes physically in or at the edge of the stated locus: FAM114A2, MFAP3, and GALNT10. These were researched and are included in the evidence table for completeness, but none carry internal-pipeline prioritization support.

Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

SAP30L — CONTESTED

"Two independent problems. First, tissue/phenotype relevance: the only functional citation (Teittinen 2012, PMID 22821512) shows cardiac/hematopoietic zebrafish defects — not lung, not immune/inflammatory in the COPD sense. The remaining six citations (PMID 16820529, 18070604, 19015240, 26609676, 29599190, 32294434) are pure Sin3A/HDAC biochemistry with no disease phenotype at all — corepressor-complex structural biology being used to backfill a gene with literally zero COPD genetic-association evidence in Open Targets. Second, and more serious: SAP30L sits ~246kb outside the stated locus boundary (154,445,976-154,461,053 vs. locus end 154,200,000) and did not even appear in the direct Ensembl overlap query for the interval. MAGMA gene-based tests aggregate SNP signal in a window around a gene body (typically ±10-50kb, occasionally larger), so it's possible the association is driven by boundary-proximal SNPs, but a candidate gene whose body sits nearly a quarter-megabase away from the credible-set/locus definition, with zero disease-relevant literature and zero Open Targets support, is a weak nomination on physical-proximity grounds alone — genuine reason to distrust the MAGMA prioritization here rather than treat it as equally-weighted alongside GRIA1. Verdict: CONTESTED — both on physical-distance/LD grounds and on total absence of tissue-relevant biology."

LINC01470 — CONTESTED (for the "regulates GRIA1" inference specifically)

"Carries the lead variant and nearly the entire credible set, but has zero independent literature and zero Open Targets evidence. The researcher's 'bystander/positional annotation, most plausibly regulating GRIA1' framing is explicitly labeled inference, which is appropriate — but this is pure speculation with no eQTL, no Hi-C/chromatin-interaction data, and no expression data cited to support the 'regulates GRIA1' hypothesis specifically (as opposed to regulating SAP30L, FAM114A2, MFAP3, or nothing at all). Long intronic/ non-coding credible sets like this are exactly the scenario where the nearest-protein-coding- gene assumption is weakest. 'Most plausibly GRIA1' should not be treated as established — it is one of several equally unsupported guesses. Verdict: CONTESTED for the specific 'regulates GRIA1' inference (not for the gene's existence/role generally, which the researcher correctly declines to claim)."

MFAP3 — CONTESTED

"Clean case of paralog substitution: the only lung-relevant citation (Zhu et al. 2020, PMID 32572962) is about MFAP4, not MFAP3, showing emphysema-like pathology in Mfap4-knockout mice. The researcher correctly flags this, and correctly notes MFAP3 itself has no lung-specific knockout phenotype anywhere and is 'ubiquitously expressed.' The original cloning paper (Abrams 1995, PMID 7782085) locates MFAP3 in ocular-zonule microfibrils — eye tissue, not lung, and 30 years old with no follow-up disease association. There is no genuine MFAP3-COPD or MFAP3-lung-function evidence at all; the entire case is 'sits near a locus, has a paralog with an unrelated emphysema phenotype.' That is not evidence for MFAP3, it's an absence of evidence dressed up by proximity. Verdict: CONTESTED."

GALNT10 — CONTESTED

"Only overlaps the last ~10kb of the locus (mostly outside it), and its only citations are three oncology papers in unrelated organs (gastric PMID 33275228, cholangiocarcinoma PMID 33301605, HCC PMID 25422324). The researcher's implicit rationale — GALNT10 is a mucin-glycosylation enzyme and mucin biology is COPD-relevant — is a plausible mechanistic hypothesis but is not supported by any citation actually shown; it's pattern-matching from gene function class, not literature. No lung/airway/mucociliary citation was found despite what should be a fairly directed search space (airway mucin biology is a well-studied COPD theme). Reads as a gene included for completeness with essentially no supporting evidence. Verdict: CONTESTED."

Genes marked WEAKENED (not full CONTESTED, but flagged for overclaiming risk) — verbatim reasoning

GRIA1 — WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED

"The researcher's own account undercuts the headline claim. Every statistical-genetics citation for GRIA1 is a 'could not confirm' (Sakornsakolpat PMID 30804561, Shrine PMID 36914875, Saunders/GSCAN PMID 36477530) except one: Nievergelt et al. 2024 (PMID 38637617), a PTSD GWAS that names GRIA1 as a prioritized gene in an entirely different phenotype. The 'smoking-mediated' framing is built on (a) a PTSD gene list, (b) a paralog (GRIA4, PMID 30287806) associated with nicotine-dependence/depression comorbidity, and (c) a CNS mouse-genetics paper on alcohol relapse (PMID 26609150) — none of which is GRIA1-COPD, GRIA1-lung-function, or GRIA1-smoking-GWAS evidence at *this* locus. The neuronal/ psychiatric literature is more plausible than the (nonexistent) pulmonary literature, but 'consistent with a smoking-behavior-mediated mechanism' overstates what's shown — it should read 'no direct pulmonary evidence exists, and the only positive genetic signal for this gene family is in neuropsychiatric/addiction traits, which is suggestive but not a confirmed smoking-GWAS overlap at this specific locus.' The airway-smooth-muscle papers (PMID 25888577, 27371735, 22447835) are explicitly NMDA/GRIN receptors, not AMPA/GRIA1 — correctly flagged by the researcher as off-target, but this is a receptor-subtype mismatch, a classic overclaiming trap (glutamate receptor superfamily != GRIA1-specific mechanism). Given (1) zero direct GRIA1-COPD or GRIA1-lung-function evidence, (2) zero confirmed overlap with smoking-behavior GWAS loci, and (3) a credible set 100% annotated to LINC01470 rather than GRIA1 itself, GRIA1's case rests entirely on nearest-gene + FLAMES/PoPS computational prioritization plus indirect family/PTSD-list inference. Verdict: WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED given total absence of both pulmonary and confirmed-smoking evidence for GRIA1 itself."

FAM114A2 — WEAKENED

"Not flagged by internal prioritization, found only by manual Ensembl query — should be weighted below the internally-prioritized genes. The De et al. 2022 citation (PMID 34380996) is about anti-TNFalpha nonresponse in IBD via a FAM114A2 eQTL variant (rs34767465) and macrophage autophagy/TNFalpha secretion — a plausible inflammatory mechanism (macrophage/TNF axis is relevant to COPD's innate-immune programs per the locus prior on inflammatory/immune complexity), but different disease, different variant, and critically no LD check was done between rs34767465 and this locus's credible set. Without that, this is a 'same gene symbol, different signal' risk — cannot be used as if it were locus-specific evidence. Ding et al. 2025 (PMID 41131870, HIV-latency/PKC-beta-NF-kB) is essentially irrelevant to COPD and reads like a keyword-match citation. Verdict: WEAKENED — the TNF/autophagy mechanism is biologically interesting and worth flagging for follow-up (specifically an LD/coloc check against rs34767465), but as presented it is not locus-specific evidence and shouldn't be scored as if it were."

Red-team-skeptic's search-coverage caveat (applies to this entire critique)

"My web-search budget was exhausted before I could run any independent contradiction searches (all four queries — GRIA1/rs57976819 vs. smoking-GWAS loci, GRIA1 vs. lung-function GWAS tables, SAP30L vs. COPD, FAM114A2 vs. lung/emphysema — returned 'budget exhausted,' zero results). None of the six genes at this locus received an independent verification or contradiction search from me. This critique is therefore based entirely on internal consistency, logical scrutiny of the researcher's own citations, and the standing biological priors, not on new literature. ... If synthesis-lead has search budget remaining, this locus (all six genes, but GRIA1 and SAP30L especially) should get an independent contradiction pass before final write-up."

This caveat is carried forward unresolved: no independent contradiction search was performed for this locus by the orchestrating pipeline beyond what is recorded above. Any future rerun of this locus should prioritize (1) a direct GSCAN/PGC substance-use GWAS locus-table lookup at rs57976819/GRIA1, (2) confirmation of whether GRIA1 is among the 559 genes in Shrine et al. 2023's lung-function gene list, and (3) an LD/colocalization check between the FAM114A2 IBD eQTL variant (rs34767465) and this locus's credible set.
