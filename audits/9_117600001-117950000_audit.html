Genes/claims marked CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

TNC — CONTESTED

Invoking CONTESTED rather than WEAKENED because there is a specific, verified, independently-confirmed (Ensembl REST + Open Targets, per the dossier) genomic fact — TNC does not lie within this GRCh38-defined locus — that directly contradicts the premise that TNC is a candidate gene "at this locus" at all. Combined with TNC's near-zero Open Targets genetic_association score (0.06, literature-only), its absence from L2G at this locus, its absence from any locus-specific GWAS/eQTL/coloc evidence, and its own tissue-expression literature being internally split (Löfdahl positive vs. Muñoz-Esquerre negative), the case for TNC at this locus should be treated as most likely a coordinate/build artifact in the MAGMA/PoPS pipeline rather than genuine biological linkage. Recommend the synthesis-lead flag this upstream to whoever runs the statistical-prioritization step, since a systematic GRCh37/38 mismatch of this kind could affect other loci in the same pipeline run, not just this one. This should be marked for re-verification of the underlying MAGMA/PoPS input coordinates — that re-check is a statistical/pipeline QA issue, not a new literature citation, so it doesn't require citation-verifier re-routing, but it does warrant a pipeline-level bug report.

Underlying finding this verdict is based on: under GRCh38 (the build stated for this locus), TNC's actual gene coordinates are chr9:115,019,575-115,118,257 (confirmed independently by both Ensembl REST, queried by the researcher, and Open Targets `genomicLocation`, queried directly by the orchestrator) — approximately 2.6-2.7 Mb OUTSIDE this locus's stated interval (117,600,001-117,950,000), i.e. TNC does not physically overlap this locus at all under the stated build. TNC's GRCh37/hg19 coordinates (chr9:117,782,806-117,880,536) DO numerically fall inside the locus's stated GRCh38 range, which is consistent with a genome-build coordinate mismatch having occurred somewhere in the upstream MAGMA/PoPS gene-prioritization step.

TLR4 — WEAKENED (not CONTESTED, but flagged as overclaimed if treated as mechanistically confirmed)

TLR4 is the nearest gene, the only protein-coding gene physically in the GRCh38 interval, and has a large (if literature-driven) prior COPD literature base — none of which is nothing. But the specific inferential chain "credible-set variant -> TLR4 expression/function -> COPD" has no direct support: no eQTL, no coloc with expression or lung-function traits, and a purpose-built systematic eQTL-integration study that looked for exactly this and did not find it (Lamontagne 2018). The supporting mechanistic literature is real but is about different variants, is directionally inconsistent across both mouse models and human populations, and its human-disease association score in Open Targets is explicitly noted to be literature-inflated rather than genetics-driven. The colocalization pattern (behavioral/ frailty traits, not lung function, not smoking) further complicates a clean "pulmonary mechanism" story. I would not call this CONTESTED (there is no credible paper naming a different gene at this locus, and TLR4 is genuinely the only positional candidate), but the causal-gene claim as often stated ("TLR4 is the COPD gene at this locus") overreaches what the locus-specific evidence supports; it should be presented as "positional/statistical candidate with a large but non-locus-specific literature base and no direct functional/eQTL tie to the credible-set variants."

Specific internally-contradictory citation pairs flagged by the skeptic (both individually VERIFIED, but jointly illustrating that the underlying biology/genetics is contested, not settled): - Zhang et al. 2006 (PMID 17053835) reports TLR4 loss-of-function causes spontaneous emphysema in unchallenged aging mice, while Haw et al. 2017 (PMID 29025711) reports TLR4 loss-of-function protects against cigarette-smoke-induced emphysema — opposite direction of effect depending on the exposure model; Haw et al.'s own abstract states TLR2/TLR4's role in COPD pathogenesis is "controversial and conflicting." - Rohde et al. 2005 (PMID 16219455) reports the TLR4 Asp299Gly minor allele is protective against COPD, while Gutiérrez-Romero et al. 2024 (PMID 39139567) reports the same SNP's minor allele (AA genotype) confers markedly increased COPD risk (OR=3.53) in a different population — a direct cross-population reversal of effect direction for the identical variant. - Ito et al. 2012 (PMID 22251849) and Berenson et al. 2015 (PMID 26361369) both report null/ negative findings for TLR4 variants once adjusted for age/smoking or compared across groups, further undercutting the robustness of the candidate-gene literature as a whole.

Cross-cutting caveats carried into this audit (not verdicts on a single gene, but process/data gaps)

  1. **Red-team-skeptic's WebSearch budget was exhausted before it could run any independent confirmation searches for this locus.** Its critique is a re-analysis of the researcher/citation-verifier dossier only, not a fresh independent literature check. This is a genuine limitation of this locus's review, not a confirmed absence of further contradicting evidence.
  2. **The orchestrator's own WebSearch budget was also exhausted** partway through this locus's review, preventing independent confirmation of rs1927907's exact genomic coordinates relative to the credible-set cluster (see citation 6 above).
  3. **Unresolved gap:** whether this exact locus (chr9:117.6-117.95Mb / rs1887781 / TLR4) is among the loci reported in Shrine et al. 2019 (279 lung-function signals) or Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 (82 COPD loci) could not be determined from PubMed/bioRxiv abstract search alone; this would require direct supplementary-table lookup and was not completed for this review.
  4. **Alternative-hypothesis flag (per pipeline prior #1/#2):** Open Targets colocalization at this locus is strong for subjective well-being, hip osteoarthritis, frailty (general factor), shortness of breath, and mood swings (5 UKB releases) — but absent for both spirometric lung-function traits (FEV1, FEV1/FVC) and smoking-behavior traits (initiation, cigarettes/day, pack-years, cessation). Neither of the pipeline's two standard alternative explanations (classic pulmonary biology vs. smoking-behavior mediation) is cleanly supported by the locus-specific colocalization data; a third possibility — a general frailty/subjective-symptom/behavioral-health axis — is raised by the red-team-skeptic and should be weighed by the study team rather than defaulted away.
