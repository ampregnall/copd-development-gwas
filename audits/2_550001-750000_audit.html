Red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

**Disclosure on skeptic's own search pass:** The skeptic reported that its own attempted independent contradiction-search pass (two queries: "TMEM18 lung function FEV1 COPD gene expression" and "TMEM18 rs6548238 smoking pack-years pleiotropy neural tissue") returned zero results because its web-search tool budget was already exhausted before its turn began — i.e., no search was actually executed. The critique below is based on re-reasoning over the researcher's already-verified evidence, not an independent literature check. This is recorded transparently and should be treated as an open/undone check, not a completed null result.

1. Is "BMI/smoking pleiotropy, no independent pulmonary biology" well-supported?

Largely yes — one of the better-supported pleiotropy calls in this pipeline, given: (a) zero Open Targets L2G evidence for COPD and zero evidence of any kind for FEV1, against 127 L2G rows for BMI; (b) the single most mechanistically rigorous TMEM18 study (Larder et al. 2017, PMID 28811369) is exhaustively CNS/appetite-focused and never examined lung phenotype; (c) the cell-biology papers (Jurvansuu 2008, 2011) are non-lung and correctly caveated as such. HOWEVER: "no independent pulmonary support was found" is a literature-search statement, not equivalent to "pulmonary biology has been tested and ruled out." Larder et al. didn't look at lungs at all — absence of inquiry, not a negative result. The researcher does not overclaim the stronger version, which is correct restraint, but this distinction should be preserved in the final write-up.

2. Alternative explanations not considered

The diffuse, 173-variant, entirely-noncoding, flat-PIP (max 0.0368) credible set spanning ~45kb is the architecture expected for a large LD block tagging a regulatory haplotype, consistent with (strengthening, not weakening) the BMI-pleiotropy reading — this overlaps the well-known 2p25.3 obesity LD block from the original BMI GWAS discovery papers. The lead PIP variant (rs4423631) not itself being a known BMI hit is statistically unremarkable in a large flat LD block (fine-mapping often surfaces a different top-PIP SNP than the discovery tag SNP) and should not be over-weighted as evidence against the BMI explanation. A genuine but purely speculative alternative not explored: an uncharacterized lung-specific regulatory element/lncRNA in this interval acting independently of TMEM18/BMI biology — zero evidence for this, but it should be named as an untested possibility rather than treated as closed off.

3. Are citations #2 (Çolak 2016) and #1/Claim A+B (Mikkelsen 2025) appropriately weighted?

This is the weakest point in the researcher's synthesis. Both papers test a **composite BMI allele score** (FTO + MC4R + TMEM18, +2 more in Mikkelsen) — not TMEM18 individually. These studies cannot distinguish whether the observed BMI-COPD-exacerbation/pneumonia or BMI-COPD-risk effect is driven by FTO (by far the largest-effect, most pleiotropic BMI locus), MC4R, or TMEM18's contribution specifically. These should be understood as evidence that *the BMI-obesity pathway* causally affects COPD outcomes/morbidity, **not** as evidence that TMEM18's gene product itself does anything COPD-relevant. This distinction should be made explicit in the evidence table (downgrade from "TMEM18 evidence" framing).

The ambiguity is compounded for the smoking-pleiotropy claim: the verifier could not confirm the Mikkelsen et al. discussion sentence about "two genetic variants... pleiotropic effects on smoking... expressed in neural tissue" refers specifically to TMEM18 (vs. FTO+MC4R, or FTO + one of the other three BMI SNPs). The strongest actual evidence for TMEM18-specific smoking-pleiotropy is the **Open Targets L2G background data** (7 rows, 0.42-0.44), not the ambiguous secondhand MR-paper sentence — this distinction should be made explicit in the write-up.

4. Should TMEM18 be marked CONTESTED?

**No.** CONTESTED should be reserved for cases where credible literature actively disagrees about the gene's role. Here there is no such disagreement — every piece of evidence is compatible with one coherent story (real BMI/adiposity and smoking-behavior genetic effects at 2p25.3, no demonstrated independent lung mechanism for TMEM18 itself). The negative-result citations (Mishra & Mishra 2025 not implicating TMEM18; Brazel et al. 2019 not mentioning TMEM18/2p25.3) are transparently reported as documented negative searches, not competing claims.

**Correct verdict: WEAKENED.** The evidence is real (strong BMI genetic support, moderate independent smoking-initiation genetic support, a plausible causal MR pathway from BMI/smoking to COPD morbidity), but the inference that TMEM18 itself (as opposed to the BMI/smoking pathway broadly, potentially driven by FTO or other loci in composite instruments) is a COPD-relevant gene overreaches what the gene-specific evidence supports. Zero L2G evidence for COPD, zero FEV1 evidence, and a mouse KO study that never examined lungs collectively mean the pulmonary-biology case for TMEM18 specifically is essentially absent, not just weak.

5. Other notes

- No overclaiming detected in handling of negative/excluded citations (#7 Mishra, #8 Brazel, and the Lamonaca exclusion) — transparent, good-faith reporting. - The Jurvansuu papers (#4, #6) are appropriately caveated as general/non-lung molecular biology, not stretched into false lung relevance. - Correction needed before synthesis: explicitly downgrade citations #2/#1 from "TMEM18-specific evidence" to "BMI-pathway/composite-instrument evidence in which TMEM18 is one of several unweighted SNPs," and explicitly attribute the smoking-pleiotropy claim to the Open Targets L2G background stat rather than to the ambiguous Mikkelsen discussion sentence. - Gene identification thoroughness is not in question (sole protein-coding gene at locus, Ensembl-confirmed). Main residual gap is the skeptic's own unfulfilled independent search pass (budget exhausted) — treat as an open, undone check.

**Overall verdict for TMEM18 at this locus: WEAKENED (not CONTESTED).**
