CONTESTED gene: NUDT19

Red-team-skeptic verdict, reasoning preserved verbatim:

**NUDT19 — CONTESTED (effectively disqualified as reported).** Credible contradicting evidence is already in the record, not newly found by me, but it is underweighted by calling it merely "undermined": (1) the gene's own coordinates place it ~440–500kb outside the stated locus/credible-set window — a PoPS window artifact rather than membership in the fine-mapped interval; (2) it was never an L2G hit at the lead variant; (3) zero human genetic association with COPD, asthma, or lung function anywhere in the literature search; (4) the only COPD-specific evidence is 3 IMPC mouse-KO rows via the animal_model datasource, which is thin and non-specific. Candidacy rests entirely on one genome-wide similarity-based method (PoPS) with no corroboration from any fine-mapping, colocalization, or human-genetics source, and is contradicted by a simple physical-location fact. I'd recommend synthesis lead treat this as very likely a false positive rather than a genuine competing candidate, pending confirmation of the coordinate discrepancy.

All NUDT19 citations in the evidence table (Shumar 2018, Saporito 2026, Görigk 2022, Song 2013, Wang 2026) are individually VERIFIED as accurately described, but every one of them describes non-lung (renal/hepatic/oncologic/molecular) biology with no independent connection to COPD — they are included in the evidence table only as accurately-cited negative/ non-supportive findings, consistent with the CONTESTED verdict above.
