Genes flagged WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (neither gene was marked CONTESTED)

Per pipeline instructions, this appendix must preserve verbatim any CONTESTED gene reasoning. Red-team-skeptic did not mark either candidate gene CONTESTED at this locus — both were marked **WEAKENED**. That reasoning is preserved verbatim below for full traceability, since it materially shaped the confidence level in the Prioritized Gene section of the evidence table.

LILRB2 — WEAKENED (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

Real statistical/variant-level case within the LILR cluster, but COPD-specific mechanistic evidence is absent, spirometry concordance is explicitly negative in the largest available study, the L2G signal is multi-trait pleiotropic rather than COPD-specific, paralogs are not cleanly excluded, and the missense-variant literature contains corrected/reversed/unconfirmed claims that should be downweighted or excluded per the list above.

Supporting detail (verbatim): "The strongest gene-specific (not just locus-specific) evidence is that two credible-set variants are missense changes within the LILRB2 coding sequence (rs383369 R20H, PIP=0.378; rs386056, PIP=0.047) and one is an intronic LILRB2 variant carrying the top PIP (0.433). That's better than pure "nearest gene" bias — it's variant-level gene assignment, not proximity inference. However, the Open Targets record itself undercuts confidence: LILRA1 and LILRB5 show molecular-QTL colocalization at the same variants in monocyte/macrophage panels but simply lack a GWAS study to generate an L2G score — that's an artifact of statistical power/study availability, not evidence those paralogs are uninvolved. In a family of near-identical duplicated genes with shared regulatory architecture, this is a real, unresolved ambiguity, and the case rests substantially on the L2G algorithm's output plus generic LILR-family immunobiology (inhibitory receptor on myeloid cells) that mechanistically could apply about equally to LILRB2, LILRB3, or LILRA5."

On pleiotropy (verbatim): "A variant with equally high L2G to LILRB2 for hematocrit and HDL-C, in addition to COPD, is more consistent with LILRB2 sitting at a generic myeloid/blood-cell regulatory hub than with COPD-specific pulmonary biology. This should temper — not eliminate — confidence in a COPD-specific mechanism."

On spirometry discordance (verbatim): "Per this pipeline's own prior #2, spirometry concordance is supposed to strengthen prioritization, and its absence 'warrants scrutiny.' Here we have an actual negative: the largest multi-ancestry lung-function GWAS (~400K, 279 signals) found nothing at 19q13.42/LILRB2. That is a direct strike against a baseline-airflow-limitation mechanism."

Thoroughness flag (verbatim): "The researcher's own stated gap (no COPD sputum/BAL/CD8+ T-cell/macrophage-polarization literature for LILRB2) should be taken seriously. LILRB2 has multiple aliases (ILT4, CD85d, LIR-2, MIR-10); I can't confirm from the record whether alias-based searching was exhaustive. Recommend synthesis-lead flag this for a possible supplementary search pass before finalizing, rather than treating current absence as definitive absence."

NLRP12 — WEAKENED (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

The PoPS signal is a genuinely independent line of evidence (function-similarity-based, not positional), and dismissing it outright would be overreach given prior #3's instruction to consider heterogeneous inflammatory programs rather than defaulting to the positionally-obvious gene. But it currently has zero statistical fine-mapping or colocalization support at this locus, sits physically outside the credible-set window, and its only human-disease phenotype is mechanistically distant. This is WEAKENED — a plausible, mechanistically interesting alternative that should remain flagged in the synthesis as low-confidence rather than being either promoted to a real competing candidate or fully dropped. It should not be called CONTESTED, because nothing in the record actively argues against an NLRP12 role in COPD specifically — the evidence is simply absent/indirect, not contradictory.

On the negative allergic-airway mouse study (verbatim): "correctly, the researcher/framing notes this tested a Th2/OVA/HDM asthma-type model specifically and found no phenotype; it does not test a neutrophilic, cigarette-smoke, or innate-immune/inflammasome-driven model, which is the more COPD-relevant inflammatory program per this pipeline's prior #3. NLRP12 is a documented negative regulator of NF-κB/inflammasome signaling in myeloid cells (Allen 2012, Lukens 2015), so a hypothesis where NLRP12 loss-of-function or reduced expression permits excess NF-κB-driven neutrophilic/innate inflammation in the lung is mechanistically coherent and untested directly, not refuted."

Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

Preliminary disclosure — search budget exhausted before independent checks could run

I attempted four targeted contradiction/verification searches (paralog-specificity in COPD fine-mapping, smoking/nicotine-GWAS lookup for rs383925/rs383369, NLRP12-lung-function check, LILRB2 lung eQTL check) and all four failed — the session's web search budget was already exhausted (200/200 used) before my queries ran. I was not able to complete even a shallow independent pass on either gene. This critique is therefore based entirely on internal analysis of the researcher/verifier record provided, not on any new literature I retrieved. Synthesis-lead should treat both LILRB2 and NLRP12 as having received zero independent contradiction-search coverage from me, and should weight that gap accordingly (e.g., a follow-up pass on LILR-family GWAS/smoking-GWAS lookups is genuinely still needed, not just a formality).

Smoking-mediation

The diesel-exhaust MR finding (Li A 2022, PMID 35602164) is worth flagging explicitly as a distinct confound axis from the pipeline's core smoking-behavior concern (prior #1). Prior #1 is about behavioral smoking genetics (initiation/dependence/heaviness) confounding unadjusted COPD case-control GWAS. The diesel-exhaust MR result is about a toxicant/particulate-exposure-response mechanism — plausibly relevant to cigarette smoke as an inhaled particulate too, but that would actually be a genuine lung-relevant inflammatory pathway (response to inhaled particulates), not a behavioral confound. These should not be conflated: this citation raises an "inhalation-response biology" hypothesis, not evidence that the locus is really about smoking initiation/nicotine dependence rather than lung pathology. Given searches for direct smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence GWAS associations at rs383925/rs383369 failed to run (budget exhaustion), this specific prior-#1 check is unresolved and should be flagged as an open item for a follow-up GWAS-catalog lookup, not assumed either way.

No new citations were surfaced by this critique (independent search budget was exhausted before any query executed), so no additional items were sent back to citation-verifier.

Additional context notes (not citations, not evidence — locus/gene-mapping caveats from the researcher)

- NLRP12 (the PoPS-prioritized gene) is **not physically within** the 19:54,200,001-54,350,000 credible-set window: its gene body is at chr19:53,792,139-53,824,403 (GRCh38), ~375-560 kb centromeric of the window. PoPS is a similarity-based method not restricted by physical distance, so this does not itself invalidate the PoPS score, but it does weaken the positional plausibility of a direct regulatory relationship between the credible-set variants and NLRP12. - LILRA1 (ENSG00000104974) is annotated as an unprocessed pseudogene in the current Ensembl GRCh38 gene set for this region — it shows molecular-QTL colocalization at the same credible-set variants (per Open Targets) but was not pursued as an independent protein-coding candidate given this pseudogene annotation. - RPS9 (a housekeeping ribosomal protein gene) also physically spans much of this window but has no documented COPD/immune/structural lung biology and was not pursued as a candidate. - Background-only oncology literature on LILRB2/ILT4 (7 PMIDs: 38705566, 37622462, 33537094, 38313233, 37444609, 32549766, 38450191) was spot-checked for existence only (all VERIFIED as real, non-retracted publications) but deliberately not itemized as individual COPD-supporting evidence rows in the main table, per both the researcher's and red-team-skeptic's judgment that these are background biological-plausibility context (LILRB2 is genuinely expressed/functional in lung tissue myeloid/tumor microenvironment) rather than COPD-specific findings.
