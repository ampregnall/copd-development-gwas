TCF20 — WEAKENED

"The fine-mapping case is strong and well-described... But the functional/disease case built on top of it is where the overreach lives.

- Vetrini 2019 (verified, claim matches) establishes TCF20 haploinsufficiency causes a monogenic neurodevelopmental syndrome. This is real but is evidence for a completely different phenotype (developmental delay/ID) via a completely different presumed mechanism (dosage-sensitive haploinsufficiency from severe LoF variants) than a common noncoding intronic variant (rs8142378, MODIFIER impact) nudging COPD risk in a GWAS. Citing a monogenic LoF-syndrome paper to support relevance of a common noncoding regulatory variant is a category error common in these prioritizations — it establishes the gene is biologically important and dosage-sensitive, not that this variant/mechanism is relevant to airflow obstruction. This should be labeled explicitly as constraint/precedent evidence, not mechanistic disease evidence. - The Xu 2024 circRNA-Tcf20 citation is honestly flagged by the researcher itself as mechanistically distinct (acute LPS sepsis/ARDS, not chronic COPD) and about a circRNA transcribed from the locus rather than TCF20 protein. Good that this was caveated, but its inclusion at all risks being read as 'TCF20 has an acute lung injury connection' when it's really 'a transcript from this genomic region shows differential expression in an unrelated acute lung injury model.' I'd downgrade this to near-irrelevant rather than supportive-with-caveats. - The GSCAN (Liu 2019) citation is the crux issue. The citation-verifier flagged CLAIM_MISMATCH... The researcher's framing — 'TCF20 as smoking-behavior-mediated candidate, but unverified' — is more hedged than an unqualified claim would be... That hedge is appropriate and should stay in the writeup. However, the framing still risks overclaiming in synthesis: calling TCF20 'the smoking-behavior-mediated candidate' implies smoking-mediation is the leading mechanistic hypothesis for this locus, when in fact the only actual evidence for that link is an aggregated Open Targets score (0.267, itself downstream of unknown underlying GWAS hits, possibly including this exact GSCAN study that failed verification) — not an independently confirmed genetic association at this locus... That's a meaningfully weaker claim than 'candidate for smoking-mediated mechanism,' and the synthesis lead should not upgrade this into 'TCF20 acts via smoking behavior' without the GSCAN locus-level check being redone. - Total absence of TCF20-COPD, TCF20-lung-function, and rs8142378 literature (zero PubMed hits across all three targeted searches) is itself important negative evidence given prior #2... This is a locus where 'we don't know' is the honest state, not 'probably smoking-mediated.'

Verdict: WEAKENED. The fine-mapping support for TCF20 as the causal gene is solid, and constraint argues for a real functional gene at this locus. But the disease-mechanism narrative overreaches... The honest summary is 'TCF20 is the fine-mapped gene at this locus with no confirmed lung, COPD, or smoking-locus-level evidence; smoking-behavior mediation is a plausible but currently unsubstantiated hypothesis, not a finding.'"

SHISA8 — WEAKENED

"Zero credible-set variant overlap (PoPS-only) is a serious problem independent of anything else — per the scope note, PoPS reflects genome-wide functional similarity, not this locus's actual fine-mapping, so SHISA8's inclusion as a 'candidate' here is already on much weaker footing than TCF20 or even NFAM1. All three cited papers... describe exclusively neuronal, non-pulmonary, non-immune biology, and the researcher's own summary judgment says this plainly. No overclaiming detected here — if anything the researcher already appropriately deflated this gene... Verdict: WEAKENED (already appropriately downgraded by the researcher, but flagging that its presence in the candidate list at all should be clearly labeled as PoPS-genome-wide-similarity-only, not locus-supported, in any synthesis table — a reader skimming gene names could mistake it for having comparable statistical support to TCF20)."

NFAM1 — WEAKENED

"The physical case is very weak: one synonymous variant at PIP=0.003 is essentially noise-level in a credible set where TCF20 carries 86%+ of the mass — this variant would not be expected to survive an LD/fine-mapping sanity check as the causal signal. That needs to be stated unambiguously in any synthesis, not softened into 'plausible immune-relevant candidate.'

On the biological side, per prior #3..., NFAM1 is an ITAM-signaling adaptor with genuine immune biology, and the researcher is right to note the asthma-exacerbation transcriptomic association (Liu 2025) as the only airway-disease-adjacent evidence. But that study is asthma, not COPD — these are overlapping but distinct diseases with different dominant inflammatory programs (Th2/eosinophilic vs. the neutrophilic/CD8+/Th1/Th17 mix more typical of COPD per the priors)... The other three immune citations... are generic immune-adaptor biology with no lung specificity at all — they support 'NFAM1 is a real immune signaling gene' but not 'NFAM1 is relevant to COPD lung pathology' specifically. The Open Targets 'upper respiratory tract disorder' score (0.178) is explicitly caveated by the researcher as weak, non-COPD-specific, and unsupported by any fine-mapping/colocalization (none exists at all for this region) — appropriately hedged, not overclaimed.

Net assessment on NFAM1 as a competing candidate: the immune biology is real but diffuse and non-COPD-specific, and it is undermined by the near-total absence of statistical support at this locus (0.003 PIP, synonymous, non-lead). I do not think NFAM1 is currently a credible alternative to TCF20 at this locus — it reads more like 'a gene with interesting general immune biology that happens to sit near the locus,' which is exactly the LD/nearest-gene-bias concern prior #1 warns about. The researcher's own summary judgment already lands here appropriately.

Verdict: WEAKENED. The immune-relevance framing is fair as generic gene biology but overstates NFAM1's case as a locus candidate — it should be presented as a low-probability, non-specific alternative, not a competing candidate on par with TCF20. No direct COPD evidence exists for NFAM1 at all (only asthma and non-pulmonary immune contexts), and its statistical support at the locus is negligible."

Overall framing assessment (verbatim)

"As written by the researcher (with the 'but unverified'/'could not be traced to a verified primary GWAS locus-level finding' hedge included), this is appropriately cautious and should NOT be simplified upward in synthesis... the defensible synthesis statement is: 'TCF20 is the dominant fine-mapped gene at this locus by PIP, is highly constrained, and has strong monogenic neurodevelopmental-disease precedent; there is no confirmed pulmonary-function or COPD-specific mechanistic evidence, and the smoking-behavior-mediation hypothesis — while consistent with TCF20's known neurobehavioral disease spectrum and an aggregate Open Targets smoking-initiation signal — has not been independently confirmed at the primary-GWAS locus level (the GSCAN citation intended to support it failed independent verification of chromosome/locus relevance).' Anything shorter that drops the hedge (e.g., 'TCF20 likely acts through smoking behavior') would be overclaiming relative to the actual evidence base, and I'd flag that as a CONTESTED framing if it appears in synthesis without the caveat. As currently written by the researcher, it holds because the hedge is intact — this is a caution for the synthesis-lead's downstream compression, not a fault in the researcher's own text.
