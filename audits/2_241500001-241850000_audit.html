Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

D2HGDH — CONTESTED

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

This is the locus's biggest overclaim risk. D2HGDH's "highest OT COPD score" is being used to call it "the strongest statistical-genetics candidate," but that score is entirely composed of ~50 independent GWAS credible sets for asthma, allergic rhinitis, atopic dermatitis, eosinophil count/fraction, and hayfever — never COPD directly, and no FEV1/FEV1-FVC support is reported either. Per prior #2, a COPD case-control signal without spirometric support should be viewed skeptically for alternative pathways; here it's worse — the actual supporting genetic signal is for a completely different, Th2/ eosinophilic/atopic disease cluster. The researcher's own Hayden et al. 2018 citation (PMID 30373671) is doing more rhetorical work than its content supports: it shows childhood-asthma history predicts adult COPD in smokers and that *other, named* asthma loci (IL1RL1, IL13, GSDMB, C11orf30-LRRC32) associate with childhood asthma in a COPD cohort — it says nothing about D2HGDH and was not designed to test this locus. Using it as a bridge to argue D2HGDH's COPD relevance via asthma-COPD overlap is exactly the kind of citation-relevance overreach the instructions ask me to flag: the citation is accurate (verifier confirmed it), but its deployment here strengthens the D2HGDH argument beyond what it can support. The more parsimonious alternative reading: this locus (via D2HGDH/GAL3ST2, its consistent OT-favored gene pair) drives an eosinophilic/atopic phenotype, and its appearance in the COPD GWAS could reflect case misclassification/ comorbid asthma contamination in COPD case ascertainment (a known issue in large-scale, often self-report or ICD-code-based COPD GWAS) rather than a neutrophilic/pulmonary- obstruction mechanism. I'd mark this CONTESTED: not because D2HGDH lacks a real genetic signal at this locus, but because the signal, on its own terms, argues for an allergic/ atopic/eosinophilic-disease gene that has been re-labeled as a COPD candidate largely on the strength of an aggregate score and an inapplicable precedent citation. Synthesis-lead should treat D2HGDH as "plausible pleiotropic/comorbidity confound with atopic disease" rather than "top COPD candidate."

Genes marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (not full CONTESTED, but flagged as

ATG4B — WEAKENED

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

The "strongest circumstantial case" framing overreaches on three counts. First, the lead variant's own PIP is only 0.046 — very low, meaning the fine-mapping itself is highly uncertain and most posterior mass sits elsewhere (including on D2HGDH/GAL3ST2/ING5-linked variants). Calling ATG4B "strongest" when it isn't even confidently the top variant-level candidate within its own credible set overstates confidence. Second, there is no GWAS/coloc/TWAS/MR evidence linking ATG4B to COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC at all — the entire case rests on Chen et al. 2008 (PMID 18830406), which is about cigarette-smoke-extract- induced autophagy via Egr-1/E2F-4 and an *Egr-1* (not *Atg4b*) knockout mouse phenotype. That's real pulmonary biology, but it is two steps removed from this locus: no ATG4B genetic variant, human or mouse, is shown to modulate this pathway or COPD risk. Third, and most concerning, OT's own L2G analysis at the exact lead SNP (rs62190299) assigns it to ATG4B most confidently for a **BMI** credible set (0.682), not COPD — i.e., the one piece of independent gene-assignment evidence for this precise variant points to a metabolic/adiposity trait, not lung. That raises an alternative hypothesis (adiposity- mediated pleiotropy, or a genuinely non-lung regulatory effect) that the researcher's writeup doesn't engage with. Note: the CS-induced-autophagy mechanism is a smoking- *exposure*-dependent pulmonary injury pathway, not a smoking-*initiation*/nicotine- dependence confound (prior #1) — that distinction is fine and should be preserved — but it does not rescue the missing genetic link. Net: plausible pathway, zero gene-specific genetic evidence, and the one relevant L2G data point argues against a lung-specific mechanism at this exact variant.

GAL3ST2 — WEAKENED

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

The Zhu et al. 2026 PNAS finding (PMID 41774787) is genuinely the most airway-tissue- specific, mechanistically concrete paper at this locus (primary human airway epithelial cells, AAV delivery, antiviral glycan-sulfation effect relevant to virally-triggered exacerbations). But "worth flagging prominently" overstates what's actually known: (1) GAL3ST2 has never been tested in a COPD cohort, COPD tissue, or COPD model; (2) direction of effect is unestablished — it's not shown whether the COPD-associated allele increases or decreases GAL3ST2 function/expression, so even granting the mechanism, whether it would be risk-conferring or protective for COPD/exacerbation is unknown; (3) GAL3ST2's own OT COPD score (0.0257) is lower than D2HGDH's, and it is the *secondary* gene in most of the same allergic/eosinophil credible sets that dominate this locus, again raising the eosinophilic/atopic-pleiotropy alternative rather than a COPD-specific one. This is a good mechanistic hypothesis to retain for follow-up, not a leading candidate to elevate above better-genetically-supported (even if trait-mismatched) genes.
