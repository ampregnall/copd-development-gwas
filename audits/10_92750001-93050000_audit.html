Audit Report - Locus 10:92750001-93050000 (nearest gene EXOC6)

This file records everything excluded from the evidence table (evidence_tables/10_92750001-93050000.md): rejected citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH) and full reasoning for genes the red-team-skeptic critiqued, preserved verbatim. Kept separate from the evidence table so the evidence table can be handed out on its own.

Excluded citations

**Citation: Sec8 modulates TGF-beta induced EMT by controlling N-cadherin via regulation of Smad3/4, Tanaka T, Goto K, Iino M, Cellular Signalling 2016** Identifier: PMID 27769780 / DOI 10.1016/j.cellsig.2016.10.007 Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH Why excluded: The researcher originally flagged this as indirect (studying a different exocyst subunit than EXOC6), and citation-verifier confirmed the caveat was necessary, not optional. The abstract lists the full exocyst complex nomenclature, including "Sec15 (EXOC6)," but the actual experimental work studies only Sec8 (EXOC4) — "Sec8 regulates N-cadherin expression by controlling Smad3 and Smad4 expression at the basal transcriptional level." No EXOC6-specific experiments are reported. Using this paper as EXOC6-specific evidence would overstate what the paper shows, even though it is real, peer-reviewed, and not retracted, and even though it does use a lung-derived cell line (A549) — the lung relevance attaches to EXOC4, not the locus's candidate gene EXOC6.

**Citation: Proteomic analysis of HIV-1 Nef cellular binding partners reveals a role for exocyst complex proteins in mediating enhancement of intercellular nanotube formation, Mukerji J, Olivieri KC, Misra V, Agopian KA, Gabuzda D, Retrovirology 2012** Identifier: PMID 22534017 / DOI 10.1186/1742-4690-9-33 Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH Why excluded: The abstract reports wild-type HIV-1 Nef "was associated with 5 components of the exocyst complex (EXOC1, EXOC2, EXOC3, EXOC4, and EXOC6)" by proteomics, but only EXOC1-EXOC4 were independently validated by co-immunoprecipitation and carried forward into the functional nanotube-formation assays. EXOC6 was detected but not functionally implicated in this paper's own downstream experiments. Using this as EXOC6-specific functional immune evidence would overstate the paper's actual findings for this particular subunit.

No citations were rejected as RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND at this locus. All 16 citations checked (15 from the researcher's original search, 1 surfaced later by red-team-skeptic) resolved to real, non-retracted, correctly-attributed articles; only the two above failed the claim-correspondence step specifically for EXOC6-gene-specificity.

Gene-level critique preserved verbatim (red-team-skeptic)

Neither gene at this locus was marked strictly CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (both were marked WEAKENED, with PCGF5 explicitly flagged as "bordering on CONTESTED"). Given how close that call is, and per the instruction to preserve reasoning transparently, both genes' full critiques are reproduced verbatim below rather than only a strictly-CONTESTED gene.

EXOC6 — WEAKENED

"LD/nearest-gene bias. This is one of the cleaner cases for nearest-gene inference: Ensembl REST confirms EXOC6 is the *only* protein-coding gene in the strict locus window, and 51/54 credible-set variants (including the lead SNP and the #2 PIP variant) fall in EXOC6 introns/ flanking sequence. That materially reduces (though doesn't eliminate) the usual LD-block ambiguity problem -- there's no plausible competing gene physically inside the window. However, PIP=0.183 for the lead variant means the credible set still reflects substantial fine-mapping uncertainty, and the researcher reports no GTEx/lung eQTL or sQTL colocalization analysis tying the credible-set variants to EXOC6 expression specifically. Positional dominance within a gene body is suggestive but is not the same evidentiary class as an eQTL/coloc link -- this is a real gap, not just a nice-to-have.

Smoking-mediation. Open Targets L2G shows EXOC6 = 0 for smoking initiation and 0 for COPD/FEV1FVC/lung function directly, with its only nonzero L2G (0.330) being for 'Chronic Obstructive Asthma,' an ambiguous composite phenotype. My independent search for EXOC6 + smoking/nicotine GWAS returned nothing. So the case for smoking-mediation is not supported, but neither is a direct lung mechanism -- the evidence is simply silent on both, not adjudicating between them.

Contradicting evidence / alternative gene. I did not find any paper arguing directly against EXOC6 at this locus. However, two things temper the case: (1) CYP26A1, ~11-23kb outside the strict 3' boundary and not prioritized by any statistical method, has a *direct, disease-tissue, gene-specific* finding (Ng-Blichfeldt 2017, PMID 28087752) -- CYP26A1 protein elevated in human emphysema lung tissue with a plausible angiogenesis mechanism -- which is mechanistically stronger evidence than anything found for EXOC6 itself, even though it is not statistically implicated at this locus. (2) The broader 10q23.33 cytoband is a well-established pleiotropic region (IDE-KIF11-HHEX T2D/lipid/BMI locus, ~54-95kb away, different LD block per the researcher), which is a general caution about this genomic neighborhood's tendency to harbor multiple independent, phenotype-specific signals rather than direct evidence against EXOC6.

Overclaiming check. The researcher's write-up correctly downgrades the two CLAIM_MISMATCH citations (Tanaka 2016 studies EXOC4 not EXOC6; Mukerji 2012's EXOC6 hit was proteomic-only, not functionally validated) and the overall assessment ('no direct COPD, lung-function, smoking-trait, or COPD-relevant-immune literature was found for EXOC6 itself') is appropriately conservative -- it does not overreach beyond what the verified citations support. No overclaiming detected.

Thoroughness gap -- new finding. My search surfaced a relevant citation the researcher's structural/developmental search missed: Lin, Wu & Tang, 'The Multifaceted Role of EXOC6A in Ciliogenesis,' eLife 2026, PMID 42159049 (also PMC12330657/bioRxiv 2025.07.25.666797) -- this shows EXOC6A/myosin-Va cooperatively drive ciliary vesicle maturation and transition-zone assembly, and EXOC6A knockout arrests ciliogenesis. This directly bears on the researcher's stated negative ('No PubMed hits for EXOC6 in... mucociliary clearance directly') -- ciliogenesis mechanism is upstream of mucociliary clearance, a COPD-relevant pathway per prior #2/#3. The paper is *not lung-tissue-specific* (appears to use generic ciliogenesis cell models, not airway epithelium), so it would strengthen rather than transform the case, but it is a genuine omission. Flagging this citation as UNVERIFIED for the citation-verifier and recommending the researcher/synthesis-lead incorporate it before finalizing -- it's the most mechanistically on-topic (cilia/airway-clearance-relevant) EXOC6 paper found across either search.

Verdict: WEAKENED. The gene-level case rests almost entirely on physical/positional dominance of the credible set (genuinely strong for this locus) plus generic, non-lung- specific exocyst trafficking biology. There is no lung tissue, no COPD, no spirometry, no smoking-trait, and no COPD-relevant immune-cell evidence directly implicating EXOC6 -- the inference that EXOC6 is the causal gene here is plausible but not yet evidenced beyond 'only gene in the window, most SNPs sit in it.' The newly found ciliogenesis paper should be added before this is called settled."

[Note: PMID 42159049 was subsequently verified by citation-verifier — see evidence_tables/_status/10_92750001-93050000/verified_citations.md Citation 16; the PMC ID cited by the skeptic above (PMC12330657) was corrected to the actual PMC13189621 during verification. The PMID/DOI/journal/claim were all confirmed accurate.]

PCGF5 — WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED

"LD/nearest-gene bias / physical-distance problem. This is the dominant issue. The researcher's own critical caveat -- PCGF5 sits ~1.5 Mb centromeric of the credible-set window and is not returned even by a widened Ensembl overlap query, with zero credible-set variants annotated to it -- is a serious, possibly disqualifying problem, not a minor caveat. A PoPS score alone (a similarity-based/polygenic prioritization method, not a fine-mapping or functional-genomics method) is weak evidence for gene assignment when it's the *only* signal and it directly conflicts with the credible-set variant locations. No paper was found reporting a coloc/eQTL or long-range regulatory (e.g., Hi-C/enhancer- looping) link connecting this locus's credible-set variants to PCGF5 expression, and independent searches for 'PCGF5 lung function COPD GWAS' returned nothing supportive.

Smoking-mediation. The Open Targets L2G pattern (0.676 for smoking initiation, 0 for COPD/FEV1FVC/lung function) is internally consistent with a smoking-behavior mechanism rather than direct pulmonary biology -- this is the strongest, most coherent story in the PCGF5 evidence, but it rests on a database score, not a literature-verified GWAS hit (researcher could not confirm PCGF5 is among the GSCAN 406 loci). If PCGF5 is real, prior #1 suggests this signal is more likely neurobehavioral/smoking-initiation-mediated than lung-biology-mediated -- but given the coordinate mismatch, it may not be 'real' for this locus at all.

Contradicting evidence. No paper was found explicitly arguing against PCGF5 at 10q23, but the coordinate/distance mismatch functions as de facto contradicting evidence against assigning this locus's COPD signal to PCGF5 -- the credible set simply isn't near it.

Overclaiming check. The researcher's own language is appropriately hedged ('PCGF5's candidacy rests entirely on a PoPS score with no credible-set variant nearby and a possible ~1.5Mb physical-distance mismatch') -- this is honest, not overclaimed.

Thoroughness. Adequately searched given what's findable; the structural-biology (limb development) and immunology (B-cell lymphoma) citations are correctly characterized as off-target (non-lung, non-COPD-immune).

Verdict: WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED on positional grounds -- not because a paper argues against PCGF5, but because the researcher's own unresolved distance discrepancy (~1.5 Mb, outside even a widened gene-overlap window, zero credible-set variants) undercuts the core premise that PCGF5 is 'at' this locus at all. Recommend synthesis-lead treat PCGF5 as a low-confidence candidate pending independent confirmation of its coordinates via Ensembl REST (the researcher flagged this as UNVERIFIED/WebSearch-only) -- this is a thoroughness gap that should be closed (a single Ensembl REST lookup) before PCGF5 is weighed against EXOC6 in the final synthesis."

[Note: the orchestrator subsequently closed this thoroughness gap with a direct Ensembl REST lookup — see evidence_tables/_status/10_92750001-93050000/verified_citations.md, "Orchestrator follow-up" section. Result: PCGF5 = ENSG00000180628, 10:91,162,339-91,284,337 (GRCh38), confirming the researcher's original WebSearch-derived estimate was accurate and the ~1.47 Mb coordinate discrepancy is real, not a research artifact.]
