Audit Report - Locus 7:32150001-32400000 (nearest gene gene)

This file contains everything excluded from the main evidence table (`evidence_tables/7_32150001-32400000.md`), plus the reasoning verbatim from citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic, for full traceability.

Excluded citations

1. Schuit E, Panagiotou OA, Munafò MR, Bennett DA, Bergen AW, David SP.

**Reason for exclusion: RETRACTED.**

Citation-verifier's verbatim verdict:

Verdict: RETRACTED Evidence: Paper confirmed (PMID 28884473, Cochrane Database Syst Rev 2017, PMC6483659). RETRACTED PUBLICATION. PubMed record shows retraction: "Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2021 Nov 30;11:CD011823. doi: 10.1002/14651858.CD011823.pub3" (Retraction PMID: 34847240). The retraction is listed in CommentsCorrectionsList as "Retraction in" with pubtype "Retracted Publication." Although the paper did mention rs215605 (PDE1C) among nine tested SNPs, it found no statistically significant differential treatment effects for this variant specifically. Regardless of content, the retracted status takes precedence.

The claim this paper was originally going to support (rs215605/PDE1C tested among nine a priori genome-wide-significant smoking-related SNPs for pharmacogenomic modification of smoking-cessation pharmacotherapy efficacy, with no significant differential effect found) is therefore **not usable as evidence** in this review, regardless of whether the underlying finding might be considered independently plausible.

Citations retained with material caveats (not excluded, but flagged)

These were NOT excluded, but are flagged here for transparency since the red-team-skeptic found the researcher's original framing of them overstated their evidentiary weight. See the main evidence table for how each is now labeled.

2. Shrine N, et al. Nature Genetics, 2019. PMID: 30804560.

Citation-verifier verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH** (on the locus-specific sub-claim only; the paper itself is real, non-retracted, and its general scope claim is accurate).

Paper is VERIFIED to exist (Nature Genetics, 2019, PMID 30804560, PMC6397078) and not retracted. General scope claim confirmed: ~400,102 individuals, 279 lung-function signals, multi-ancestry design. However, the specific claim about whether the PDE1C locus (chr7:32.15-32.4 Mb, lead variant rs12701207) is reported among the significant FEV1/FEV1-FVC signals cannot be verified from the accessible abstract, which does not list individual signals by chromosomal location. Full supplementary tables are not accessible. Without access to supplementary variant-level data, the specific locus-presence claim remains unverified.

Retained in the main table for its general, verified scope statement only — not as evidence for or against this locus's presence in that GWAS's significant signal list.

3. Liu M, Jiang Y, Wedow R, et al. (GSCAN). Nature Genetics, 2019. PMID: 30643251.

Citation-verifier: VERIFIED (paper real, general scope accurate; specific locus-table membership not confirmed). Red-team-skeptic critique:

GSCAN's locus membership being unconfirmed is already appropriately flagged by the verifier; the synthesis should say explicitly "inferred from Open Targets credible-set overlap, not confirmed in the GSCAN loci table" rather than "probable source," which reads as more confirmed than it is.

Retained, but re-labeled per the skeptic's language in the main table.

4. Wu W, Liu H, Song F, et al. Oncotarget, 2016. PMID: 27344179.

Citation-verifier: VERIFIED (all claims as literally stated are accurate). Red-team-skeptic critique (this is the key caveat — the paper is real and accurately described, but its use as evidence for THIS locus's smoking-mediation hypothesis is contested):

Wu et al. is being used in the synthesis as if it corroborates "PDE1C variation tracks smoking behavior," but it doesn't show that — it shows a different variant's effect on a different disease (melanoma) is statistically modified by smoking status. That is evidence of a gene-environment interaction somewhere in the PDE1C region, not evidence that PDE1C associates with smoking initiation/heaviness/cessation. It should not be presented in the same evidentiary tier as a smoking-behavior GWAS hit.

Retained in the main table, downweighted/re-labeled per the skeptic's language.

Gene-level CONTESTED assessment

The red-team-skeptic was explicitly asked whether PDE1C should be marked CONTESTED. Its verbatim answer:

Verdict: WEAKENED The underlying facts (retraction correctly excluded, real vascular-remodeling literature, real Open Targets smoking/behavioral L2G dominance, real absence of COPD/FEV1 signal) are not in dispute, and I found nothing that actively contradicts PDE1C or promotes a rival gene at this locus, so this is not CONTESTED. But the synthesis overreaches in exactly the way prior-check #4 (overclaiming) warns against: it presents the smoking-mediation case as resting on "strong, repeated, independent" literature when two of the three supporting citations are, respectively, retracted and mismatched-variant/tangential-phenotype, and it extends the PAH/vascular biology toward COPD relevance in a direction (comorbidity to primary driver) that isn't actually established.

So PDE1C is **not** listed as CONTESTED in the main evidence table, but is flagged WEAKENED — the underlying facts stand, but the strength of the smoking-mediation narrative and the COPD-relevance of the PAH/vascular literature were both downgraded from how the researcher originally framed them.

Full disclosed limitations of this review round

- **Red-team-skeptic's own independent contradiction-search pass could not be executed** (its web-search budget was already exhausted before it could run any query, and its two WebFetch attempts against GWAS Catalog and Open Targets pages returned empty/JS-rendered shells with no extractable data). Verbatim disclosure:

> My web-search budget was already exhausted before I could run any query (both initial > searches returned "budget used" errors), and my two WebFetch attempts (GWAS Catalog > gene page, Open Targets associations page) returned empty/JS-rendered shells with no > extractable data. I was not able to perform any independent contradiction-search pass > for PDE1C — this is the only candidate gene at this locus, so there is no "budget > spread across genes" issue, but synthesis-lead should know this critique is based > entirely on re-analysis of the researcher's own material, not on independent > verification or a fresh adversarial search.

This locus's "no contradicting evidence found" status must therefore be read as **untested by this review round**, not as a clean bill of health.

- **No CNS/neuronal/reward-pathway literature search was performed for PDE1C**, despite the locus's dominant smoking-behavior/cognitive-trait profile making this arguably the most mechanistically relevant tissue context to check (by analogy to PDE1B's established striatal/addiction biology). Flagged by red-team-skeptic as a real gap and a recommended follow-up, not resolved in this review round.

- **Population-stratification/pure-pleiotropy was not tested as a null alternative** explanation for this locus's broad multi-domain trait promiscuity (smoking, cognition, lipids/adiposity, lymphocyte count).

- The researcher's own bioRxiv search tool timed out and its general WebSearch budget was reportedly exhausted by other work in the same session, so its literature search relied entirely on PubMed.
