It preserves excluded citations, contested/weakened gene verdicts, and the full verbatim red-team-skeptic critique, so a reviewer can see what was screened out or downweighted and why, without needing this file to evaluate the evidence table itself.

None. Search-coverage caveat disclosed by red-team-skeptic

The red-team-skeptic's web search budget was exhausted (200/200) before it could run any independent contradiction-search query for this locus. Its critique is therefore a desk review of the researcher's own findings and the orchestrator-supplied Open Targets context, not a literature search in its own right. It explicitly recommends a follow-up search — specifically to identify the original discovery GWAS behind PMID 38886662's "DTWD1" mention, and to check whether DTWD1 and FAM227B are in high LD in prior COPD GWAS — before treating the DTWD1-vs-FAM227B prioritization call as settled. This caveat is carried through to the "Prioritized gene" section of the evidence table, which is marked confidence LOW accordingly.

Additional candidate gene identified outside the input file's prioritization methods

COPS2 (COP9 signalosome subunit 2) lies physically within the locus boundaries (15:49,106,068-49,155,661) per an Ensembl REST region lookup performed by the researcher, but is not flagged by nearest-gene, MAGMA, PoPS, ABC, or any credible-set variant in the locus input file. It was added to the researched candidate set for completeness only. Both of its citations (PMID 30503783, PMID 28423737) are tangential (an unrelated psychiatric GWAS naming it only as one of several positional candidates near a haplotype, and an unrelated hepatocellular-carcinoma pathway study). The researcher's own disposition: "not a plausible candidate based on current evidence; noted for completeness only." No further action was taken on COPS2 in this pipeline run; it was not queried against Open Targets by the orchestrator (that lookup was completed before COPS2 was identified by the researcher).

Variant-level coverage note

A GWAS Catalog lookup confirmed its physical location and intron_variant consequence but did not resolve trait-association specifics with the tools available to the researcher. This is disclosed as an open/inconclusive item rather than treated as a confirmed absence of GWAS-Catalog-level trait association.
