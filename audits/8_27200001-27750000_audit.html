The pass reported "Identifier as provided: PMID 28012752 [INCORRECT]... Correct Identifier: PMID 28028752" and issued a verdict of NOT_FOUND — but PMID 28028752 (the PMID the verifier itself called "correct") was in fact exactly what had been submitted for checking in the original dispatch prompt. This was treated as a transcription slip inside the verifier's own search process, not a real citation problem, and was resolved via a second, narrow, single-citation re-verification pass that independently confirmed PMID 28028752 / DOI 10.1007/s10753-016-0495-z resolves to this exact article (PMCID PMC5357505, not retracted), with the claim fully supported by the abstract. Final verdict: VERIFIED. This citation appears in the main evidence table with its corrected, confirmed verdict.

CONTESTED / WEAKENED gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic reasoning, preserved verbatim)

CCDC25 — WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED

All mechanistic literature is genuine NET-receptor biology (ILK/β-parvin pathway) but entirely in cancer-metastasis and PAH-vascular-remodeling contexts — zero direct CCDC25-COPD studies exist, a fact the researcher states plainly and doesn't obscure. This is exactly the "real biology, wrong disease context" pattern the prompt anticipates, and it's being reported as such rather than overclaimed as COPD evidence — good practice. But the Open Targets context adds an important independent reason for skepticism beyond "just extrapolation": CCDC25 has zero disease-association records for COPD and never appears as an L2G gene in any of ~40 credible sets, despite being the MAGMA-flagged gene. That's a textbook signature of MAGMA (a proximity/gene-based aggregation method) picking up a false-positive attribution driven by regional LD around a genuine regulatory element for a neighboring gene (CLU/EPHX2/CHRNA2), rather than CCDC25 itself being functionally implicated — the LD/nearest-gene-bias concern flagged in the task instructions. **WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED** — the null L2G/disease-association result across every credible set functions as structural counter-evidence to the MAGMA call, even though no single paper explicitly argues against CCDC25.

CLU — WEAKENED

The two locus-relevant papers are both reactive-biomarker/correlation studies (elevated serum/tissue clusterin correlating with COPD severity, FEV1, emphysema on CT) plus one in vitro CSE-induction/partial-knockdown-rescue study. None of this distinguishes cause from consequence — clusterin is a stress-inducible chaperone/complement-regulatory protein, and elevation in more severe disease is equally consistent with reverse causation (tissue damage → clusterin upregulation) as with clusterin driving pathology. The in vitro rescue is explicitly "partial." Critically, the researcher found zero literature placing CLU as the fine-mapped/colocalized causal gene at this specific locus, and no eQTL/colocalization evidence is presented anywhere in the findings. Structurally, CLU's COPD L2G (0.137) and chronic-airway-obstruction L2G (0.182, essentially tied with CHRNA2) don't clearly beat CHRNA2, and CLU's own single highest L2G value across the entire locus is for monocyte count (0.69, dataset-wide max) and atherosclerosis (0.37) — i.e., the strongest statistical signal attached to CLU at this locus is lipid/monocyte-related..., an alternative hypothesis (COPD-cardiovascular comorbidity/shared inflammatory-smoking risk rather than airway-intrinsic mechanism) that the researcher's writeup doesn't engage with at all. Combined with the total absence of any spirometry-trait credible set at this locus, the case for CLU acting through lung-intrinsic airflow-obstruction biology specifically (rather than being a "nearest gene" default, or a monocyte/atherosclerosis-mediated comorbidity gene) is not well established by what's presented. **WEAKENED** — real biomarker/mechanistic association with COPD severity exists, but it is correlational, non-locus-specific, doesn't clearly beat CHRNA2 on L2G, and ignores a plausible non-airway (monocyte/atherosclerosis) alternative explanation for CLU's own strongest statistical signal at this locus. [Follow-up pass, after CHRNA2/eQTL searches:] Nearest-gene/PoPS status and known clusterin lung-injury biology are real, but this pass found no primary-literature eQTL/colocalization evidence tying this locus's credible-set variants to CLU expression, and independently-corroborated CHRNA2 smoking-gene literature plus the background's L2G/disease-association pattern (most credible sets are smoking-behavior traits) give a plausible competing explanation. The inference that CLU is "the" causal gene here overreaches what functional evidence currently supports — it is a nearest/statistically-prioritized candidate, not a functionally anchored one.

CHRNA2 — CONTESTED as a direct-lung-mechanism gene / STRENGTHENED as the smoking-mediation explanation

New, independently verified literature (Wang 2014; Al-Soufi 2021) confirms CHRNA2 (chr8p21) is a genuine, distinct-from-chr15-cluster nicotine-dependence/smoking-behavior gene with its own colocalization evidence (albeit colocalizing with schizophrenia/smoking in a different phenotype pairing, not this exact COPD credible set). Combined with the background pattern that ~40 shared credible sets are overwhelmingly smoking/nicotine/psychiatric traits rather than spirometry traits, this substantially raises confidence that this locus's COPD association is smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence-mediated rather than reflecting direct pulmonary biology — consistent with prior #1's caution. No lung-tissue eQTL evidence was found either for or against a direct pulmonary role. [First pass, prior to the CHRNA2 literature search:] This is the gene most likely under-evaluated relative to its statistical support. Open Targets shows CHRNA2 has the single highest overall COPD disease-association score at the locus (0.376, genetic_association sub-score 0.618, both notably higher than CLU's), and ties CLU for chronic-airway-obstruction L2G (0.182 vs 0.182). Given that essentially all ~40 shared credible sets at this lead variant are smoking-behavior/nicotine-dependence/psychiatric traits, a nicotinic receptor subunit gene sitting under a signal dominated by smoking-related trait labels is mechanistically the most parsimonious explanation — yet the researcher found zero primary literature confirming a CHRNA2-smoking-behavior link despite multiple attempts, and explicitly did not search structural/immune biology for CHRNA2 at all (deemed out of scope for time reasons). This is a genuine thoroughness gap, not a negative finding... Given prior #1 (smoking-mediation must be actively weighed, not assumed away), this gap is exactly the wrong one to leave unresolved before a final gene call is made.

EPHX2 — WEAKENED / UNRESOLVED

This is the strongest mechanistic case at the locus: two independent Ephx2-knockout mouse cigarette-smoke studies (Li 2017, He 2026) plus a pharmacological sEH-inhibitor study (Podolin 2013), all concordant in direction (loss/inhibition of EPHX2 function → less smoke-induced inflammation/emphysema, better preserved lung function), which is qualitatively better causal-inference design (loss-of-function + pharmacology) than CLU's correlational biomarker studies. However, none of these studies test the GWAS locus, the lead variant, or an eQTL/colocalization link between credible-set variants and EPHX2 expression — they establish that EPHX2 protein function matters for smoke-induced emphysema in mice, not that common regulatory variation at 8p21 in humans acts through EPHX2. Its absence from all four internal pipeline methods (nearest/PoPS/MAGMA/credible-set overlap) is a real gap... "chronic bronchitis" is a distinct phenotype from spirometry-confirmed airflow obstruction, and — as with CLU — no FEV1/FEV1-FVC credible set exists at this locus at all, so the single most decisive evidence type for EPHX2's specific predicted mechanism (an airflow-obstruction/spirometry association) is unavailable here, same gap as for CLU. **WEAKENED** — strong, replicated, mechanistically superior evidence that EPHX2 protein is causally involved in smoke-induced emphysema in mice, but no evidence in the verified literature ties GWAS variation at this locus to EPHX2 expression/dosage; using it as a synthesis "override" of the four pipeline methods requires an unstated and untested eQTL/colocalization assumption. [Follow-up pass:] Not flagged by any internal prioritization method, and these two targeted searches added no new supporting evidence... EPHX2 has plausible generic inflammation-resolution biology (soluble epoxide hydrolase) but nothing locus-specific surfaced here. Its candidacy remains speculative and should not be elevated by synthesis-lead absent further evidence.

This is the single biggest missing evidence category and cuts equally against CLU, CCDC25, CHRNA2, and EPHX2 — prioritization currently rests on statistical/database gene-prioritization scores (PoPS, MAGMA, Open Targets L2G) and mechanistic-but-locus- nonspecific functional biology, not on independently-published functional genomics tying variants to expression at this exact locus. - No spirometry/FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS credible set was found sharing this locus's signal among the ~40 examined via Open Targets — a locus-wide gap in the evidence type (concordance with pulmonary-function traits) that the pipeline's biological priors flag as informative when present, and whose absence here should lower confidence in any purely lung-intrinsic story.
