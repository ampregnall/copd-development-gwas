Caveat on an otherwise-VERIFIED citation

- **Meyer-Hoffert U et al., PLoS ONE 2009, PMID 19190773 (SPINK9).** VERIFIED WITH LOCATION DISCREPANCY. The biological claim used (SPINK9/LEKTI-2 is a skin stratum-corneum-localized kallikrein-5-specific protease inhibitor, expressed in human skin/keratinocytes) is accurately represented. However, the source paper itself states the gene is at "chromosome 5p33.1" -- this does not match current genome builds (SPINK9 is at 5q32, and "5p33.1" is not even a valid cytogenetic band, since chromosome 5's short arm does not extend to band 33). This is a discrepancy in the source paper's own stated location, not a citation-accuracy problem for the claim as used here. Retained in the evidence table with this caveat rather than excluded.

Full researcher-flagged evidentiary gaps (not exclusions, but disclosed limitations)

- **Shrine et al. 2023 (PMID 36914875):** Verified to exist at the stated scale (580,869 participants, 1,020 signals, 559 genes), but citation-verifier confirmed its abstract does NOT name HTR4, SH3TC2, or FBXO38 at the 5q33 locus specifically. Used only as general modern-GWAS-scale context in the evidence table, per red-team-skeptic's explicit warning not to let this imply gene-specific reconfirmation. - **Kim et al. 2026 (PMID 41535900):** HTR4 is named as a fine-mapping-resolution example ("Multi-ancestry analysis improved fine-mapping resolution, e.g., HTR4 and RIN3") but is explicitly EXCLUDED from this same paper's own confirmed lung-eQTL-colocalization gene list (ADAM19, THSD4, C4B, PSMA4). Both facts are carried into the evidence table; red-team- skeptic warned against compressing this into "the paper supports HTR4." - **SH3TC2's Open Targets FEV1 L2G-style score (0.834, 0.834, 0.791 across 3 rows):** the single highest such score at this locus, higher than any HTR4 row, but Open Targets could not supply variant/study identifiers for these rows via the API, and no independent literature search (by the researcher, citation-verifier, or red-team-skeptic) could trace this signal to a specific primary-literature claim. Genes marked CONTESTED or otherwise flagged by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

SH3TC2 -- CONTESTED

"No literature connects this gene to lung/COPD/FEV1 at all -- only MAGMA/PoPS prioritization (proximity/expression-based, not tied to any specific credible-set variant), and its only validated biology (CMT4C, Schwann-cell myelination, LOEUF ~0.80 consistent with recessive-disease tolerance) is unrelated to lung. Ordinarily that would justify calling it unsupported. However, Open Targets structured data contradicts a clean dismissal: SH3TC2 carries the single highest FEV1 L2G-style scores at the entire locus (0.79-0.83), despite zero COPD-trait rows. This is a genuine, unresolved discordant data point that argues against fully writing SH3TC2 off. This specific numeric claim should be flagged as UNVERIFIED/needs tracing (variant IDs, study accession) before being used to argue either for or against SH3TC2 in synthesis.

VERDICT: CONTESTED. Not because of dueling literature (there is none), but because there is a real, high-magnitude, unexplained quantitative signal (OT FEV1 L2G ~0.8) in tension with the 'no lung connection' conclusion the literature absence would otherwise support. Should not be resolved by default toward HTR4 without someone tracing the underlying OT rows."

HTR4 -- WEAKENED (not contested, but confidence capped)

"The statistical-genetics case (three independent FEV1/COPD-concordant GWAS) is genuinely strong and satisfies prior #2 well. But the mechanistic case overreaches if presented as 'HTR4 causes COPD-like airflow obstruction' -- the mouse/human tissue data collectively point to a reversible, vagally-mediated AHR phenotype with no structural or inflammatory COPD-model phenotype, which is a materially different (and asthma-adjacent) mechanism than classic COPD pathology. The Shrine 2023 citation is used in a way that implies more contemporary confirmation than it delivers. Smoking-mediation is plausibly (not conclusively) ruled out."

Specific mechanistic-mismatch reasoning (verbatim): "What the mouse data actually show is a reversible, cholinergic/vagally-mediated bronchoconstrictive phenotype with explicitly no structural, inflammatory, or fibrotic phenotype in any COPD-relevant injury model (LPS = neutrophilic/innate, bleomycin = fibrotic/injury, HDM = allergic). That is close to being negative evidence for HTR4 acting through classic COPD mechanisms, and positive evidence for an AHR/neural-reflex mechanism that overlaps more naturally with asthma or asthma-COPD overlap (ACO) than with emphysema-type COPD... the neural/vagal mechanism (House 2017) is a plausible alternative but is a distinct, more speculative causal chain than 'HTR4 acts in the lung.'"

FBXO38 -- HOLDS as not-a-strong-candidate, but PD-1 contradiction flagged as a distraction risk

"Not prioritized by any statistical method, found only via manual proximity scan, zero lung/COPD literature. The Meng 2018 vs. Dibus 2024 PD-1-ubiquitination contradiction is real (both VERIFIED, genuinely head-to-head) and the cGAS-STING/CD8+ T-cell finding (Wu 2024) is a distinct, more recent immune mechanism -- but neither touches lung, airway, or COPD in any way, and FBXO38's only validated human disease role (dHMN type IID, motor-neuron axon biology) is also lung-irrelevant. Including a paragraph on an unrelated immune contradiction for a gene with no connection to this locus's phenotype is not wrong to document (it shows due diligence that the neighbor gene was checked and its own literature is internally contested on an unrelated topic), but it risks reading as locus-relevant immune biology if synthesis isn't careful -- it is not. Recommend this be compressed to one sentence in synthesis."

SPINK7 / SPINK9 / SPINK13 -- HOLDS as not credible candidates

"All three are real genes with real (verified) biology, but SPINK7's epithelial-barrier evidence is entirely esophageal (eosinophilic esophagitis, esophageal cancer), SPINK9 is skin-restricted (also note: the cited source paper itself mislabels the cytogenetic location as '5p33.1,' a red flag about that paper's own care with genomic coordinates, though not about the biological claim), and SPINK13 has no literature at all. All three sit ~150+ kb from the nearest credible-set variant, well outside anything a PIP-weighted credible set implicates, and none is flagged by any prioritization method. This is appropriately treated as very-low-priority background, not a real competing hypothesis."
