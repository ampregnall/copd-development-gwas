CLAIM_MISMATCH — excluded as direct evidence for the gene claimed

**PMID 34230638** (Morrison J et al., Genet Med 2021;23(11):2213-2218 — cited under NAA25). Citation-verifier's verbatim verdict: "Article identifies pathogenic missense variants in **NAA20**, not NAA25. NAA20 is the catalytic subunit and NAA25 the auxiliary subunit of the same NatB complex, so the researcher's framing... is directionally accurate about the complex biology, but the paper's disease-causing variants and patient phenotypes are in NAA20, NOT NAA25 itself. Flag as CLAIM_MISMATCH: do not cite this paper as direct evidence of NAA25 pathogenicity/phenotype; it is only indirect (complex-partner) evidence." Retained in the main evidence table ONLY as indirect complex-biology context, explicitly labeled as not direct NAA25-specific evidence, per citation-verifier's guidance.

Not peer-reviewed — preprint only, excluded from main evidence table

**PMID 37662190** (Berry SB et al., "Disruption of Aldehyde Dehydrogenase 2 protects against bacterial infection," bioRxiv preprint, cited under ALDH2 immunology). Citation-verifier's verbatim verdict: "bioRxiv metadata shows published_doi: 'NA' (not formally published in a peer-reviewed journal as of verification). Posted 2023-08-26, still preprint status. Preprint abstract does support the claim about Aldh2-deficient macrophages/enhanced TB control, but this is unvetted (non-peer-reviewed) research and MUST be labeled as a preprint, not treated as equivalent to a peer-reviewed finding." The paper exists, is not retracted, and its claim (Aldh2-deficient macrophages/mice show enhanced control of intracellular bacterial infection, including increased resistance to pulmonary M. tuberculosis in a hypersusceptible model) is accurately represented by the researcher — but because it has not cleared peer review and the pipeline's evidence table is restricted to VERIFIED (peer-reviewed-equivalent) citations, it is excluded from the main table pending formal publication. Synthesis-lead/study team may wish to track this preprint for future publication, as it is the only immune/host-defense-specific ALDH2-lung mechanistic finding identified in this search.

- **ALDH2 (WEAKENED, not simply "top gene")**: "ALDH2 has the strongest statistical... and the most abundant literature at the locus, but the literature itself is mixed-to-contradictory on mechanism and repeatedly undercuts a clean COPD-diagnosis story — it supports a spirometric/smoking-gene-environment-interaction case more than a direct-disease-causation case." - **SH2B3 (WEAKENED)**: "biologically plausible immune candidate but no lung/COPD evidence exists; ranking it at the same tier as PTPN11... implies a level of confidence the underlying data don't support." - **TRAFD1 (WEAKENED — overclaim identified)**: "Ranking TRAFD1 #2 overall, above SH2B3/PTPN11/HECTD4 which all have higher L2G scores, is an overreach: the synthesis is privileging mechanistic specificity/method rigor from an unrelated disease [celiac disease] over the actual statistical genetic evidence for COPD at this locus... the immune mechanism is real and rigorously established, but 'directly relevant' to COPD is not demonstrated." - **CUX2 (HOLDS, but flagged as an important unresolved divergence)**: "This is a case where strong, repeated statistical fine-mapping/colocalization signal is not matched by any biological story, which should raise — not lower — suspicion that CUX2's apparent primacy is a fine-mapping/LD artifact... or alternatively that CUX2 has an as-yet-uncharacterized lung role... Constraint asymmetry note: CUX2 LOEUF 0.383 [constrained] vs. SH2B3 LOEUF 1.35 [tolerant] — this constraint asymmetry is actually a point in CUX2's favor as the more plausible dosage-sensitive causal gene, which the researcher did not use as an argument."
