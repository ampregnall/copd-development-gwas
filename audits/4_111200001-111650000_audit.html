Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (full reasoning, verbatim)

PITX2 — CONTESTED

The evidence assembled is honestly labeled throughout — the researcher does not overclaim (Acquaah-Mensah correctly flagged as bioinformatic/hypothesis-generating, Banerjee correctly flagged as developmental not adult-disease, cancer papers correctly kept separate). No translation-creep from "associated" to "causal" is evident in the summarized claims.

But the cumulative case is weaker than the citation count suggests, and there's a live competing explanation the evidence itself supports:

- **Genetics-heavy, biology-empty.** 5 Open Targets COPD credible-set rows (0.58-0.78) but PITX2 fails to crack the top 50 of 6,323 all-datasource COPD targets — meaning almost no non-genetic corroboration exists for PITX2-COPD specifically, despite PITX2 being a well-studied, well-phenotyped gene (unlike FAM241A, this isn't "understudied," it's "extensively studied and still nothing surfaces for COPD"). Every mechanistic lung finding in hand is either embryonic/developmental patterning (Banerjee) or lung-cancer-specific (Zhang, Wang, Soukup) — none touch adult COPD-relevant biology (protease-antiprotease balance, emphysema, airway remodeling, neutrophilic/CD8+/Th17 inflammation), and the researcher confirms this gap persisted across multiple search phrasings. - **No spirometry concordance.** Per the pulmonary-function prior, this is exactly the situation that should trigger scrutiny: a COPD case-control genetic signal with no corroborating FEV1/FEV1-FVC hit. An independent PubMed check by the skeptic (see below) corroborates zero literature at the intersection of 4q25/PITX2 and lung function. - **Smoking-mediation is directly supported, not just theoretical.** All three credible-set variants (including the PIP=0.556 lead SNP) hit GWAS Catalog for smoking initiation/cessation/status and correlated behavioral traits, with none hitting FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD directly. Per the smoking-mediation prior, this is close to the textbook signature of a smoking-behavior locus riding into a COPD GWAS via unadjusted smoking confounding. - **A specific, already-verified paper argues against the alternative "shared cardiopulmonary mechanism" framing.** Naesilla/Quint/Zuber 2026 (PMID 41241414) directly examined COPD-AF comorbidity with bidirectional MR and found limited direct genetic causal evidence between COPD/CRD and AF/CVD, with the comorbidity better explained by shared upstream smoking and IL6R/IL1RN inflammatory pathways. This is meaningful contradicting evidence against treating "PITX2 is the AF gene at chr4q25, therefore it plausibly also drives COPD via a shared cardiopulmonary axis" as a strong inference — the paper cited for exactly that purpose concludes the opposite of what a naive pleiotropy story would predict. - **PITX2 is the textbook example of nearest/regulatory-distance bias done right for AF, not necessarily for COPD.** This exact gene desert is the well-established long-range PITX2 cardiac enhancer architecture. That strengthens the case that if something at this desert acts on disease, PITX2 is the mechanistically privileged candidate over ENPEP/FAM241A — but the enhancers characterized in that AF literature are cardiac-tissue enhancers; tissue-specific enhancer activity in lung is not established here, so "PITX2 is the right gene for AF" does not transitively establish "PITX2 is the right gene for COPD" even setting the LD question aside.

Taken together, the more parsimonious explanation is that this signal reflects smoking-behavior pleiotropy and/or AF-COPD comorbidity/ascertainment overlap in an older, heavily-smoking population, rather than a direct PITX2 lung mechanism. PITX2 should be presented as the strongest of the three by genetic score but with the smoking/AF-confound explicitly flagged rather than treated as a clean lung-biology hit.

ENPEP — WEAKENED

- Genetic support is markedly weaker than PITX2 (OT score 0.093, doesn't rank top 50) and the gene is essentially LoF-tolerant (obs/exp 0.857), which argues against a dosage-sensitive causal mechanism (in contrast to PITX2's constraint). - The literature offered is honestly framed as non-COPD: the 1993 paper's immune angle (BP-1/6C3 B-lymphocyte differentiation marker) is about B-lymphopoiesis, not the neutrophilic/CD8+/Th17/macrophage programs the immune-complexity prior flags as COPD-relevant — a mismatch the researcher doesn't paper over. The 2024 Arppo atlas paper is COVID/ACE2-cofactor focused, noting para-alveolar fibroblast expression as an incidental finding, not a COPD result. - No overclaiming detected — both citations are used only for baseline expression/discovery facts, not COPD claims. - An alternative-pleiotropy pattern parallel to PITX2 was flagged but not pursued: 9 additional PMIDs link ENPEP to hypertension/blood-pressure genetics, unexamined by the researcher (listed above). ENPEP (aminopeptidase A) has a known mechanistic role in the renin-angiotensin system (Ang II -> Ang III). Given COPD and hypertension share risk factors (age, smoking, systemic inflammation) and cardiometabolic comorbidity is common in COPD cohorts, this is a live alternative explanation — ENPEP's genetically best-supported disease association elsewhere may be cardiovascular/BP-related, echoing the PITX2/AF situation — that the researcher's own findings surface but leave unresolved.

The evidence assembled is real but thin (weak score, broad/nonspecific tissue expression, an off-topic immune marker discovery, a COVID-focused atlas paper) and does not support treating ENPEP as a COPD-relevant lung gene; the inference that ENPEP belongs in this candidate set rests almost entirely on physical proximity in a gene desert rather than on any COPD-relevant biology, and an unresolved hypertension-pleiotropy alternative should be checked before this gene is weighted at all in synthesis.

FAM241A / C4orf32 — CONTESTED

- Zero COPD genetic evidence of any kind (0 Open Targets credible-set rows for COPD, versus PITX2's 5 and ENPEP's 1) — the weakest genetic case of the three despite being labeled "nearest gene." - All literature is lung-cancer bioinformatic/correlational (MR+coloc gene-prioritization screen for lung cancer risk; a computationally-predicted ceRNA "tumor suppressive axis" in Cr(VI)-transformed bronchial cells) — neither paper makes any COPD claim, and both are appropriately labeled hypothesis-generating rather than mechanistic by the researcher. No overclaiming detected. - The gene is functionally uncharacterized beyond these two cancer-correlation papers — no domain, no pathway, no developmental role, no COPD-relevant immune or structural biology of any kind. - The "nearest gene" framing is itself questionable in this instance: the locus is a documented gene desert with the well-characterized chr4q25/PITX2 long-range regulatory architecture as backdrop. Given this is the classic distal-PITX2-enhancer desert, the linear-distance argument for FAM241A being the functional target is undercut by a well-known competing mechanistic story (PITX2 regulation) at the same desert — this is exactly the nearest-gene-bias scenario the pipeline's priors warn about, and here it isn't hypothetical: there's a specific, well-established alternative target gene for this exact desert architecture.

Not on the strength of a paper specifically refuting FAM241A, but because (a) it has zero COPD genetic support of any kind — the weakest of the three by a wide margin, (b) all its literature is cancer-specific and correlational, and (c) the desert's well-established regulatory relationship is with PITX2, a different gene, which the pipeline's own structural context supplies as a directly competing explanation for this same locus. This satisfies the "implicates a different gene at the same locus" contradiction criterion using material already in the record (no new citation needed).

Locus-level synthesis note (from red-team-skeptic, preserved verbatim)

Across all three genes, the single most load-bearing fact is the GWAS Catalog smoking-behavior check: all three credible-set variants hit smoking-initiation/ cessation/behavioral-trait GWAS records and none hit FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD directly. Per the smoking-mediation and pulmonary-function priors, this is close to the canonical signature of a smoking-mediated signal rather than a direct-lung-biology signal, and it should anchor overall confidence at this locus regardless of which of the three genes is favored. One important caveat (skeptic's own reasoning, not a new citation): GSCAN/Saunders-type multi-trait smoking-behavior GWAS are enormous (hundreds of thousands to millions of participants) and report genome-wide-significant hits at thousands of loci genome-wide, some with very small effect sizes — a GWAS Catalog "hit" alone doesn't establish that smoking behavior is a large or dominant contributor at this specific locus, only that it's plausible and unrefuted. The researcher's own caveat that PMID-to-SNP-row attribution wasn't literally confirmed (inferred from phenotype/method-signature matching) compounds this — treat the smoking-mediation case as directionally strong but not fully nailed down, not as fully closed.
