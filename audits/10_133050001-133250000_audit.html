Cross-cutting methodological note (applies to all genes)

GENE: ADGRA1

  1. **Smoking-mediation claim overreaches on the current evidence.** The researcher (correctly) flags that no primary GSCAN/smoking-GWAS paper was actually confirmed to report this locus, and the one smoking-GWAS citation submitted resolves to the wrong paper (Eucalyptus terpene-oil GWAS, NOT_FOUND) while the correct GSCAN PMID (30643251) was never cited or checked against. My own independent search likewise found zero literature placing ADGRA1/GPR123/this locus in a smoking-behavior GWAS. So "consistent with, though not proof of, smoking-behavior mediation" is appropriately hedged language — but the underlying evidentiary basis for even raising it is Open-Targets-coloc-only, unconfirmed against primary summary statistics. This should be stated even more plainly to synthesis-lead: the smoking-mediation hypothesis here rests entirely on an unverified database coloc field, not on any literature, verified or otherwise.
  1. **"Unresolved mechanism" vs. "evidence against lung-intrinsic mechanism."** The researcher frames the CNS/neuroendocrine-only literature plus absent pulmonary/immune literature as leaving the mechanism "unresolved." I'd push further: given (a) dominant fine-mapping mass, (b) exhaustive functional literature in only two organ systems (hypothalamus, hippocampus) with a clear, mechanistically dense, unrelated biology (thermogenesis, inhibitory synaptic tone), (c) zero COPD/lung-function GWAS or coloc signal indexed anywhere in Open Targets for this locus, and (d) OT's own top indexed traits for these credible-set variants being systolic BP, insomnia, GERD, and back pain — this pattern is more consistent with a locus that is pleiotropic for neurobehavioral/autonomic traits and reaches COPD via smoking-behavior or another indirect route than with an unresolved-but-plausible lung mechanism. This is not proof, but the researcher's "unresolved" framing understates how thin the positive case for lung-intrinsic biology actually is.
  1. Overclaiming check: the one-sentence synthesis matches the underlying VERIFIED abstracts reasonably well (Zhang 2021, Tosun 2026/bioRxiv). No claim inflation detected for ADGRA1 specifically. The Hamann/misattribution CLAIM_MISMATCH is trivial (authorship error, not a content error) and doesn't affect the gene-level inference.
  1. No contradicting-gene literature found naming a different gene as causal at this exact locus (also consistent with the general absence of any locus-specific COPD/lung literature).

**WEAKENED** — fine-mapping support is real and gene assignment is reasonably specific (not an LD/nearest-gene artifact), but the researcher's "unresolved mechanism, possible smoking-mediation" framing is too neutral. The evidence pattern (dominant CNS-only biology, zero lung/immune literature, zero COPD/lung-function GWAS signal, coloc-only with smoking/behavioral/autonomic traits) should be read as active evidence against a lung-intrinsic mechanism and in favor of an indirect (behavioral/autonomic-pleiotropy) explanation, even though that explanation itself lacks primary-literature confirmation. Synthesis-lead should treat ADGRA1 as low-confidence for direct pulmonary biology despite its statistical dominance.

GENE: KNDC1

**WEAKENED** — modest statistical support, no pulmonary/immune biology, brain-restricted expression, and the Alzheimer's-linkage citation's key quantitative claim is unverified. The gene is appropriately deprioritized by the researcher already, but the parallel "smoking-mediation" hedge for KNDC1 should be flagged even more explicitly as speculative-on-speculative (unconfirmed coloc + unconfirmed neurobiology-implies-smoking logic) rather than presented as a real alternative hypothesis on par with ADGRA1's.

GENE: VENTX

**WEAKENED** — the biology is real and on-prior, but the inference that this is a "biologically interesting alternative" for this locus overreaches given zero fine-mapping support at any credible-set variant. It should be reported to synthesis-lead as a flagged non-candidate (interesting gene, wrong locus-evidence), not as a live alternative to ADGRA1/KNDC1.

**WEAKENED/effectively HOLDS-as-weak** — researcher's own characterization ("weak candidate, included for completeness") is accurate and not overreached. No further escalation warranted; flagging to synthesis-lead only as a low-priority background note.
