Genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic (excluded from the evidence table)

PACSIN1

Skeptic verdict verbatim: "**CONTESTED** — the gene's own defining characterization argues against lung relevance, and the sole 'immune' citation is not actually about PACSIN1 biology. Recommend exclusion from the candidate list rather than carrying it forward as a weak positive."

Full reasoning: "The foundational citation (Wasiak 2001) itself states PACSIN1 is **neuron-specific**, explicitly contrasting it with the broadly-expressed paralog PACSIN2 (which is the one showing brain-vs-lung differential expression) — this is intrinsic evidence *against* lung/COPD relevance, not merely absence of support. The second citation (Ngo 2025) uses the Pacsin1 promoter purely as a genetic driver/marker to target pDCs; it demonstrates nothing about PACSIN1 function and is not evidence for this gene at all — it is citation padding that happens to contain the gene's name."

Citations discussed (both individually VERIFIED by citation-verifier, but the skeptic judges the gene-level case CONTESTED because the citations argue against, or are irrelevant to, PACSIN1's candidacy): - Wasiak S et al. (2001), J Biol Chem, PMID 11352907, DOI 10.1074/jbc.M100591200 — states PACSIN1 is neuron-specific, unlike its paralog PACSIN2 which shows brain-vs-lung tissue-specific binding differences. - Ngo C et al. (2025), Nature Immunology, PMID 41023479, DOI 10.1038/s41590-025-02288-3 — uses the Pacsin1 promoter only as a plasmacytoid-dendritic-cell genetic marker/driver in a mouse pDC-depletion study; the paper's finding concerns pDC biology, not PACSIN1 function.

PACSIN1 has no COPD/lung-function genetic evidence (researcher found none) and no prioritization-method support (nearest/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC all silent, no credible-set variant).

TAF11

Skeptic verdict verbatim: "**CONTESTED** — the one available citation, read accurately, is evidence *against* TAF11 being the mediator gene in its own source study, and the disease context (RA-ILD) doesn't transfer to COPD. Should not be carried forward as supporting evidence; if kept, must be reframed as a null/negative result."

Full reasoning: "General TFIID subunit; the single citation (Liu 2024) is a Mendelian-randomization screen in **rheumatoid arthritis → interstitial lung disease** (not COPD) in which TAF11 was one of nine *unvalidated* candidate mediator genes, and the study's own validated/prioritized gene was **SLAMF1**, not TAF11. Citing this as support for TAF11 misrepresents what the source paper concluded — the paper itself deprioritized TAF11 relative to another gene, in a different disease."

Citation discussed (VERIFIED by citation-verifier as an accurate representation of the paper's content, but the skeptic judges the gene-level inferential use of it CONTESTED): - Liu M et al. (2024), International Immunopharmacology, PMID 39260308, DOI 10.1016/j.intimp.2024.113082 — identifies TAF11 as one of nine candidate blood-eQTL mediator genes in an RA→interstitial-lung-disease MR screen; the paper's own validated mediator was SLAMF1, not TAF11.

TAF11 has no COPD/lung-function genetic evidence, no prioritization-method support, and no credible-set variant annotated to it.

Process caveats affecting this locus's evidence base

  1. **Red-team-skeptic's WebSearch budget was exhausted before the critique task began** (reported by the agent as "200 of 200" already used). It substituted two targeted WebFetch/PubMed queries (for ILRUN and SPDEF only, both returning zero contradicting or additional hits) and performed **no independent search pass at all** for SNRPC, BLTP3A, PACSIN1, ANKS1A, or TAF11 — its critique of those five genes rests entirely on re-scrutinizing the researcher's own citations and the Open Targets structured-data context, not on independent verification of completeness. Any literature the researcher might have missed for those five genes would not have been caught by this critique pass.
  2. **PoPS-prioritized "C6orf106" is not a fourth candidate gene** — it resolves to the same Ensembl gene ID (ENSG00000196821) as ILRUN and is treated throughout as PoPS support for ILRUN, not a separate gene.
  3. **Four candidate genes (SPDEF, PACSIN1, ANKS1A, TAF11) were identified by the researcher independently of the input pipeline's four prioritization methods**, by enumerating all protein-coding genes physically overlapping the 6:34,450,001-34,950,000 region via Ensembl REST API. None of them has a credible-set variant annotated to it in the locus's own fine-mapping output, and none was queried against Open Targets (which was run only against the three pipeline-flagged genes before the researcher dispatch). Their absence from Open Targets querying reflects a scoping decision made before the researcher's region-enumeration finding was known, not a null database result.
  4. **Colocalization detail** (per-QTL-study H4/PP4 values) beyond the aggregate Open-Targets L2G-style credible-set score was not resolvable through the GraphQL schema fields explored for this locus — noted as a data-completeness gap in the Open Targets summary, not a negative finding.
