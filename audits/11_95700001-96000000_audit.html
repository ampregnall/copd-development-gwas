Excluded citations

RETRACTED

**Lee JJ et al., "Gene discovery and polygenic prediction from a genome-wide association study of educational attainment in 1.1 million individuals," Nat Genet, 2018.** PMID 30038396, PMC6393768, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0147-3.

- Researcher's claim: cited as a plausible (not gene-confirmed) source of the educational-attainment locus-to-gene (L2G) signal reported by Open Targets for CEP57 at this locus. - **Citation-verifier verdict: RETRACTED**, with justification: "PubMed metadata confirms retraction on August 1, 2018. Paper exists but is withdrawn from literature." - **Orchestrator flag (important):** This verdict is surprising and should be treated with suspicion pending independent human re-verification. This is a widely known, extremely heavily cited (>3,000 citations) social-science-genomics GWAS ("EA3") that, to the orchestrator's and red-team-skeptic's knowledge, remains an active, non-retracted paper in the literature as of each agent's respective knowledge. The verifier's own stated retraction date (August 1, 2018) is suspiciously identical to the paper's original publication date — a pattern consistent with the verifier having misread or misattributed a PubMed metadata field (e.g. confusing a "received"/"published" date field with an actual retraction notice) rather than having found a genuine retraction notice. - **Red-team-skeptic's independent comment (verbatim):** "I'm confident, from general knowledge, that Lee JJ et al. 2018 Nat Genet ... is a landmark, extremely heavily-cited (>3,000 citations), **non-retracted** paper — it remains foundational in the EA/cognition GWAS literature as of my knowledge cutoff. The orchestrator's suspicion is well-founded: a 'retraction date identical to publication date' is a classic signature of a metadata-parsing error ... not a real retraction. I'd recommend this specific verifier call be sent back for manual re-check rather than trusted." - **Disposition:** Per pipeline rules, the orchestrator does not overrule the citation-verifier's verdict, so this citation is excluded from the verified evidence table (`evidence_tables/11_95700001-96000000.md`) regardless of this suspicion. This exclusion is not load-bearing for any conclusion in the evidence table: the citation was only ever used as an unconfirmed, gene-unspecific "plausible source" of a locus-level educational-attainment signal already independently corroborated (for the whole FAM76B/MTMR2/CEP57 block, not CEP57 specifically) by the VERIFIED Sanna et al. 2020 citation. **A human reviewer should independently check PubMed/Retraction Watch for PMID 30038396 before treating this exclusion as settled**, and consider re-running citation verification on this specific PMID if a manuscript depends on it.
