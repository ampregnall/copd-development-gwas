2026-09-11 ADDENDUM: THRA added after genome-build-error correction

**Background:** The study team discovered a genome-build error in the internal MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file (`loci/copd-all-gene-prioritization.txt`) used to generate candidate-gene lists. A corrected re-merge surfaced THRA (thyroid hormone receptor alpha) as a candidate gene at this locus that was never evaluated in the original 13-gene review.

**Step 1 — coordinate check (performed by orchestrator, not a subagent):** THRA (ENSG00000126351) was independently confirmed via Ensembl coordinates (through the Open Targets GraphQL API) to span chr17:40,058,290-40,093,867 (GRCh38). This lies entirely inside the locus's defined 1 Mb window (17:39,150,001-40,150,000), confirming THRA is a genuine physical resident of the locus and that the build-error correction is legitimate, not spurious. However, THRA does NOT overlap the locus's actual fine-mapped credible-set span: the nearest credible-set variant is rs7224129 (17:39,919,173, PIP=0.00062, annotated to ORMDL3 as downstream_gene_variant), approximately 139 kb from THRA's start coordinate. No credible-set variant is annotated to THRA. This places THRA in an evidentiary category similar to CSF3/PSMD3 from the original review (window-resident, no credible-set variant), though physically closer to the credible-set cluster (~139 kb vs. CSF3/PSMD3's ~300-450 kb). Because THRA does plausibly belong at this locus (window overlap confirmed), the pipeline proceeded to full researcher/citation-verifier/red-team-skeptic evaluation rather than stopping at the coordinate check.

**Step 1b — Open Targets structured lookup (performed by orchestrator):** THRA has the single highest COPD-association score of any gene queried at this locus (0.252, vs. GSDMB 0.192 and ORMDL3 0.131), essentially no asthma-association score (unlike every other high-COPD-scoring gene here), a notable "bronchopulmonary dysplasia" top-hit (0.259, its single highest score across 1836 diseases), and strong LOF constraint (gnomAD lof oe=0.276, 90% CI 0.179-0.439). This structured database context (NOT a verified literature claim) motivated the full researcher dispatch — see full detail in `evidence_tables/_status/17_39150001-40150000/opentargets.md`.

**Steps 2-4 — researcher, citation-verifier, red-team-skeptic:** All 13 citations returned by the THRA-focused researcher dispatch were VERIFIED by citation-verifier (no retractions, no NOT_FOUND, no CLAIM_MISMATCH — see `verified_citations_thra_addendum.md`). The red-team-skeptic then critiqued THRA and explicitly assessed whether it changes the original ORMDL3-vs-GSDMB tension (see full verbatim critique below and in `critique_thra_addendum.md`).

**Outcome:** THRA is marked CONTESTED (see below) and does NOT change the original prioritized-gene call. ORMDL3 remains the prioritized gene, confidence LOW, unchanged from the original review. See `evidence_tables/17_39150001-40150000.md` Prioritized gene section for full reasoning.

Cross-cutting points from the red-team-skeptic (verbatim, original critique)

(a) GSDMB/ORMDL3 vs. asthma: Not adequately distinguished in the current write-up for GSDMB (see WEAKENED critique above) — the "deepest mechanistic literature" framing overstates COPD-specificity when the underlying functional characterization is asthma-outcome literature. ORMDL3 has a better (but still incomplete) case for genuine smoke-specific pulmonary biology independent of the asthma story, though causal-gene assignment within the shared regulatory block remains unresolved by the credible set as presented. (b) CSF3 inclusion: Should be flagged to synthesis-lead as unverified-to-locus (see WEAKENED critique) — compelling gene-level biology, unconfirmed LD/positional relevance to this specific credible set. (c) Balantic 2013: Currently used as supporting context rather than a headline claim in the researcher's ORMDL3 write-up, which is appropriate, but I'd flag explicitly that it should not be elevated to "human COPD genetic validation" in synthesis — it's underpowered, marginal, unadjusted for smoking, and conflates two phenotype comparisons. (d) PGAP3 fair treatment: Yes — researcher and I agree PGAP3 is fairly treated as likely non-causal (CONTESTED) despite topping the positional/ABC ranking; this is a well-reasoned exception to "nearest/top-ranked gene wins." Thoroughness flag for synthesis-lead: No independent contradiction search was completed by me for any gene this session (search budget exhausted before either of my two attempts returned results). The LD relationship between CSF3/PSMD3 signals and the rs1495100 credible set, and a dedicated look for an asthma-vs-COPD Mendelian randomization / genetic-correlation study at 17q21 (which would directly test whether the COPD signal is fully explained by asthma liability), are the two most valuable follow-up searches before finalizing this locus — I recommend the researcher or a fresh search pass address these specifically rather than treating the current write-up as final on those two questions.
